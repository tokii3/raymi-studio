# Idea and story - think the video in detail before a pixel

toki 2026-10-25: "du sollst NACHDENKEN wie das Video und das Skript aussehen soll, im Detail, mit Liebe und Kreativität,
und es dann umsetzen." The best videos (Renai, Let's Get Weird, POP IN 2) came from ONE strong idea and a deep
hand-written treatment (CASEBOOK). Process weight never replaces the idea.

## 1. Order (writers_room/README.md has the files)
0. `0_BRIEF.md` (skill raymi-reading-toki) - her words, core, classes, visible proof, feasibility.
1. Dossier - the song's meaning line by line, its existing videos (the gap), the measured song map.
2. Ideas - 3 really different worlds as IMAGE boards (references paired), toki picks.
3. Treatment - script-critic PASS **and** brief-keeper PASS (her contract lines all present).
4. Script + shots.json - every moment: music, her action, camera, light, transition, reference segment, WHY.
   The maker agents (camera-director, choreographer, editor, look-designer) draft their layer from the contract +
   references; I merge and keep the whole.

## 2. Craft (principles in knowledge/PRINCIPLES.md A/B)
- One world from the song + Raymi (her name 灯闇, body, lore), with a change and a payoff; another VTuber could not
  do it.
- A story of actions, understood muted; 5 planned wows, the strongest at the end; a cold open with the best picture.
- Every element: meaning -> idea -> perfect version -> what exists -> reinvented in her ocean.
- Love = specific details with reasons (a sticker that says something, a prop she uses, a light that means something).
- Write the PERFECT version first (wishlist), then find how to build it - never design from what is easy in code.
- Watch at least 3 references of the song's genre for each section type before writing it (`refs find`).

## 3. Self-check before review
- Read the treatment next to her words: is every core phrase there, in its full class, visibly?
- Could the treatment be another VTuber's? Is any section "she dances in a room"?
- Is there one moment you would show a friend? Five?
