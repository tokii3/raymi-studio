# Slop tells - the AI default next to what a professional does (2026-10-27)

From the free motion-design packs (`knowledge/skillpacks/README.md`), translated to a Raymi MV. A frame or a moment
that shows a left-column item is a fault class: find every instance over the film (scene probe / contact sheet) and
fix the shared part, not shot by shot.

| the AI default | a professional does |
|---|---|
| crossfade between scenes | hard cut on motion, a match cut, or the effect becomes the next scene (her ink, her glow, water) |
| cut when the move has settled | cut at peak speed. The next shot opens mid-move, same direction (`raymi seams`) |
| neighbouring cuts in opposite directions (ping-pong) | one current per film. A flip only on a hit / a force |
| everything fades in | things arrive by moving (offset, scale, blur decaying), opacity only as a short helper |
| grow from zero | arrive from oversize and come down (~3.5x) |
| one long ease-out zoom | a one-frame snap + a 0.3-0.6 s settling tail |
| one ease for a whole pan | two pushes with different accelerations |
| idle wobble to fill time (float, breathe, glow pulse) | a story beat: a staged reveal, the camera travelling with intent, her acting |
| a dead stretch > 2-4 s | something new every 2-4 s, or a designed breath (0.3-1.5 s) before a payoff |
| a glitch / flash over the whole frame | a local, short (<= 6 f) effect at the one moment it means something |
| bounce / elastic overshoot everywhere | one visible spring per film at most |
| a generic wipe / circle wipe | a transition built from a shape that lives in the film |
| uniform pacing | an arc: holds -> shortening -> densest just BEFORE the breath -> the hit -> resolve |
| a uniform stagger (timer delay) | uneven 1-3 f gaps, tails overlapping |
| two heroes of equal weight in a frame | one focal point (her, or the one object), everything else supports |
| a pinned object at the same spot through 3+ shots | carriers travel. Nothing parks in one screen position |
| ending on a fade to black by default | end in motion or on a designed stillness; the end is the start, changed |

## How to look (the "describe first" rule)
Before judging a contact sheet, describe EVERY frame in one sentence (what is on screen, what is the hero, would it
pass as a still from the reference video?). Then judge. Then name the weakest shot and what would fix it. A gate
claimed without the numbers printed by the tools (`reality`, `cuts`, `seams`, `probe`) is not a gate.
