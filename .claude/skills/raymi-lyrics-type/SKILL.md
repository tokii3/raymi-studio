# Lyrics and type - part of the picture (her most repeated complaint, x10+)

toki: "zu klein, zu dünn, wirken aufgesetzt / nicht stimmig", "abgeschnitten, hinter Raymi, clippen in Assets",
Japanese lines need romaji + English. Watch Me: lyrics OFF on her request - the brief decides whether there are any.
Sources: knowledge/craft/06, studies/typography.md, PRINCIPLES 11; references `yoasobi-idol`, `ado-usseewa`,
`td-giants`, `kda-more` (type as architecture), This Is War 5/6 (local, old engine).

## Craft
- Her font only (Raymi-Regular / Raymi-Heavy, missing glyphs built in her style). One family; variety from size
  (key word huge, fillers small), one material / accent per key word, angle following the pose / the space.
- Timed from the vocal stem word by word, one frame before the syllable.
- Lives IN the world: lit by the scene, a shadow, on a surface in perspective, colour from the light; or the medium's
  own type (UI, neon, chalk, tape).
- Readable at phone size: key words >= 10-12 % of frame height (cap height) in the heavy weight, high value contrast.
- May fly over her briefly; never parked on her face or burying her for a line.
- Outside the Shorts UI bands; CTA readable >= 2.5 s before the end.

## Judging
At phone size: readable in one glance? Does it look designed with the picture or pasted on? Timing vs the voice
(reality / song map)? Cut off, behind her, clipping into things?
