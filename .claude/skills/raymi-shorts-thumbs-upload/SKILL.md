# Shorts, thumbnails, upload

Channel data (canon/10_kanal_puls*): lore Shorts ~1 sub / 100 views, song Shorts ~0.15; lore keeps 45-51 % past the
swipe, song Shorts 22-28 %. toki: hook strong in the first second, thumbnails 4-5 per format also for the Short, titles
BIG and readable at 168 px, upload texts in Raymi's voice.

## Shorts
- Hook a swiper perceives in 0.5-1 s: her face big and bright, one promise, <= 8 words, held >= 1.2 s.
- Visual change every 2-4 s; a comment question readable >= 2.5 s before the end; loops cleanly.
- Safe zones 1080x1920: top ~15 %, bottom 25-35 %, right ~140 px free of text.
- Song Short: 12-20 s around the hook, cut from the MV, a Raymi line or gag; ends on the music phrase.

## Thumbnails
4-5 candidates per format; one message, not a tidy layout; her face / the main element huge; a bold title readable
at 168 px on dark AND light feed (`python -m raymi thumbs`); thumb-critic ranks, toki picks. Compare with thumbnails of
the professional references of the same genre.

## Upload texts
Raymi first person, lowercase, kaomoji, no em dashes; titles search-first from real research (song name + "fan
animation" / "cover"), never jargon; credits complete (song, artists, music bed CC BY); never call her AI.
Files: `<upload day>_Raymi_<title>_<Short|MV>.mp4` + upload.md, sent in the chat (memory deliver-in-chat).
