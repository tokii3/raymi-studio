# Composition - every frame a designed picture

toki: "oft wirkt alles etwas leer und Raymi weit weg" (10-25), "leer, lieblos, ohne Details" (sets), "Places with love
that make sense". Source: knowledge/craft/05 Komposition und Form, studies/framing.md.

## Craft
- **One focus per frame**; the eye path starts there (brightest value contrast, sharpest, most saturated, a face).
- **Fill or empty on purpose**: she fills the frame, or the emptiness IS the statement (tiny in a huge wide). Never an
  accidental empty floor.
- **Depth**: foreground (blurred object, frame, hand, particles) / subject / background; overlap creates depth.
- **Measured, not taught** (knowledge/references/LEARNED.md, 5,999 pro MV shots, 2026-10-26): the performer sits in
  the centre band in 57 % of shots, on a thirds point in only 30 % - in an MV centring = presence, the eye does not
  search. Thirds for story / landscape / two-shots. ~45 % of the frame stays empty; the shorter the shot, the emptier
  and simpler (54 % empty under 0.25 s - a 4-frame shot must read at once).
- **Diagonals, too big for the frame, cut by the edges**: power and attack (ecu_scream, lens_attack cards).
- **Leading lines**; headroom small in close-ups.
- **Every placement has a reason + effect** you can name, like the pros' (`refs why`: decision -> why -> effect).
  No reference frame for a moment? Use its principle card (`refs card` / `refs measure <still> --card <name>`).
- **Frames inside frames** that belong to the world (porthole, phone, mirror, CCTV), with a reason.
- **Sets with love**: lived-in details with reasons, physically plausible, a material family; nothing floating.
- **Phone size**: judge at ~390 px wide; the main element must read there.
- **Depth roles in motion** (skill packs, 2026-10-27): background ambient (0.1-0.3x parallax, low contrast, soft),
  mid secondary (0.5-0.7x), foreground primary (1.0-1.5x, sharp on her; props crossing the lens blur).
- **Hierarchy order**: size > contrast > colour > position. Two elements at equal size and contrast = no focus.
- **9:16 safe area**: top ~12-14 %, bottom ~18-20 %, sides ~6 % are platform UI - nothing that matters there.
- **An entrance across more than a third of the frame in one unbroken move reads as a slide**: break it (a key in
  between) or pair the travel with scale / blur.

## Judging
Greyscale thumbnail: where does the eye land? Is the frame full, or empty by intention? Three depth layers? Anything
cut awkwardly (head, hands at joints, text at the edge)? Does the set look built with love or like a placeholder?
