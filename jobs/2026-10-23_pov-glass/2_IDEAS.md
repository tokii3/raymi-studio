# IDEAS - POV: you keep tapping her aquarium glass

Chosen from the POV Shorts plan of 2026-10-23 (nine claims, toki picked 1-7). Series dossier:
`knowledge/studies/pov_shorts_series_2026-10.md`.

## The idea
- **The one sentence:** You are the visitor who keeps tapping on her aquarium glass; she points at the sign, taps back, fogs the glass and writes NO, and when you tap once more she buries herself in the sand like a ray.
- **World:** the aquarium (set `aquarium`): her tank, the DO NOT TAP THE GLASS placard, the shell, the castle, sand; the hall dark, no crowd - just you at the glass.
- **Why only Raymi:** (1) a stingray buries itself in sand when bothered - only her body ends the scene this way; (2) the aquarium is her own KING world; (3) her breath fog writing (the fog lyric material) - a sea creature writing on the glass from inside.
- **Story of actions, muted:** Tap: she glares through the glass -> points at the sign -> you tap again, she taps back harder -> she breathes on the glass and writes NO -> you tap twice, she warns you -> one more tap: she dives and buries herself in the sand, only her eyes and ears show -> she asks how many taps you counted.
- **Main element:** her face at the glass, then the sand mound with her eyes; the glass taps (ripples) as events.
- **Hook (spoken + stamp, <= 8 words):** "stop tapping the glass."
- **CTA (spoken + stamp, >= 2.5 s before the end):** "how many taps did you count?"
- **Risk:** the camera-as-viewer moves must read as YOUR body (recoil, step, being grabbed), not as a cinematic
  move; her clips must keep her alive between lines (never a held pose for more than a beat).
