## Goal behind it
A viewer should feel hit and swept along from the first second (no slow intro - PRINCIPLES 6, Know My Name 69 % at
3 s), get goosebumps on the drops, and still want to pause on frames because they are beautiful. Why she wants it
(my reading, labelled guess): Love is War is a vocaloid classic about a love so desperate it becomes a declaration of
war (the original's image: Miku shouting into a megaphone on a ruin); the Mwk remix turns it into a hard dance track -
the video has to carry that force. Her own past verdicts point the same way: "statisch, undynamisch ... nichts wird
gewagt" (Electric Angel), "total langweilig" (Watch Me run 67) vs the parts she loved ("animationen, transitions und
effekte mag ich alle sehr" KING). Channel: an evergreen vocaloid song for the VTuber audience (CLAUDE.md 4).

- "on beat / melodie" -> every musical layer can drive the picture, not only the kick: kick, snare, hi-hat rolls,
  bass drops, risers / builds, stops and silences, the vocal line (each syllable, long held notes), the lead synth /
  guitar melody (its pitch going up and down), chord changes, the drop's first hit, a fill before a section.

- "effekte" -> not only overlays: impact frames (inverted / two-tone single frames), speed lines, smears, camera
  shake and crash zooms, light (her dots, strobes of her own glow, a lamp blowing out), colour flips per hit,
  shattering / tearing of the picture, type that slams in, physical things in her world reacting (water, ink, sand,
  glass), frame-rate tricks (holds, on twos, freeze), chromatic split, halftone / print grit, a cut itself as the effect.

  world reacting to the viewer, scale (tiny in a huge space, then huge in the lens).
- "schön und aesthetisch" -> composed frames, a colour script with calm masses + loud accents (craft-taste, never

  floating), her pale skin, materials that are not plastic - frames that work as a wallpaper.
