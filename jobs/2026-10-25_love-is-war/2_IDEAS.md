## Idea A - The Lighthouse That Never Looks
- **The one sentence:** the one she loves is a lighthouse - its beam sweeps over the sea and never stops on her - so
  the lamp in the dark goes to war against the storm to make the light look at her.
- **World** (built): the open sea at night in a storm (set nightsea: waves, rain with light rings, cloud over the moon,
  sea glow from below, split waterline lens) + a NEW lighthouse on a black rock (tower, rotating lamp, beam through
  rain) - one place, one storm, the lighthouse always on the horizon.
- **Why only Raymi:** 灯闇 = a lamp in the dark: a small living lamp against the biggest lamp of the sea. She rises
  from the deep, glides flat over the waves, pings with her electro-sense instead of a megaphone, predicts the weather
  wrong (the sky clears in verse 2).
- **Story of actions, muted:** She rises out of the black sea and sees the lighthouse beam pass over her. She shouts
  her ping at it - rings over the water - the beam passes again. Her ping breaks against the rock, the storm stops
  dead and a mocking moon comes out; she wipes the rain off her face and glares. She charges through the storm and
  climbs the rock, wave after wave, lightning on every hit. At the top she grabs the great lamp and turns it - the
  beam lands on the lens: on you.
- **Main element:** the lighthouse beam (and her own light against it) - readable in every key frame as one bright
  line in the dark.
- **Lyric medium:** none (toki 2026-10-25). The words become the beam, the pings and her mouth.
- **5 wows:** 9.36 lightning frame - she bursts out of the sea in the flash; 62.6 the war cry through the lens (ring
  bursts out of the frame); 79.35 the crane up on the held B5, her light a pillar in the storm; 105 the storm stops
  dead, a giant moon, her silhouette (the beautiful one); 150-160 she turns the lamp and the beam swings onto the
  lens (strongest, last).
- **The Short:** 62.2-80.5 (stop -> war cry through the lens -> the pillar on the held note); twist: the beam sweeps
  over the viewer at the end - "did it see you?".
- **Risk:** near FIRESTARTER's night sea (same set) - the storm, the lighthouse and the beam must make it new; the
  lighthouse is a new build (tower, lamp, beam in rain); a storm can turn grey and muddy (X1: beauty in light).
- **Key frames:** boards/A1.jpg (she rises in the lightning, ref edgerunners@74), A2 (the war cry through the lens,
  ref edgerunners@85), A3 (silhouette before the moon, ref edgerunners@91).

## Idea B - Ink War
- **The one sentence:** her world is a sheet of white paper and her weapon is Sumi's ink - love is a war she paints
  with her whole body, until the white is black and her light is the only thing left in it.
- **World** (built): a white paper void (floor and sky one sheet, a fold line as horizon) where ink is physical:
  splashes, slashes, bleeding wet edges, drying cracks. Three colours only: paper white, ink black, one red (the one
  she loves: a red paper heart / seal that is always out of reach), and her UV dots.
- **Why only Raymi:** Sumi (her mascot) is ink, rays are "flat as paper" (her LGW gag), her dots glow in the black
  (灯闇). The verse-1 verb of the song means ink bleeding into paper.
- **Story of actions, muted:** A drop of ink lands and she rises out of it on the white. She slashes ink at the red
  heart floating ahead - it drifts away, the slash falls short. Rain starts: her ink bleeds and runs, the brush snaps,
  she kneels in the grey wash and glares up. She stands, and paints with her whole body - every hit a stroke, every
  held note a long line that follows the tune - until the page is black. In the black only her dots shine: a night sky
  of her own stars, and the red heart sinks into her hands.
- **Main element:** the ink line (her melody stroke) - one black line the eye follows across the white.
- **Lyric medium:** none. The vocal melody IS drawn: a brush line whose height follows the sung pitch.
- **5 wows:** 9.36 a full-frame ink slash, she stands in the cut; 62.6 the declaration - ink explodes from her in a
  crown of spikes (two-tone frame); 86.4 the rain washes her world grey (Weltschmerz); 120.4 the page tears and she
  charges through the tear; 165 the page turns black, her dots become stars (strongest, last).
- **The Short:** 9.4-24.4 (first hit -> drop 1 -> the instrumental drop): pure ink action, the melody stroke on the
  held note.
- **Risk:** the 3D Raymi on a flat white void can read like a model on a backdrop (needs ink ON her, shadows, depth
  through ink layers); Renai and LGW both used Sumi ink (for writing) - here ink is the battlefield, not text; white
  can burn out (exposure check).
- **Key frames:** B1 (the ink slash on the hit, ref hsr@108 crack on white), B2 (two-tone declaration, ref ado@98),
  B3 (her stars in the black, ref hsr@112 beams in the dark).

## Idea C - Signal War
- **The one sentence:** the one she loves is a signal she can feel but not see - so she rides the light pulses of the
  sea-floor cables across the whole ocean to reach it, and lights up the world on the way.
- **World** (built): the deep sea floor crossed by real undersea data cables (thick armoured lines over black basalt,
  set deep) - pulses of light run through them on every beat; junction boxes, sparks, the cable climbing a cliff to
  the shore. Dark, cyan pulses, her purple.
- **Why only Raymi:** electro-sense is real ray biology (ampullae of Lorenzini: rays feel electric fields) - she is
  the one creature who can feel a signal in a cable; the song is a 2008 internet song (niconico) - love sent over the
  wire.
- **Story of actions, muted:** In the black she feels a pulse pass through a cable and follows it. She grabs the cable
  and sends her own pulse after it - it races away and vanishes into the dark. The cable snaps, sparks, blackout; she
  hits the floor in the dark and the field lines of her sense flicker out. She tears the two ends together with her
  hands, the current runs THROUGH her and she rides it, faster and faster across the ocean floor. At the end the whole
  network lights purple from above - a map of the world's sea floor, hers.
- **Main element:** the pulse (a light in a cable) - one travelling light per beat the eye follows.
- **Lyric medium:** none. The melody runs as a pulse train along the cable (pitch = brightness / speed).
- **5 wows:** 9.36 the first pulse explodes along every cable at once; 62.6 she sends her pulse - it rips through the
  lens; 86.4 the cable snaps - sparks, total blackout; 120.4 she joins the ends, the current runs through her body
  (two-tone electric frames); 165 the top-down: the ocean floor network lit purple (strongest, last).
- **The Short:** 119.9-137.2 (the stop -> joining the cable -> riding the current).
- **Risk:** a tech world can feel cold, not "schön"; cables are less iconic than a lighthouse; set deep is
  FIRESTARTER's place (needs the cables to make it new); the story needs her hands on the cable (clips: reaching /
  grabbing - partly MISSING, check the clip sheet).
- **Key frames:** C1 (the pulse along the cable on the hit, ref arcane@100 glowing veins), C2 (current through her,
  ref edgerunners@75 two-tone), C3 (the network lit, ref hsr@113 beams).

purple sea) one world. From B I would take the melody stroke idea as her light ribbon on the held notes.
