1. DESCRIPTION. Dominant colours are near-black navy (low value, medium saturation), violet-blue hair and skin (mid value, high saturation), and warm gold or cream beam light (high value, medium saturation).
- Light: the beam and lamp come from the upper right or behind the set. Raymi's face is lit frontally in a flat, bright way, from a different direction (m05, m07). She does not share the set's key light.
- Eye path: the eye goes to the brightest thing first, which is the gold beam or lamp. That is the face only in the close-ups (m07, m10, m11).
- Layers: foreground is rain dashes and ink, subject is Raymi, background is the wave layers and lighthouse.

2. COLOUR. The palette is cohesive, a strong navy, violet and gold complement. The big masses are calm and the gold is the accent, which is correct.
- Saturation competition: m08 floods the frame with saturated orange and black, so the orange competes with her. m17 and m18 add a muddy red disc (the lamp dome) that clashes with the violet.
- Dead area: m14 is about 40% dead navy at the bottom.
- Her skin: it holds pale grey-blue in m05, m07, m11 and m21. It turns plain white in m18 and m19, and a grey-lavender in m11 (the sleeve patches).
- Shadows: they are mostly darkened, not coloured. The sea is a black void, not a coloured shadow.

3. LIGHT.
- Keys: two keys fight. The beam (gold, from the upper right) and her face fill (cool, frontal) are separate.
- Contact: she has no cast shadow from the lamp and no gold rim from the beam in m06, m09 or m16. In m09 she stands in front of the beam with no spill on her. In m04 the dress has no shadow on the dark floor.
- Water: in m14 and m12 she sits in the water with ring graphics and no reflection. The set reads as a stage and she is pasted onto it.
- m18 and m19: the blow-out is not a controlled light. It is exposure clipping.

4. COMPOSITION AND DEPTH.
- Strong: m05 and m06 are strong close-ups, with a diagonal ink streak in m05 and a spike burst in m06. m09 has a good wide frame with the lamp beam as a horizon.
- Weak: m14 and m12 have no focus. The moon and lighthouse in m14 are better than she is.
- Crops: m16 is a low angle with a tilted tower in the same value as her. m15 crops her knees and legs awkwardly at the bottom edge.
- m13: a triangle shard cluster sits dead on her forehead and blurs her face into a smear.
- Grayscale: figure and ground are weak in m16 (white and red tower behind her white skirt) and m19.

5. ASSET QUALITY AND ARTEFACTS.
- m02: the whole frame is heavily blurred. She is unreadable and the blur looks like a failed upscale, not a designed motion blur.
- m11: grey patchy sleeve or shoulder shapes at the bottom of the face. These look like unfinished cuts, with a pale cuff colour that does not match her skin.
- m10: the mouth is a single flat dark capsule shape, like a paper punch. It is not painted and is out of style with the painted eyes.
- m07: the fang mouth in the extreme close-up is a flat vector fill next to the soft painted face.
- m05: jagged ink cut across the chest is a hard black polygon with sharp vertices. It is clip-art next to her.
- m15 and m16: the lighthouse tower texture is a banded plastic cylinder. The windows or brick on it are aliased and moiré.
- m21: gold pillars have a streaky motion texture on one side and are flat on the other. The sparkle crosses are generic stock particles.
- m09 and m15: the dashed circle is a thin generic HUD ring.

6. DETAIL AND LOVE. The wave layers (stacked paper-like ribbons) have a nice illustrated feel, and the lighthouse is a recognisable object. The rest is thin.
- Void: the sea floor is flat black with a few stroke rings, so it is an empty stage. In m03, m10, m12 and m14 there is nothing in the frame to look at beyond her.
- Throne test: the lamp room in m17 and m18 does not read as a lamp room. It reads as a gold bowl and posts with a red umbrella, which the viewer would not identify as a lighthouse lantern.
- Rain: the rain streaks are the same thin dash everywhere, with no depth variation.

7. ONE ARTWORK? Mostly, but the seams show.
- The painted wave world and the toon Raymi are aligned in style in m05, m08 and m21.
- The black polygon ink shapes (m05, m06, m08), the dashed HUD circle (m09, m15, m16) and the shard triangles (m13) are vector motion-graphics. They sit on top of the painted world and look like a different hand.
- The lamp interior (m17, m18, m19) is in a different, more plastic, lit-3D style than the paper-wave set.

Compared with the bar: Edgerunners and Chainsaw Man frames keep a hard value separation between character and background, with the hero face always the brightest or most saturated thing. Here the face is overpowered by the beam in half the frames. Their impact frames are single designed two-tone hits. Here m08 (gold flood) is a two-tone hit, but m17, m18 and m19 are exposure blow-outs, not designed hits.

Strongest frames (keep): m05, m06, m07 (the fang close-up, which is distinct), m09 as a wide frame, and m21, which is quiet and beautiful. m11 has a good look-down eye contact.

FIX 1
observation: m17, m18, m19 - the lamp-room frames blow Raymi to white or lose her entirely.
effect: the viewer loses the hero at the climax. The skin is no longer pale grey-blue, and the face is a white mask.
cause: bloom and exposure on the lamp is not limited around her, and her unlit sprite is lit by the same flood (module 02, 01).
change: cap bloom so her face luminance stays <= 0.85 and the skin hue stays at 220-240 degrees. Add a dark navy rim-only separation behind her head, and put the lamp flood behind her, not in front.
priority: high
level: structural:studio
retest: m17-m19 stills, face crop shows the grey-blue skin with eye detail, and a grayscale thumbnail still separates her head from the beam.

priority: high
level: structural:elements

priority: medium
level: local
