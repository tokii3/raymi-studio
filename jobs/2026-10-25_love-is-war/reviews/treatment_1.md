**Retell (muted).** Black, then violet dots light one by one and eyes open. A girl sleeps curled by a thick cable on the sea floor. A pulse runs up the cable and her eyes snap open. She glides up the cable through dark layers, pings three times and breaks into a storm. A lighthouse beam passes over her again and again. After a stop she screams into the lens, and ink detonates with a climbing line. Her light rises as a pillar, then comes a still moon. Her ping shatters on the rock, a wave throws her back, and a mocking moon appears. She wipes the rain away and glares. She climbs the tower, turns the lamp, and the beam stops on the lens. The sky inks over, her dots become stars, and it goes black. The text lets me see this. It is a real story, so the retell does not fail.
