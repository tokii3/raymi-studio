## 6. Where Raymi meets this song
Her name 灯闇 (lamp in the dark), body, lore, attitude (canon/01, canon/02 T01-T17):
1. The megaphone = her ELECTRO-SENSE / sonar (T02/T03): she cannot shout, so she "pings" - her feelings as rings that
   should reach him; verse 2's broken megaphone = the ping that comes back empty.
2. Her dots (T01) as weapons: the lamp in the dark goes to war - light as the only colour in a grey world.
3. The barb (T04): the sting under the cuteness - the declaration of war is a barb flick at the lens.
4. Grey clouds / blurred world (verse 1) = the sea above her turned grey; a storm (T12 rain predictions - she
   predicted sun, it pours; or in verse 2 the sky clears "wrong").
5. Sumi ink (T09) as war paint / the battlefield spreading; ink swallowing the grey.
6. UV purple claims the world (T14) as she wins ground - the grade turns from grey to her colour by the end.
7. Burying in sand (T06) = the trench; rising out of it = the charge.
8. Flat body / gliding (T05): she dives at the lens like a ray attacking from above - the immersive attack shot.
9. "Not crying" + tsundere (T15): she turns the tear into anger, mock-offended at her own feeling.
10. The salmon (T11): the one who sees her (comic relief would break "raw" - maybe one beat of loyalty only).
11. Water itself (T08): pressure, depth, a storm at sea - the war happens IN her ocean; caustics as beauty between hits.
12. The tako ears (T13) flattening back like a fighter's before the charge.
