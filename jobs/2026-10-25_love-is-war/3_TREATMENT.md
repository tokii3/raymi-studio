## The one idea
**"The one she loves is a lighthouse that never stops its light on her - so the lamp in the dark goes to war."**
One place: the open sea at night in a storm, a lighthouse on a black rock, and - under the surface - the sea floor
where the lighthouse's own power cable runs down into the deep. Three rules:
1. **The beam never stops on her.** It sweeps the sea on the bars of the song; it passes over her every time. Only at
   the end, when she has turned the lamp herself, does it stop - on the lens, on the viewer (C1, the strongest moment).
2. **Her war has two weapons from her body and lore:** her electro-sense (rays feel electric fields - T02) = the
   SIGNAL she sends up the cable and her sonar ping across the water (T03, the song's megaphone reinvented); and Sumi's
   ink (T09) = every hit she lands is a brush slash of ink across the picture.
3. **The world is drawn, not modelled** (toki: "eher richtung 2"): sky, clouds, waves, rain, rock and lighthouse are
   painted 2D layers in depth (parallax cards with ink contours and brush texture), every hit is a two-tone impact
   frame (black / paper white / one colour), the 3D Raymi stands in it with her own light. No primitive 3D props, no
   plastic (toki 10-25: "immernoch plastik look").
Fused with her ocean: the lighthouse is the biggest lamp of the sea, she is the smallest (灯闇 = a lamp in the dark);
the cable feeding the light runs through HER deep - she follows its signal up from where she lives.

## Main element(s) (the hero - read at phone size)
- **The beam** (`beam`, kind prop): one bright wedge of white-gold light in the dark - the love that never looks at
  her. Must read in acts 2, 4, 5 and the outro: as the brightest value of the frame, at least 25 % of the frame width
  when it crosses; its stop on the lens fills the frame.
- **Her face** (`face`, kind face): the war is in her face - glare, war cry with the mouth open, the tear wiped away.
  Every hit frame: face >= 12 % of frame height, mouth readable (no forced-closed mouth: the boards had `mouth:
  "silent"` everywhere - in the script every shout has the mouth open by sound or by expression).
- **The ink** (`ink`, kind word-free graphic): the black slash on each landed hit; at phone size a full-frame
  diagonal, never a small leaf (boards rounds 1-2).

## Raymi's part (not only the song)
- Attitude: fierce, stubborn, competitive about something she cannot win (canon/01: competitive about nothing; light
  tsundere: she would never admit she cried - X3). Her Weltschmerz is in the world (grey storm, a mocking moon), her
  answer is attack.
- Her own lore gag: T12 rain predictions - in verse 2 the storm clears exactly when she is at her lowest; she glares at
  the sky like it lied to her (she called rain - wrong again, rain record).
- Her light (T01): her dots are the only colour in the grey storm; at the end they become the stars of the ink night.
- The salmon (T11): one silhouette in the outro, swimming beside her in the lit water - the one who always sees her
  (one beat, no gag).

## Places make sense + love and detail
- **The sea floor** (act 1): dark sand with ripples, a few of her knick-knacks around her sleeping dip (shells, a spoon
  - T16), the cable half buried, crusted with barnacles, its armour joints; marine snow drifting. She sleeps here
  because the cable is warm (it carries the light's power) - the only warm place in the deep.
- **The storm sea** (acts 2-5): drawn waves with brushed crests and foam, rain in three depths (near streaks, mid,
  far haze), the rock black and wet with white surf at its foot; the lighthouse plaster cracked, salt stains, the red
  bands worn, the gallery rail bent; one window lit low in the tower (someone keeps it running - the light is a job,
  not a person who could look at her).
- **The lamp room** (act 5): the big Fresnel lens (painted rings of glass) that she grabs and turns.
- Physical sense: she floats in the water (never stands on it), climbs the rock and the tower on its ladder / stones,
  stands on the gallery floor (contact shadow).

## Animation principles in this video
- Anticipation 2-6 frames before every hit (pull back, compress), then the blow (impact frame) and a hold.
- Body on held key poses for the strikes (Arc System Works approach: the clip's strongest frame held 4-8 frames),
  hair and tail follow through.
- Smears on fast strikes (motion.ts smear), speed lines of varied weight only on the charges.
- Staging: one read per moment - her face, OR the beam, OR the ink.
