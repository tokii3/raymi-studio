# IDEAS - Rock paper scissors against a stingray

Chosen from the POV Shorts plan of 2026-10-23 (nine claims, toki picked 1-7). Series dossier:
`knowledge/studies/pov_shorts_series_2026-10.md`.

## The idea
- **The one sentence:** She challenges you to rock paper scissors, freezes so you can pick, and plays 'stingray' - then proves on the beach that a ray beats rock, paper and a crab's scissors.
- **World:** her beach (set `beach`): sand, the water's edge, a rock, a sheet of paper blowing, a small crab.
- **Why only Raymi:** (1) 'stingray' as a hand sign only works for her (flat hand gliding = a ray); (2) her flat ray body proves each win (glides, lies flatter than paper); (3) competitive about nothing: 'undefeated'.
- **Story of actions, muted:** She pops up with a fist: you lose -> rock, paper, scissors on the beat -> FREEZE: pick now -> she plays stingray -> glides over a rock -> lies flatter than a sheet of paper -> dodges a crab's pinch -> flexes: undefeated, what did you pick?
- **Main element:** her hand sign (fist, then the flat 'stingray' hand) big in frame; her face.
- **Hook (spoken + stamp, <= 8 words):** "rock paper scissors. you lose."
- **CTA (spoken + stamp, >= 2.5 s before the end):** "what did you pick? honestly."
- **Risk:** the camera-as-viewer moves must read as YOUR body (recoil, step, being grabbed), not as a cinematic
  move; her clips must keep her alive between lines (never a held pose for more than a beat).
