Judged from the 8 stills only (no motion, timing or critic kit). Phone size = the 16:9 frame scaled to about 20 % (390 px wide).

PER SHOT, v1 vs v2
- S_surface. v1: one line of about 60 px on the felt, small key word. v2: 3-row lockup (JP, romaji, English); the key word 思い is about 130 px and gold, and the rows follow the table's perspective, so it reads as lying on the felt. Missing in v2: contact shadow and any lamp light on the letters; 張り touches the card's bottom edge. At 390 px: 思い is about 26 px and readable; romaji (about 7 px) is not; English (about 10 px) is marginal.
- W_wide. v1: foam-white JP line crossing the shell and coral. v2: giant ライトサイド above the shell, the strongest frame. But the romaji and English rows sit behind the shell and coral, so "saido raito" and "side" are partly hidden (a cut word). The type hangs in open water with no carrier. At 390 px the key word survives, the lower rows do not.
- N_own_light. v1: small glowing line. v2: パッパッパ at about 130 px, white with a violet glow, which looks like a layer on top. The lightning bolt and a cloud show through the letters. Romaji (about 30 px, over green and navy) is gone at 390 px.
- C_close. v1: small cream single line. v2: bigger yellow key word, plus white tilted romaji and English floating on the map with no clear carrier. The ticker beside it ("WITH RAYMI") is more diegetic than the lyrics. Her eyes are clear. At 390 px only the key word survives.

HIERARCHY AND LEGIBILITY
- Hierarchy in v2 is much better than v1: key word about 3x the fillers.
- The m glyph reads as n ("onoi kone" for omoi kome), so the romaji is miswritten on screen.

VARIETY
- Only the colour changes (gold, foam, violet, yellow). The lockup recipe is identical in all four shots, and the words do not act (パッ does not pop, 照れ does not blush).

WHICH IS CLOSER
v2 is closer to type that belongs to the picture, because of scale, perspective on the felt, and the diegetic colours. It is still mostly a scaled-up layer. It lacks occlusion and contact shadow, scene lighting, words tied to an object (card, ticker, LIVE chip), and per-word behaviour. Extreme direction: one huge acted key word per shot, with the rest reduced or dropped. Half-hearted direction: the current three small rows nobody can read on a phone.

BLOCK: fault: W_wide v2, romaji and English rows partly hidden behind the shell and coral, so words are cut.
