# IDEAS - POV: you try to touch her tako ears

Chosen from the POV Shorts plan of 2026-10-23 (nine claims, toki picked 1-7). Series dossier:
`knowledge/studies/pov_shorts_series_2026-10.md`.

## The idea
- **The one sentence:** She warns you not to touch her tako ears, turns away, and every time she looks back your camera freezes a little closer - until she slaps her hand over the lens.
- **World:** her bedroom in the air dome (set `bedroom`): the shell bed, the knick-knack shelf with the tiny salmon plush, the round sea window.
- **Why only Raymi:** (1) her tako ears are her design and her canon fear; (2) she pretends to watch you while busy with her knick-knacks (her hobby); (3) the punchline is her own competitive streak - she ends it on HER terms, then asks how far you got.
- **Story of actions, muted:** She covers her ears and warns you -> turns to her shelf -> you creep a step -> she whips round, you freeze -> she turns away, you creep two steps -> she turns round, her face is right there -> she turns once more, you lunge -> her hand covers the lens -> from the door: she sits on her bed holding her ears, smug, and asks how close you got.
- **Main element:** her tako ears (big in the creep shots) and her face on each turn.
- **Hook (spoken + stamp, <= 8 words):** "don't touch my ears. i mean it."
- **CTA (spoken + stamp, >= 2.5 s before the end):** "how close did you get?"
- **Risk:** the camera-as-viewer moves must read as YOUR body (recoil, step, being grabbed), not as a cinematic
  move; her clips must keep her alive between lines (never a held pose for more than a beat).
