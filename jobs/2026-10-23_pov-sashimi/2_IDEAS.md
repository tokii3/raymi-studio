# IDEAS - POV: you caught her eating salmon sashimi

Chosen from the POV Shorts plan of 2026-10-23 (nine claims, toki picked 1-7). Series dossier:
`knowledge/studies/pov_shorts_series_2026-10.md`.

## The idea
- **The one sentence:** You open her door at night and catch her with salmon sashimi halfway to her mouth; she swears it is purple sweet potato - then her best friend, the salmon, presses his face to the window.
- **World:** her bedroom at night (set `bedroom`, dark 0.75, the lamp clicked on by you): she sits on the edge of the shell bed with a little shell plate of salmon sashimi and chopsticks; the round sea window behind her.
- **Why only Raymi:** (1) the salmon is her best friend - only her lore makes eating salmon a betrayal; (2) purple sweet potato is her real favourite food (her lie uses her own canon); (3) she bribes YOU, the lens, the only witness.
- **Story of actions, muted:** Light on: she freezes with a slice on her chopsticks -> lowers it, lies: purple sweet potato -> sweats: very orange purple sweet potato -> the salmon swims up to the window and looks in -> she jumps in front of the plate, waves: nothing here, just rice -> turns to you, finger on lips -> asks you whether she should tell him.
- **Main element:** the sashimi on her chopsticks / plate (the crime) and the salmon at the window (the witness).
- **Hook (spoken + stamp, <= 8 words):** "this is not what it looks like."
- **CTA (spoken + stamp, >= 2.5 s before the end):** "should i tell him? yes / no"
- **Risk:** the camera-as-viewer moves must read as YOUR body (recoil, step, being grabbed), not as a cinematic
  move; her clips must keep her alive between lines (never a held pose for more than a beat).
