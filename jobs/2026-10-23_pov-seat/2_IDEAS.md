# IDEAS - POV: you sat in her seat

Chosen from the POV Shorts plan of 2026-10-23 (nine claims, toki picked 1-7). Series dossier:
`knowledge/studies/pov_shorts_series_2026-10.md`.

## The idea
- **The one sentence:** You took the window seat on her deep-sea train; she glares at you from the aisle seat, explains why the sea belongs to her, and in a tunnel's blackout only her glowing dots slide across - lights on, she sits at the window and you are the one in the aisle.
- **World:** the deep-sea commuter train (set `train`, built for Take Me to the Beach) + a forward-facing seat pair by a window (new set option `pair`), the dark sea streaming past.
- **Why only Raymi:** (1) the claim 'i saw the sea first' only works for a sea creature riding a train under the sea; (2) the tunnel swap is shown ONLY by her bioluminescent chest dots moving in the black; (3) her ray rules ('rays have rules') + the salmon swimming past the window as the witness.
- **Story of actions, muted:** She glares at you (you sit at the window) -> leans in, you lean back -> she points past you at the sea outside: hers -> crosses her arms -> TUNNEL, black, only her dots glide across -> light: she sits at the window, you are on the aisle side, she waves -> asks you to pick a side.
- **Main element:** her face in the glare (big, readable) and later the window seat itself (the prize changes owner).
- **Hook (spoken + stamp, <= 8 words):** "you sat in MY seat."
- **CTA (spoken + stamp, >= 2.5 s before the end):** "window or aisle? choose wisely."
- **Risk:** the camera-as-viewer moves must read as YOUR body (recoil, step, being grabbed), not as a cinematic
  move; her clips must keep her alive between lines (never a held pose for more than a beat).
