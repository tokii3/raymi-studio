# CAMERA SCRIPT - Watch Me (K/DA) - written by hand, bar by bar (2026-10-25)

Why this file exists (knowledge/HANDOFF_2026-10-25_watchme_camera.md): seven takes had a camera made by RULES (director,
camera v3 = "3/4 angle + orbit") and toki found every one boring, frontal, generic, Raymi at the edge, and Raymi never
played with the lens. This script replaces the rules: every moment is designed for its bar of music, in the vocabulary
of toki's reference (K/DA FIRESTARTER MV 1:43-1:55, knowledge/studies/kda_firestarter_1m43.md), and SHE always does
something TO the camera. The story / worlds / gags of 4_SCRIPT.md stay; only the lens and her lens-play are rewritten.

toki's core (restated): Raymi flirts with and stages herself for the camera, which IS the viewer. The camera never
stands still, comes close, finds unusual angles, bursts on "move" / "K/DA" / "ay", moves (not cuts) between moments,
and she always fills the frame - never at the edge or bottom.

Times = measured (lyrics_timed.json word / line starts, song_map.json hits / stops). Lyrics on screen are OFF.

## The vocabulary (only these - studio part `raymi/camera_moves.py`, key = shots.json `{"move": {"kind": ...}}`)
| code | move (`kind`) | what the lens does | what SHE does to it | FIRESTARTER |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PULL | `pull` | extreme close on her eyes, holds a breath, pulls out and rises | eyes in the lens, a wink / a smile as we leave | close-up into the flare (114.7 s) |
| HERO | `hero` | low wide hero push: lens at her hip (0.75-0.85 m), fov 55, pushing in on an arc, looking up | towers over us, extended pose, looks DOWN into the lens | low wide on the high kick (111.7 s) |
| INTO | `into` | lens close and wide in front of her; a punch on contact | her hand / phone / wrist / tail comes INTO the lens (foreshortened, fills it) | hand thrust at the lens (103-107 s) |
| WALK | `walk_in` | from her full figure to her face filling the frame, accelerating | walks / steps / leans into the lens until her face fills it | push to her face (106 s) |
| DROP | `drop_worm` | overhead looking down (slow spin) - on the hit a 4-frame whip to the worm's eye, far, looking up | looks UP into the overhead lens, then the pose lands on the hit | overhead -> from below on the kick |
| 360 | `whip360` | a full turn round her in ~1 s from the hit, then a slow drift | dances, but her eyes stay on the lens the whole turn | the orbit in the smoke (108 s) |
| DUTCH | `dutch` | dutch close-up, the tilt flips sides on the hit | tilts her head against the tilt; a look / a smirk on the flip | head tilted close-up (113 s) |
| OTS | `ots` | behind her shoulder; when she looks back the lens slides round to meet her eyes | turns her back, looks back over her shoulder at us | (her back-look - toki's "Blick über die Schulter") |
| BURST | `burst` | a new angle on every hit, 4-frame whips between (felt moves, not cuts) | a new extended pose on every hit (kick, arms wide, reach) | the hit burst 111.7-114.7 s |
| PUSH | `slow_push` | a slow push on a held note | still - only her eyes / a blink / a breath | the slow push on the held phrase |
Transitions stay made of things: cover (palm / hair / tail / phone / frame bar), flash flare, a whip; the next shot's
lens starts where the move left off (`bridge`). No two neighbouring rows use the same code.
