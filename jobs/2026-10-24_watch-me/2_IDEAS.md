## toki's core, restated (the same in all three ideas - they differ in the PLACE)
ONE place, Raymi in its centre for 3 minutes. A fast "TikTok" film: a new angle / staging every 2-4 s, built by camera
moves and by things flying in and away around her - never by cutting to another world. Permanent self-staging: she
flirts, poses, presents herself to the lens, and the lens is the viewer. Her moves serve the picture: catwalk for the
wides, poses on the stops, short dance accents - poses also as held frames from the clips, inspired by voguing and
JoJo posing (toki 2026-10-24). Unusual angles and moves (worm's eye, top-down, dutch, orbit, crash zoom, vertigo).
Lessons from Beach (toki 2026-10-24): one colour script and one material set for the whole film (Beach read
"zusammengeklatscht"), she never clips, never falls in from the sky, moves fluidly; QC v2 judges the real picture.

## The spine (shared): a story with a turn, from her name 灯闇 (lamp in the dark)
1. **Cold open 0-8 s:** dark. On the hard stop at 7.5 s: "camera on" - one flash, she is already posed, looking
   straight at us. Strongest picture first.
2. **Verse 1 + chorus 1 (8-66 s):** she conquers the media one by one - each line a staging that assembles around her
   on the beat and flies away: the mosaic of her photo, the gold frame ("the Louvre gon' hang it"), pins dropping when
   she walks in, the magazine cover ("watch me pose", 2-3 poses), the forecast ("hurricane", "100 % chance of slay"),
   the radar blip ("on your radar").
3. **Verse 2 (66-83 s):** the flex - the phone post that crashes the app, the fashion flat-lay, the money plant; the four
   measured STOPS (66.3 / 69.5 / 73.2 / 73.8 s) are four hard poses.
4. **Chorus 2 (83-118 s):** the same stagings, faster, wilder angles - she is now everywhere at once.
5. **The turn - break 117.6 s:** "camera off". Every light dies, every staging falls away. In the black only her own
   glowing dots - the lamp in the dark. "Watch me" (118.5 s) in a single small light: she needs no stage, she IS it.
6. **Bridge + final chorus (128-177 s):** she turns the lights back on HERSELF - every earlier staging returns at once
   and builds the final, biggest one (all covers as one mosaic of her).
7. **Outro 177-182 s:** "watch me" - she winks and switches the camera off. Black, her dots, end card.

5 wows (all ideas): 7.9 s the first flash | ~45 s the first magazine cover assembling with 2-3 poses | ~53 s the
hurricane spiral / radar sweep around her (orbit) | 117.6 s camera off, only her lights | 177-182 s the final mosaic of
everything, then she switches us off.

## Idea A - The Shoot (a photo studio at night)
- **The one sentence:** a night photo shoot where every flash rebuilds the studio into the next thing she is on.
- **World:** a photo studio: white paper cyclorama (the curved backdrop), softboxes, light stands, sandbags, a camera
  on a tripod, the Salmon as her photographer (one creature, a running gag). Matte paper + black metal + one accent
  colour per staging; the cyclorama takes any colour from the lights.
- **Why only Raymi:** the studio light dies at "camera off" and her own dots light the set (灯闇); her tail sweeps the
  backdrop paper like a train; the photographer is her best friend Salmon.
- **Story of actions, muted:** she walks onto the empty backdrop and the Salmon raises the camera; every flash snaps a
  new set around her (cover, billboard, forecast) while she poses for it; at the break the studio blows a fuse - she
  lights herself; she takes the camera from the Salmon and shoots herself into the final cover; she switches it off.
- **Main element:** the camera flash / the frame of each picture (what "cover" she is in right now).
- **Lyric medium:** printed type of each staging (masthead, cover lines, ticker, weather card) - the words ARE the
  graphics that fly in.
- **The Short:** 7.9 -> 25.5 s - flash, "camera on, camera off", three stagings in 17 s, loop on the flash.
- **Risk:** a white studio can look empty and cheap ("billig, flach, leer") - needs dark edges, gear in the foreground,
  coloured light; the most "expected" of the three.
- **Key frames:** boards/A1.jpg (the flash), A2 (the magazine cover), A3 (camera off, only her dots), A4 (worm's eye
  catwalk on the backdrop)

## Idea B - The Corner (one streetlamp in the night city)
- **The one sentence:** a dark street corner with one streetlamp - wherever its light falls, the city becomes her stage.
- **World:** one corner: sidewalk, a zebra crossing (her catwalk), the streetlamp, a newsstand with magazines, a TV
  shop window, a billboard frame on the wall above, a bus-stop poster. Wet asphalt + neon + one warm lamp = a
  natural colour script (cold blue night, warm her). "Centerfolds turn the sidewalk to a show" literally.
- **Why only Raymi:** the lamp in the dark is her NAME; at the break the lamp dies and she is the lamp; the night street
  under the sea's blue light.
- **Story of actions, muted:** she steps into the lamp's circle; the newsstand covers flip to her, the TV window shows
  her "breaking news", the billboard prints her, the crossing becomes a runway; at the break the city blacks out; she
  glows, and her light switches the city back on block by block; she clicks the lamp off.
- **Main element:** the light circle of the lamp (where she stands = where the city is hers).
- **Lyric medium:** the city's own type - newsstand headlines, the TV ticker, the billboard, the crossing's road paint,
  the bus-stop poster, neon signs.
- **The Short:** 117.6 -> 134.2 s - the blackout, her glow, the city coming back on; loops on "watch".
- **Risk:** a street is more to build (props / detail pass); the wide must stay a CORNER (one place), not a city tour.
- **Key frames:** boards/B1.jpg (her in the lamp circle, worm's eye), B2 (the newsstand / magazine cover moment),
  B3 (blackout, only her dots), B4 (the crossing as a runway, top-down)

## Idea C - The Exhibit (after hours in the museum)
- **The one sentence:** after closing time, the museum's newest exhibit refuses to stand still.
- **World:** one gallery hall: a pedestal behind a velvet rope, gold frames on deep red walls, a bench, spot lights, a
  marble floor; fish visitors gather at the rope. Gold + deep red + marble = a classic, calm colour script with her
  pale blue as the accent. "But the Louvre gon' hang it" and "the same mosaic" are literal.
- **Why only Raymi:** a stingray as "the masterpiece" with the plaque "Raymi, 2026, deep sea"; she is the exhibit that
  breaks the rule (do not touch, do not move); at the break the museum lights go off and the exhibit glows.
- **Story of actions, muted:** she stands frozen on the pedestal like a statue; when the guard turns away she moves; the
  gold frames fly onto her and every painting in the hall becomes her (cover, portrait, mosaic); at the break the
  lights go off - she glows; she steps over the rope, every frame in the hall gathers around her; the guard turns back -
  she freezes in her final pose and winks.
- **Main element:** the gold frame (whatever it frames is the masterpiece).
- **Lyric medium:** plaques, gallery wall text, the frames' brass nameplates, the "do not touch" sign.
- **The Short:** 32.2 -> 49.3 s - the frozen statue starts to move on "watch me move", the frames fly onto her.
- **Risk:** a museum invites stillness (statue / frame = static) - must be fought with the fastest moves of the three;
  freeze gags must stay short.
- **Key frames:** boards/C1.jpg (frozen on the pedestal, low angle), C2 (the gold frame / magazine cover moment),
  C3 (lights off, only her dots), C4 (the hall of frames all showing her, wide)
