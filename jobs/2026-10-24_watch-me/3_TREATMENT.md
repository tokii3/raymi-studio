## The one idea
**"Every camera in the world is pointed at one stingray - and she rebuilds the world around her until it is a
picture of her."**
Raymi stands on ONE spot for three minutes. The WORLD moves instead: a night photo studio, a city corner under one
streetlamp, a museum hall after closing. Each world turns itself into a picture of her, and when it has become her
picture it breaks apart (flies away in pieces, dissolves into scraps, melts into her sea water) and the next world
assembles around her on the beat.
Rules:
1. **The spot.** Her feet stay on a 1 m circle (her mark). Walks are TREADMILL walks: her feet walk on the spot and the
   world slides past her (the studio floor tape, the zebra stripes, the marble tiles run under her toward the lens).
   Only TWO real steps exist in the whole film: off the plinth (141.0 s) and into the gold frame (178.0 s) - 0.5 m each.
2. **Every staging is a FRAME around her** (cover border, TV bezel, billboard, gold frame) - the main element.
3. **The lens is the viewer** and she knows it: she plays to it, covers it, and at the end switches it off.
4. **The water is everywhere from frame 0**: a thin film of her sea lies on every floor (wet concrete, wet asphalt, wet
   marble) - it mirrors her and the lights, it carries every melt, and it is what makes the three worlds one.
Her name 灯闇 (lamp in the dark) is the turn: when every camera goes dark (117.6 s) she is the only light left - and
she switches the world back on herself.

## The shared look (one film, not three - the Beach lesson "zusammengeklatscht")
What stays IDENTICAL in all three worlds:
- **Her key light:** one warm-white key (#fff1e0) from front-left, 35 deg above her eye line, in every shot of every
  world. Rim from behind in the act's accent. Her skin: colour lock 0.45, pale grey-blue in every frame.
- **The floor:** dark, wet, mirror-sheen (value 10-20 %), her reflection under her in every wide.
- **The type:** her heavy weight, ink #16121f + paper #f3ede2 + ONE accent; printed on matte paper or as neon tube -
  the same two materials in all worlds.
- **The accent pair:** pink #ff3d7f and cyan #7fe0ff in every world (gel, neon, plates); gold #d9a441 joins only in
  act 3 and the end.
- **Value plan:** background 15-35 %, her face 70-85 %, nothing clipped (look-critic boards 1).
- **THE LAMP (灯闇 planted and paid off):** one warm practical lamp is in every world and reads as the same lamp: the
  bare work bulb hanging above the studio backdrop (act 1), the streetlamp (act 2), the spot above the plinth (act 3).
  At 117.6 s it goes OUT; at 127.7 s her finger snap lights it again (the museum spot); at 182.5 s her hand clicks it
  off - the last thing in the film.
- **Grain + one grade family:** a new grade `watchme` (same contrast / saturation for all acts) with three background
  tints only: studio warm grey, street night blue, museum deep red. The finale alternates ONLY the background tint and
  accent gel per beat - never the whole grade.

## Main element(s) (the hero - read at phone size)
1. **The frame around her** (cover border, TV bezel, billboard, gold frame) - thick (>= 6 % of the frame height), value
   contrast against the dark set, lit by her key. heroes: `frame`.
2. **Her face** in the portraits and on every cover: eyes 38-40 % from the top, never under a word or a piece.
3. **"WATCH ME"** - one huge instance per act (masthead, billboard / road paint, museum banner).

## Raymi's part (her whole character, canon/01)
- **Show-off with a light tsundere streak:** after the blown kiss (8.0 s) she rolls her eyes and flicks her hair -
  "that wasn't for you" - then peeks back at the lens.
- **Sleepy baseline:** in the black (118.79-126.4 s; the measured line start - the dossier's 118.5 was analysis run 1) she does one slow, heavy-lidded blink at us - calm, almost bored,
  totally unafraid of the dark. The stillness IS her.
- **Competitive about nothing:** in the street the paparazzo Salmon flashes her - she grabs his camera and out-shoots
  him (she flashes HIM, 80.1 s).
- **Salmon, her best friend, escalates through the acts:** act 1 the photographer - her first pose makes him drop the
  camera; act 2 the paparazzo - she steals his camera and the storm blows him into the newsstand, he comes out with HER
  cover stuck on his face; act 3 the guard - she sneaks past him, and in the end she puts HIM on the plinth: he freezes
  as the new exhibit while she steps into the gold frame.
- **Lore gags:** rays are flat (she flattens into the centerfold, 54 s); her rain predictions (she calls the storm, and
  it comes, act 2); she is a deep-sea creature (the plaque "RAYMI, 2026 - deep sea").

## Animation principles in this video
- **Holds on hits:** every pose is a held animation frame (toki: "Stills aus Animation"), on the measured stop / hit,
  held 6-12 frames while hair and skirt keep moving (follow-through).
- **Anticipation:** a 3-4 frame wind-up before each pose; before each cover-cut the hand / hair / tail starts toward the
  lens a beat early - the transition is timed with her movement.
- **Overshoot** on every arriving piece, **squash** on the pins' landing, **smears** on whips and the hair-flip cut.
- **Fluid body:** full frame rate, clips cross-faded on matching poses, the treadmill walk in place (in-place clips),
  checked by QC v2 (jerk, slide, drop).
