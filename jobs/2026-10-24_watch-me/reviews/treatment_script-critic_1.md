**3. Against the top three**
- Renai, LGW and POP IN 2 each had ONE world with a physical logic.
- This is three sets with three grades (`clean`, `night`, `warm`), and the finale alternates palettes per beat. That is the Beach "zusammengeklatscht" fault. The "one material set" claim is a list of words, not a plan.
- LGW's turn is built from her name. Here the turn (camera off) is strong, but acts 1 and 2 are a parade of staging parts.
- Lines like "radar", "hurricane", "pins" and "cover" are used in all three worlds because the staging part exists. Principle 12 says the order is line, then picture, then asset. The elements table is asset-first ("exists").

**4. Dead stretches and opening**

Nothing says each pose is lit long enough to read, or that the thumbnail's picture appears in 0-3 s. Make flash 1 a held, readable, lit hero frame, not a blink.
- 24.83-31.12 is a single slow push for 6.3 s. That is acceptable only if a second event is added.
- 118.79-126.4 is an 8 s hold, deliberate, and it is where the film's biggest image sits.
- 126.4-129.3 has no row at all. That is 2.9 s unplanned before the museum.
- The bridge is calm (127.7-142.9). The museum starts at 129.3 with "on your radar" and then a statue gag. Check this against the measured map.
- 65.84-83.38 (verse 2, 17.5 s) and 83.38-117.6 (chorus 2, 34 s) give almost no camera direction. The camera is named only for the hair-flip and the dutch orbit. Chorus 2 mostly says "again". That is about 50 s, 28 % of the film, with the camera unspecified.

- The red-sole Loub shoe portrait is a real brand trade dress. The dossier says keep brands off screen.

FIX 1

effect: Lyrics become a subtitle layer, or are left to the studio, and read "aufgesetzt".
cause: Principle 11 and the exemplar depth.
