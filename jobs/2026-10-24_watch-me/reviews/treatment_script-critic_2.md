FIX 1
observation: 165.19-171.94 is a locked MS where only the background tint changes on the beats. It sits at the climax.
effect: The viewer feels the camera has stopped just when the film should peak, and it reads like slides switching.
cause: Principles 7 and 13, and toki's lens-cover transitions.
change: Make every beat change a cover-cut. Hair, then tail, then palm, then a gold frame bar fill the lens for 3-4 frames, and the background tint changes behind it. Add a slow orbit or crash zoom between beats, with a different angle per beat (low, dutch, top-down).
priority: high
