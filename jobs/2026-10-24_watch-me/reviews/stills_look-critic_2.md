1. DESCRIPTION
- Dominant colours: (a) warm beige/tan, about H30, S25, V85, for the opening wall (01-07, 16). (b) near-black oxblood/maroon, about H350, S70, V15, for the dark gallery and street (08-13, 17-21). (c) Raymi's indigo-violet hair, about H255, S85, V45, with pale lilac-blue skin, S25, V90.
- Accents: pink/magenta lyrics, orange fish and picture frames (gold/amber), news-blue screens.
- Light: on Raymi it is soft, frontal and even, close to unlit. On the set it is a top spot or bulb (04, 06), then spot cones from above-left (09, 10, 13, 17, 19). The two are not consistently on the same side. In 17 the floor spot lights the pedestal but her face is lit by an unrelated flat key. In 15 the orange glow is behind her and she has a lilac rim.
- Eye path: starts on her eyes or face where the frame is close (01, 05, 16). On the wide shots (04, 08, 10) it goes to the brightest saturated object instead, which is the white light bulb, the orange fish, the pink word or the yellow NEWS sign. It ends on the lyric.
- Foreground / subject / background: usually lyric and props in front, Raymi in the middle, set behind. Depth is lost where the set is a gradient wall (02, 03, 16).

2. COLOUR
- Saturation: several peaks compete. In 10 the pink/red lyric, the orange fish, the pale blue panel and the blue-white rain all fight her. In 13 the amber frames and spot are louder than her. In 15 the orange block overpowers her. The saturation peak should sit on her eyes (the violet iris is the only really good small accent).
- Masses: the tan opening is calm and clean (02, 03, 16), but a bit sepia and low in contrast. The dark maroon and black of 08-12 and 20 is heavy and murky, with big near-black areas (11, 12, 20) where only her and the lyric exist.
- Muddy/grey: the lyric colours are muddy brown-grey on dark red (13, 14, 15, 18, 19) and look dirty. The stone pedestal and the fish in 18 and 19 are creamy-yellow-washed and flat.
- Shadows: coloured shadows are mostly absent. They are darkened, not hued. The exception is the tan wall's warm shadow (04).
- Skin: pale grey-blue is kept in most shots, but is blown to near-white (05, 21), or pinkish and magenta in 08 (the colour grade breaks her skin). Her hair goes electric blue-violet in 01 and 02 and looks plastic/glossy, rather than matte-toon.

3. LIGHT
- One source: no. She stays flat-lit in dark scenes (08, 11, 12, 20), as if lit by a separate light from the camera.
- Contact: 04 is the best (cast shadows on wall and floor, even a dropped lyric shadow). 17 and 18 have a small shadow under the feet on the pedestal. 19 shows none under her; she floats. 13 and 14 have no readable floor contact. 20 shows floor shadow rectangles that belong to nothing (they look like holes in the floor).
- Verdict: she is not standing in the set in 08-12, 14, 19, 20. She looks pasted in. The set's red/amber light never wraps onto her hair or skin.

4. COMPOSITION AND DEPTH
- One focus: 01, 03, 05, 16, 21 are strong. 04 is a nice diagonal-free centred idea but she is too small. 07 is chaos (tilted plane, pink poster, lyric on three layers). 10 is rain-and-streak overload. 17 is a good bird's-eye shot, but she is tiny and her face is not visible (hair top view). 18 is the best layered museum shot (floor, statue, her, frames) but her head is cropped.
- Grayscale: the dark shots (08, 11, 12, 20) separate poorly, because hair is near the background value. 11 works only because the wall is pure black.
- Awkward crops: 18 crops her head; 15 crops posters and a green "e" tag at top right (green bar x=1875, y=100-150 reads as a leftover label); 19 crops the "PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH" sign plus a pole through its text; 09 crops "ME" sign at the top.
- Depth: the beige wall shots have none. The gallery shots have good layers.

6. DETAIL AND LOVE

It deserves more: the walls are a flat maroon with weird splotches (the 12-20 wall texture looks like dirty stains or mould and not wallpaper or plaster), the floor is tile with noise.

- The street scene has some good ideas (RADIO - 24H, NEWS stand, BREAKING NEWS monitors) but they are dim, small and low-res, and the rain/wind streaks bury them. The NEWS box sign (09) is flat and reads as a green dumpster.

7. ONE ARTWORK?
No. Four hands are visible: (1) the soft studio-gradient wall with bokeh and glossy 3D character (01-03, 16); (2) the dark-maroon gallery (13-19) with a stylised matte look and good coloured light; (3) the news-street night scene (08-11) with a cold blue-black palette and scanline monitors; (4) the overlay layer (cloud lyric font, rain streaks, glitch bands, pink paper, hex bubbles, grey-brown dirty lyric). Her model is the same, but it is lit and graded differently in each (neon violet in 01-02, pink in 08, white-washed in 05 and 21). The film does not read as one world.

FIX 1
observation: 14, 19 (and the transparent lower body edge in 20): Raymi's legs, skirt and hair turn translucent, and the set shows through her.
effect: the main element looks like a ghost or a render error and loses her ground contact.
cause: opacity or depth-blend on the sprite (or a fade effect left on) and no alpha-clean (02, 09).
change: force her material to opaque (alpha 1.0, depthWrite on) in every shot. Check with the frame-diff that her silhouette has the same alpha in the whole frame. A cut-out or dissolve moves into a transition and is not on a held shot.
priority: high
level: local
retest: in 14 and 19 the floor and wall pixels behind her legs must not show; stills 14, 19 show an opaque body.

FIX 2
observation: 04, 08, 12, 17, 20: Raymi is small, dark or has her face hidden by hair; 18 crops her head; 05 and 21 blow her face to white.
effect: the viewer cannot find the main element in the first glance and loses the emotional connection in these beats.
cause: framing (05), value (02), her hair gets lit as a plain flat mass.
change: keep her face at least 15% of the frame height on every wide shot (04 needs a camera push or a doubled scale); hold the head fully in frame (18 needs a tilt down by about 12% of frame height); add a rim light in the set's colour on her hair so her silhouette separates in dark shots (value gap of at least 25 L* from the wall); clamp the face highlight below 245 in 05, 21 (cut bloom on skin).
priority: high
level: local
retest: phone-size grayscale of each of these frames, her head must read in under a second; hero measure has a contrast over 3:1.

FIX 3
observation: 06 a flat pink rectangle on her torso; 07 a pink poster plus a doubled lyric over her legs; 10 rain streaks, paper flakes and a pale blue panel all across her; 15 an orange slab and a lyric through her leg.
effect: noise on top of the subject, so she looks censored or hidden, which reads as an effects pile, not a design.
cause: overlay objects (paper, rain, lyric) are placed without a depth rule or a keep-out zone on the main element (05).
change: keep-out zone for her face and torso: no overlay, lyric or prop with a pixel in her bounding box except lyrics at under 50% opacity. Rain and wind streaks to max 12 lines per frame and behind her. The pink paper in 06 moves behind her or becomes a floor prop.
priority: high
level: structural:studio
retest: a keep-out check in the frame report across all shots; stills 06, 07, 10, 15 are clean over her body.

FIX 4
observation: 12, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19: grey-brown or pale-pink lyric on dark red or saturated orange. In 20 it is a squeezed sliver; in 05 it sits over her mouth and braces show.
effect: the lyrics, the second element of an MV, look dirty and are unreadable at phone size; the font looks scribbled.
cause: lyric colour is not chosen against the local background (01 colour contrast, 09).
change: lyric fill off-white or warm cream (L above 85) with a 3 px dark outline and a small glow on dark scenes; on the orange scene use deep violet or cream with dark outline; drop the stray "{ }" characters; enforce a contrast of at least 4.5:1 against the lyric's local background; no squashed text (20).
priority: high
level: local
retest: measured text contrast per shot over 4.5:1; all lyric stills legible at 360 px width.

FIX 5
observation: 02, 06, 10, 13, 14, 18, 19: the fish is a low-poly orange blob with a black dot eye, a flat triangle mouth, and unclear mic or hat props; the NEWS box is a green dumpster; the pink arc loop (09-11) has no meaning; hex bubbles (09) cover her body.
effect: it looks like a cheap clip-art sidekick next to a detailed anime face, which breaks the quality bar and toki's "Liebe zum Detail".
cause: prop modelling (09 digital/3D): no texture, no toon ramp, no outline, no secondary detail.
change: rebuild the fish with the same toon ramp, a thin dark outline, a soft belly gradient, fins as shapes with a visible edge, a big highlight eye, one clear signature item (a mic or a hat, not both). Replace the hex bubbles with small soft circles only around her, behind her, in the set's accent colour. Give the news stand a roof, a front and a rolled paper stack; cut the pink arc loop or make it a clear sign (a halo or an "ON AIR" light).
priority: high
level: structural:assets
retest: the fish, news stand and bubbles side by side with her face on a turn-around sheet; they match line weight and shading.

FIX 6
observation: opening 01-07 and 16 is a beige gradient wall with bokeh dots, 12 and 20 have a plain black void; the news scene (08-11) is a near-black room with dim props.

In the news scene raise the monitors and set lights by one stop so the blue and the red rim wrap her hair. Give the gallery wall a proper plaster texture or wallpaper instead of the splotchy stain pattern.
priority: medium
level: structural:elements
retest: the first-glance still of every scene shows a floor, at least 3 props, and a light pool where she stands.

FIX 7
observation: 01 and 02 show electric violet glossy hair and a magenta rim; 08 pushes her skin pink-magenta; 05 and 21 are white-clipped; 17 and 18 show hair as spiky fans.
effect: she changes colour and material from shot to shot (plastic, pink, white), so it is not one character in one world.

change: lock the skin to pale grey-blue (hue 230-245, S 15-25, V 85-92) with a mild set-colour tint of at most 15%; lock hair as a matte mid-violet (V 40-55) with a single rim; limit bloom; for fast hair motion cap hair-sway amplitude so strands do not fan out (17, 18, 20) and fix the left-hip cut-out in 20.
priority: medium
level: local
retest: measured skin and hair colour in the face box across all 21 stills within a tolerance band.
