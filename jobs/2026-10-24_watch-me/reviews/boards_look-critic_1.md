# look-critic on the idea boards, round 4 frames (2026-10-24) - verbatim, the builder does not edit this file

MAIN ELEMENT: no heroes.json for boards. I take Raymi (face and figure) as the main element, plus the "WATCH ME" lettering in A2 and B2. She is readable in A2, B2, C2, A3, B3 and C3. She is NOT readable in A1 (blown out), B1 (face a few pixels in a black frame), B4 (about 70 px of hair seen from above), C1 (dim, cropped at the knees) and C4 (about 60 px tall, behind heads). The unreadable frames are BLOCK: fault.

Place A is a white photo studio with a hot spotlight, B is a night street of news kiosks with neon, and C is a gallery in red and gold. I looked at the 12 frames at full size and judged each at phone size.

## Shared faults in all three rows

1. Her skin is not pale grey-blue. Wherever the set is dark or lit blue, her skin goes saturated blue-violet (A3, A4, B1, B3, C3, A1). That breaks the studio rule that her skin stays pale grey-blue under any light. She is also much more saturated than the set, so her palette fights the set instead of sitting in it.
2. Her hair is a near-pure saturated blue (#2000ff range) with hard black cut lines. Against a dark background it merges into the background.
3. Hard white and black cut-edge lines show around her in A1 (hair, skirt, leg) and C2 (skirt, hair). These look like sprite edge fringes.
4. A stray black pole hangs from the top in A1, A2, A3 and A4. It looks like a lighting boom or a stand that points at nothing. It reads as a mistake.
5. The hero lighting is applied to the sprite, but no light falls from the set onto her. Wherever there is light, as in A1, A2, C2 and C4, she looks lit by something else than the set.
6. The cinematography is inconsistent. Some frames are blown out to white (A1), others crushed to black (B1, B3, C1, C3). That does not look like a deliberate board.

## Row A: photo studio (A1-A4)

**A1: BLOCK: fault, exposure**
- What works: the dance pose is strong, with an arabesque, a real cast shadow on the floor, and a clear silhouette.
- Fault:
  - The centre is clipped to pure white over about 60% of the frame, with a yellow-brown vignette ring.
  - The floor and the wall are the same white, so there is no horizon and no depth.
  - Her white skirt and skin dissolve into the white.
  - Black outline fringes show on the leg, skirt and hair.
  - On the left there is a half-cropped black slab and a gold panel, which are unreadable props.
  - The boom pole hangs from the top for no reason.
- Phone size: only her dark hair and dress read, and the face is lost.

**A2: mixed**
- What works:
  - The pink "WATCH ME" poster is a strong, readable idea, and the neon text reads.
  - Raymi's blue against pink is a good complementary pair.
  - Her face is readable.
- Faults:
  - The four text plates overlap the poster and her. "100% CHANCE" and "DEEP SEA STAR" cover her shoulder and hair.
  - Translucent plates and white soft circles lie across her like smudges.
  - The barcode at the bottom right is a flat clip-art rectangle.
  - The floor shadow is a huge diagonal wedge that has no source.
  - The poster's drop shadow does not match her shadow.
  - She is leaning on the poster, but her leg does not touch the floor convincingly.
  - The pole hangs at the top left.
  - The pink room is flat, calm and empty, with no detail or texture.

**A3: BLOCK: fault, exposure and colour**
- What works: her face, her pose and the blue-violet separate from the grey-black background.
- Fault:
  - The background is grey-black with visible banding (concentric rings in the spot).
  - There are cut-out blobs on the left, and the dark-green and yellow halos around the wall shapes look like tonemapping artefacts, not design.
  - Her figure is cropped at the shin and the foot, which is an awkward crop through a joint.
  - The set tells nothing about the place, so it is just a void.
  - Her saturated blue skin is off-model here.

**A4: mixed**
- What works:
  - It is a quiet, calm frame with a clean blue gradient.
  - The paper-roll backdrop is a believable studio prop.
  - Her face is readable and she looks at the camera.
  - The low camera angle is a nice idea.
- Faults:
  - She is small, at the bottom centre, with no floor contact. Her legs are cropped at the frame edge and she floats against the paper.
  - The roll's edge cuts the top right on a diagonal, but nothing else uses the shape.
  - The boom pole intrudes at the top.
  - The set is a single empty backdrop with no props, so the "photo studio" is not established.
  - The sprite is lit hotter than the paper.

**Row A verdict:** weakest row. A1 and A3 are technical failures (exposure, banding, fringes). A2 has the best idea, but its overlaps and the barcode look cheap. A4 is clean but empty. FAIL.

## Row B: news street at night (B1-B4)

**B1: BLOCK: fault, her figure is lost**
- What works: the cyan "TV - RADIO" sign, the news monitors with "BREAKING NEWS", the red bollard and the sodium-orange light cone give a colourful night-street palette.
- Fault:
  - She sits in near-black at the bottom centre. Her hair is dark navy against black, her face is about 50 px, and her figure is cropped at the knee.
  - The brightest areas of the frame are the pale-cyan panel on the left (an unlabelled prop) and the big orange rectangle at the top. Both pull the eye away from her.
  - The "TV - RADIO" text is cut off at the right edge ("RADI-").
  - The "NEW" sign at the right is cropped.
  - A pink ring at the left has no reason to be there.
  - The orange light cone is a flat translucent triangle, so it reads as clip-art, not light.

**B2: best frame of the set, but with faults**
- What works:
  - The "WATCH ME" poster as a hero card, readable "RADIO - 24H", "NEWS", and the red bollard.
  - Her face and body are clear and centred, with a warm pool of light on the floor in front of the poster.
  - The palette of pink-red, cyan and yellow, plus her blue, reads as a lively neon street.
  - This is the only frame in row B where the set and she read as one place.
- Faults:
  - The text plates cover her hair on both sides (the "100% CHANCE" plate and "DEEP SEA STAR").
  - The soft white circles across the poster and floor look like blobs.
  - The grey circles at the top right and bottom right (floating discs) are meaningless.
  - The pink ring on the left is cut off.
  - The "WATCH ME" text overlaps the "TV - RADIO" sign behind it and collides with it.
  - The barcode patch at the bottom is flat clip-art on a 3D floor.
  - The red bollard is plasticky and flat-shaded.
  - The "BREAKING NEWS" monitor is tiny and mostly hidden.
  - Her feet stand on the warm floor, but no cast shadow is visible.

**B3: BLOCK: fault, exposure**
- What works: it is her best rendering, with a clear face and a dynamic arabesque. The big dance pose has presence.
- Fault:
  - The background is about 90% pure black.
  - The prop shapes that remain (a grey-blue panel, a news kiosk) are clipped and unreadable. Moiré and dither grids show in the dark squares at the monitors.
  - There is no floor, no contact and no cast shadow, so she floats in a void.
  - Her skin is saturated blue.
  - The "NEWS" sign is cropped at the right.
  - Her hair shows hard dark outlines on the right, and there is no light connecting her to the street.

**B4: BLOCK: fault, her figure is lost**
- What works: the top-down radar idea is the freshest thing in the set. It is a clear graphic and a strong gag for "on your radar".
- Fault:
  - She is a tiny dark-blue blob (about 70 px) at the centre of the radar.
  - The floor is pure black, and you see only the neon green rings.
  - The red bollard, the orange spot, the beige ellipse and the white dot are scattered with no meaning.
  - The "pavement" strip at the top is dim and muddy.
  - The radar looks pasted on, since it is an overlay with no floor under it.
- It is an idea to keep, but it needs her large, readable and lit.

**Row B verdict:** B2 is good, B4 is a good idea with an invisible figure, B1 and B3 are underexposed. The street has the most atmosphere and the strongest palette, but the exposure and the black voids cost her. FAIL.

## Row C: gallery in red and gold (C1-C4)

**C1: mixed (not a block, but weak)**
- What works: the warm gold frames against the teal paintings are a rich, painterly colour pair, and she is readable as a pale shape.
- Fault:
  - Everything is very dark (the wall is near-black red) and the frame is dim.
  - She is cropped at the shin and sits mid-frame at a small size.
  - A black cable or pole cuts diagonally through the right half of the frame and crosses the right painting.
  - A small orange dot at the top left is a stray light.
  - The painting on the right is cropped, and the white sign and the brass stanchion at the bottom right are cropped.
  - The dark "spot" top-centre looks like a dirty smudge.
  - There is little floor, so no grounding.

**C2: strongest frame of all 12**
- What works:
  - The gold frame (a portal frame) around her works as a hero frame in the picture. The composition is symmetrical, with a clear focus, and the spotlit plinth gives her a floor and a cast shadow.
  - The colour plan is strong: gold, crimson and teal, with her violet-white as the peak.
  - Depth is good: stanchions, a checkered floor, the "EXIT" sign, and paintings with spotlights.
  - It looks like one designed place.
- Faults:
  - The red panel behind her is a flat rectangle, and the halo circle behind her head is a gradient disc.
  - The big gold frame is flat-lit, very saturated and heavy, so it competes with her. Its yellow is the loudest thing in the frame.
  - The "PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH" sign at the bottom right is cropped and the text is cut ("PLEAS", "TO"), which reads as an error.
  - The stanchion at the bottom right cuts through the sign.
  - The paintings are simple, flat, clip-art-like icons (waves, a leaf).
  - Her pose is a leaning leg-kick, and her hand and arm are hidden at the hip.
  - White fringe lines appear on her skirt edge.
  - She is small relative to the frame and the dark room.

**C3: BLOCK: fault, exposure**
- What works: she is well drawn and the face is visible.
- Fault:
  - The background is a pure-black void. Two barely visible frame outlines at the back are the only trace of the gallery.
  - There is no floor, no contact and no cast shadow.
  - She floats and is cropped at the knee.
  - It is the same shot as A3 and B3, so it adds nothing to the idea of "place".

**C4: BLOCK: fault, her figure is lost**
- What works:
  - The wide shot with a real camera angle is the most "film" frame of the set. It shows depth, with a spotlight cone, a plinth, a blue pool of light, and viewers with phones.
  - The audience idea (people filming her) is a strong narrative idea for "Watch me".
  - The "100% CHANCE" screen is a nice detail.
- Fault:
  - She is tiny (about 60 px) and cut off by two viewers' heads and a blue monitor. Their silhouettes cover her and a white disc covers her face.
  - The viewers are crude capsule figures with no detail, in flat saturated colours. They look like placeholders, not like part of the same world, and their phones are flat rectangles.
  - The grey-blue disc and floor circle are translucent and flat.
  - The "100% CHANCE ...LAY" text is cut off and overlapped by phones.
  - The "PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH" sign is tiny and illegible.
  - Heavy perspective on the checker floor pulls the eye to the foreground heads.

**Row C verdict:** the best place in the set. C2 has real art direction, and C4 has the best idea (an audience). But C1 and C3 are too dark, and C4 loses her. The paintings are clip-art-like, but the set holds together as one place. FAIL as a row, and the most promising one.

## Overall

FIXES (most important first)

FIX 1
observation: B1, B3, B4, C1, C3, A3 are crushed to black; A1 is clipped to white; C4 loses her.
effect: the viewer cannot see the main element or the place. It reads as an unfinished render.
cause: no key light on the sprite or the set, hard exposure extremes (modules 02, 09)
change: set one exposure per place so that her face sits at value 70-85% and the background at 15-35% (never 0 or 100%). Add a visible practical light from the set that falls on her.
priority: high
level: structural:studio
retest: grayscale histograms of every board frame show no clipped 0 or 255 region larger than 5%, and her face is above 60 px high at 1080p.

FIX 2
observation: her skin is saturated blue-violet in A3, A4, B1, B3, C3, A1.
effect: she looks neon-blue, not pale grey-blue. It breaks the rule and her look.
cause: sprite lit by blue or emissive light (module 01)
change: keep the sprite unlit with a fixed pale grey-blue skin. Light only the set around her, and put the coloured light into the rim.
priority: high
level: structural:studio
retest: sample her skin: saturation under 35% and lightness 60-80% in every frame.

FIX 3
observation: the boom pole hangs from the top in A1, A2, A3 and A4. A black cable crosses C1.
effect: reads as a mistake.
cause: prop or camera rig visible in frame (module 05)
change: remove or hide the pole, or make it a clear lamp with a light and a head.
priority: high
level: local
retest: no unexplained object touches the frame edge in any frame.

FIX 4
observation: overlapping text plates and translucent circles on top of her in A2 and B2; cut text in B1 ("RADI"), C2 ("PLEAS", "TO"), C4 ("...LAY").
effect: smudges across the hero and looks like a layout error.
cause: layout without a safe area (module 05)
change: keep text plates beside her, never overlapping her hair or face. Cut nothing at the frame edge, or crop cleanly through a clear overlap. Drop the white blobs.
priority: high
level: local
retest: in A2 and B2 her head and torso have no overlay in front of them. All visible words are complete.

FIX 5
observation: the small figure in C4 and B4, and a crop at the knee in A3, B1, C1, C3.
effect: the main element is not the focus, and the crops cut through joints.
cause: scale and framing (module 05)
change: in every wide frame her figure must be at least 25% of the frame height. Keep the full body or crop at mid-thigh, not at the knee. Move the audience heads away from her.
priority: high
level: local
retest: figure height at phone size is over 250 px in a 1080 px frame.

FIX 6
observation: flat clip-art props: the barcode (A2, B2), the paintings (C1, C2), the capsule people (C4), the translucent light cone (B1), the discs (B2).
effect: the props look like placeholders next to her painted figure.
cause: no consistent prop quality and no texture or wear (modules 09, 05)
change: give each prop a matte, textured toon shader with a contact shadow and one detail layer (wear, scuffs, grain). Build the audience with at least a face turned away, hair or coats. Remove the discs and the barcode.
priority: medium
level: structural:assets
retest: each prop shows a shadow and a texture at phone size.

FIX 7
observation: no contact shadow or floor plane for her in A3, A4, B1, B3, C3.
effect: she looks pasted onto the frame.
cause: missing grounding (module 02)
change: always give her a floor with a cast shadow in the light direction of the set's key light, as in A1, C2.
priority: medium
level: local
retest: every full-body frame shows a shadow touching her feet.

FIX 8
observation: C2 and B2 are the frames that work.
effect: they show that the set can carry her when it has a strong hero shape (the frame, the poster) and a light pool.
cause: strong focal shape plus light pool (modules 05, 02)
change: build the other frames on the same principle (one hero shape behind her, one spotlit floor pool), and tone down the yellow frame in C2 so that it does not out-shout her.
priority: medium
level: local
retest: in C2 the frame is less saturated and bright than her face.

## Answer to the question

Row A: weakest (white blow-out, void, banding, stray pole). Row B: best palette and the freshest ideas (radar, news street), but two frames are black voids and one loses her. Row C: the best place and the most complete artwork (C2), with the best narrative idea (the audience in C4), but two frames are too dark and one loses her.

BLOCK: fault: A1 (exposure clipped to white, black edge fringes on leg/skirt/hair, stray boom pole)
BLOCK: fault: A3 (grey-black void with banding rings, cropped at the shin, saturated blue skin)
BLOCK: fault: B1 (figure lost in black, cut-off "RADI"/"NEW" text)
BLOCK: fault: B3 (90% black, moiré grids on the monitors, no floor, floating)
BLOCK: fault: B4 (figure about 70 px, unreadable, floor pure black)
BLOCK: fault: C3 (black void, floating, no floor)
BLOCK: fault: C4 (figure about 60 px and covered by heads, cut-off text, capsule-figure placeholders)
BLOCK: fault: A1, A3, A4, B1, B3, C3, C1: her skin is saturated blue, not pale grey-blue

Frames that work (not blocked): A2 and B2 (text plates must come off her), C2 (the best frame of the set, cut signage must be fixed). A4 and C1 are weak but not faults.
