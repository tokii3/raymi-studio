## Idea A - The Lamp in the Dark (deep-sea vents)
- **The one sentence:** at the bottom of the ocean, where nothing can burn, Raymi strikes her barb on a black vent
  like a match - and sets the whole sea floor alight.
- **World** (built - what it is made of): the abyss, 4,000 m down. Black basalt floor, pillow rocks, black-smoker
  chimneys (hydrothermal vents, the only real "fire" on the sea floor), cracks that glow when lit, rising mineral
  smoke, marine snow. Light: total dark at the start - only her dots; then her violet fire spreads crack by crack
  and turns red-gold ("dye this cold world red"). Colour script: black + her violet -> violet + red -> gold festival.
- **Why only Raymi** (name / body / lore): her name is a lamp in the dark; her glowing dots are the first spark; her
  stingray barb is the match ("gold venom on my teeth" = venom IS her barb); rays bury in sand = the "long darkness"
  she rises out of; Salmon = the panicking fire fighter with a bucket of water - underwater ("call the fire fighter /
  begging for water" becomes the gag only she has).
- **Story of actions, muted:** In total black two violet eyes open - she rises out of the sand where she slept "a long
  darkness" and strikes her barb on a vent: a flame, underwater. She lights crack after crack, the fire runs over the
  sea floor (0 to 100, spreads in one breath); deep-sea creatures wake in the light, Salmon rushes in with a bucket
  and pours water on it - nothing happens (it is all water). On the beat drop every vent erupts like fireworks, bubble
  bursts rise to the surface ("like a festival", eolssu!). In the bridge she rides the column of fire-light up through
  the dark water; at the surface she breaks through at night - and below her the whole sea glows like a burning city.
- **Lyric medium:** the fire itself - words written by the flame running along the cracks (key words burn in, fillers
  as embers), words in rising smoke / bubbles, Korean lines glowing in the vent mouths with romanisation + English
  as embers below. Big words may sweep across her - she is lit BY them.
- **Opening (0-3 s) + thumbnail:** black; on "I'm a fire starter" a single strike - her face lit from below by her
  own violet flame, eyes glowing, the flame between her fingers in the black water. Thumbnail = that frame, huge
  flame, 3 words max: "FIRE. UNDERWATER." The first 15 s then cut back to the dark sleep.
- **5 wows** (by line, measured times after the cloud run): 1. 0 s the strike in the black; 2. first chorus - the
  fire runs over the floor in one camera move; 3. "call the fire fighter" - Salmon's bucket gag; 4. beat drop /
  eolssu - all vents erupt, bubble fireworks; 5. final chorus - she breaks the surface, the sea glows below her.
- **The Short:** the opening hook into chorus 1 - ends when the whole floor is lit and she looks up at the surface:
  "how far does it go?" -> MV. CTA: "what would YOU set on fire?"
- **Risk:** fire + dark + her = the devil framing she must never get (no horns from her tako ears, no hellfire red
  alone - her violet leads). Needs a NEW set (abyss vents) + a reusable fire component.
- **Key frames:** boards/A1.jpg (the strike in the black), A2 (the fire spreads over the floor), A3 (the vents erupt)

## Idea B - Breaking News: Fire Warning (the weather studio)
- **The one sentence:** "Weather with Raymi" goes live with a fire warning for the ocean - and the forecaster is the
  one starting it.
- **World** (built): her TV weather studio (built, never seen on the channel - only in KING's idea boards): a big
  weather map wall, ticker, news desk, two studio cameras with tally lights, softboxes, ON AIR. The map turns from
  sunny to FIRE ALERT; flames spread across the map's islands and out of the screen into the studio; the sprinklers
  rain; the studio floods with her sea at the end. Colour script: clean broadcast blue -> alert orange-red -> her
  violet when she takes over.
- **Why only Raymi:** she "predicts rain" and is wrong more often than right - this time she predicts fire and makes
  it come true; confident, competitive, playful; Salmon is the panicking floor manager; Sumi works the camera.
- **Story of actions, muted:** LIVE: she smiles into camera 1, the map behind her ignites on "I'm a fire starter".
  The ticker screams BREAKING; she moves the fire across the map with her hand like a weather front, island by
  island. Salmon runs in with a fire extinguisher, the sprinklers go off - it rains in the studio, she holds out her
  hand (rain prediction, finally right). Beat drop: the studio cameras swing to her on their own, every screen shows
  her face; eolssu - she takes over the broadcast. End: the sea floods the studio, the fire glows under the water,
  she signs off "back to you" and the ON AIR sign burns violet.
- **Lyric medium:** the broadcast's own type - lower thirds, the ticker, the map's labels and warning banner, the
  teleprompter, captions on the studio monitors (Korean lines as a translated news caption: romanisation + English
  in the lower third). Clean and professional by its very medium.
- **Opening (0-3 s) + thumbnail:** ON AIR tally goes red, she smiles into the lens, the map behind her explodes into
  a FIRE ALERT. Thumbnail: her grin in front of a burning map, banner "FIRE WARNING".
- **5 wows:** 1. 0 s the map ignites behind her; 2. she drags the fire front across the map; 3. sprinklers - rain
  in the studio; 4. beat drop - all cameras / screens become her; 5. the sea floods the studio, fire under water.
- **The Short:** cold open into the first chorus as a live broadcast in 9:16 (a phone filming the TV) - ends with
  "after the break: the ocean" -> MV. CTA: "would you trust my forecast?"
- **Risk:** a TV studio is the least "ocean" of the three; reads as comedy more than heat; the weather set is
  toy-like in wides (critic notes) - must live in close and medium shots, screens and type.
- **Key frames:** boards/B1.jpg (live, the map ignites), B2 (the fire front on the map), B3 (rain in the studio)

## Idea C - The Lantern Festival (축제)
- **The one sentence:** the sea floor's festival has been dark for years because nothing can burn down here - Raymi
  lights the first lantern, and the whole deep follows her into the night sky.
- **World** (built): a sunken festival street on the sea floor: strings of paper lanterns (灯 = lantern, the first
  character of her name), stalls of driftwood, a drum made of a giant clam (samulnori - "eolssu!" is the Korean
  festival cheer), creatures waiting in the dark with unlit lanterns. Colour script: cold blue-black, every lantern
  dead -> warm gold spreading from her -> violet + gold lanterns rising to the surface.
- **Why only Raymi:** her name IS the lamp; she is the only one who glows down here; collecting knick-knacks (the
  stalls); confident, so she starts what nobody dares; Sumi's glowing tail tip catches the first spark.
- **Story of actions, muted:** In the dark, rows of unlit lanterns sway; the creatures wait. She glides in, cups her
  hands, breathes on her glow - a lantern lights. One by one, then all at once, the street catches (0 to 100); the
  creatures dance, the clam drum beats on the drop, eolssu. Bridge: she lets the first lantern go - it rises through
  the black water, then hundreds follow her up ("run across this pitch-black night sky"). End: at the surface, a
  night sky full of lanterns - and one more lights by itself: the comeback is everyone's now.
- **Lyric medium:** the lanterns' own paper - words painted on the lanterns light up as she lights them; key words
  on the big festival banner; Korean lines on the lanterns with romanisation + English as smaller paper tags.
- **Opening (0-3 s) + thumbnail:** black - she blows on her cupped hands, a lantern ignites in her face; cut: a
  thousand lanterns blaze at once. Thumbnail: her lit face between hundreds of glowing lanterns.
- **5 wows:** 1. 0 s the first lantern in her hands; 2. chorus 1 - the street catches in one move; 3. the clam drum
  drop - eolssu; 4. bridge - the lanterns rise; 5. the surface - a sky of lanterns.
- **The Short:** the first lantern into the street catching - ends on her letting the lantern go up: "where does it
  go?" -> MV. CTA: "make a wish - what's on YOUR lantern?"
- **Risk:** the warmest, but the least "FIRE starter" - a sky-lantern release is a known image (Tangled); it is
  more beautiful than dangerous, and the song is dangerous. Shares the abyss floor with idea A (a new set either way).
- **Key frames:** boards/C1.jpg (the first lantern), C2 (the street catches), C3 (the lanterns rise)

## My recommendation (toki decides)
**A - The Lamp in the Dark.** It is the only idea where the song's heat and her ocean collide (fire underwater is the
impossible thing a thumbnail can promise in two words), it starts in black with one strike (the cleanest possible
opening), darkness makes the studio look professional, and its fire component + abyss set are reusable for her lore.
B is the funniest, C the prettiest.
