1. The story as I see it. A shy Raymi in a dark violet undersea cave holds a small purple flame and looks proud, like it is her secret. In the next shot she sweats nervously while kneeling near the blue rocks. A small white fish (Salmon?) appears, a "!" pops above it, and she dashes after it. She pushes at a rock and a glowing crack web spreads from her footprints. She runs and leaps through the dark, and a flash cut shows the fish's tail swatting a bright orb. In the end she kneels in a net of glowing cracks while a vent erupts, then stands with her back to us looking at a row of burning candle-pillars. Honest answer: I can follow "she has fire, a fish interrupts, she chases, something ignites". I cannot say what she wants, what goes wrong, or what the payoff is. The fish is never clearly an obstacle or a goal. The ending reads as scenery, not a result.

3. Reaction icons. There are only two: the sweat drop (reads) and the "!" above the fish (reads, pops on the fish's turn). The flash and explosion beats have none. Missing:
- Her surprise or panic when the pillars ignite: a "!" or "!?".
- A heart or lightbulb when she gets the idea, which would carry the "got me fire" turn.
- A "?" when the fish appears.
- Anger or sulk when the fish ruins something.
- A sparkle or heart in the final shot.
Without these she has no emotional language.

4. The three biggest story problems.
- No want and no obstacle. The fish is never established as a thief or a goal. Fix: show the fish grabbing or stealing her flame, then a "!?" with her chase, a miss, and the payoff of her relighting the pillars.
- Pose shots in a row. Seven or more shots are poses held while a lyric appears. Fix: give each shot one action, such as striking a match on a rock, cupping the flame, or the fish bumping her.
- Wide shots lose her and the hero. The crack web and vent shots bury the flame and her. Fix: put the flame in a close-up in each of those beats and dim the lines so the flame stays the brightest thing.
