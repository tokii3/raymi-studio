TREATMENT REVIEW - FIRESTARTER, 3_TREATMENT.md (job jobs/2026-10-19_firestarter)

1. RETELL (muted). Black screen, two violet eyes. At 1 s a tail-whip throws sparks across her face for six frames. Then black again, and another strike every ~2 s, each showing a little more of the vent field. At 12.84 the flame finally stays in her palm and a crack runs over the floor. The main vent ignites at 27 s and the floor becomes a glowing map. Salmon pours water into water. At the stop (50.0) a cyan current kills every flame, and her barb glows gold and relights it. A red ring wave turns the water at 67 s, and at the drop (111 s) all seven vents erupt in bubble fireworks. She rides a heat column up through empty black water and breaks the surface at 143.8. She throws the flame into the sky and the whole sea answers with fireworks. She floats on her back and her eyes close. The text lets me see this, so it passes that test.

2. SLOP TEST: passes. Only Raymi can do these:
- the barb as the match, and the gold venom strike that answers the intro strikes;
- 灯闇 sung at 41.35;
- Salmon's bucket and the sauna towels;
- dots turning fire-coloured "in my veins";
- the eyes opening at 0 and closing at the end.
That is well over three.

3. AGAINST THE TOP THREE. The idea is as strong as LGW: a name-born premise, a turn (the kill at 50.0) and a payoff (the eyes). What it lacks:
- Place variety. Renai and POP IN 2 change places. Here acts 1-7 (0-115 s) are one vent field.
- A second lore gag. Salmon's gag is excellent but is the only one.
- Wow 4 (drop), wow 5 (finale) and the 47-50 s firework are the same bubble/firework effect.

4. DEAD STRETCHES.
OPENING: BLOCK thin.
- 0.0-1.0 s is black, with only the eyes opening on the song's first hit.
- From 1 s the flame is visible for 6 frames, then black for ~2 s, five times. The same set-up repeats with only the spark growing.
- The promised picture (a flame-lit face, "FIRE. UNDERWATER.") first appears at 12.84. The thumbnail promise is not paid in the first 3 s.
- The strongest picture (the burning floor) is withheld until 27 s.
- This is the Know My Name failure: a build-up in the dark.
- The thumbnail picture is also inconsistent. IDEAS says "lit by her own violet flame", but the treatment's 1.0 s frame is a spark flash.
Other stretches:
- Short: it starts on a spark-lit face. Hook, cliffhanger and teaser all work.
- 56.28-64.29 (8 s) is a slow circle on her face, with only the dot colour changing.
- 115.57-143.8 (28 s, the song at "high") is open black water with only snow. 121.9-137.94 is 16 s of "she swims up". The picture calms right after the loudest drop, and the world (rule b) disappears.
- 144.3-166.43 (22 s) is: she floats with the flame, Salmon pops up, one look down. It is the least-planned span and sits at the start of the "strongest third".
- Outro 188-206 is quiet to match the music and pays off frame 0. It is fine.

5. UNREASONED ELEMENTS.
- She rides Salmon at 174-185. There is no chain, and she is a ray who surfs.
- The candle blow-out at 195.0 is decoration unless tied to "the sea keeps the fire".
- The surface lamp has no cause. A fire 4,000 m down cannot light the surface; the glow needs a visible source.

6. SENSE AND LOVE.
- 41.35-43.79 puts her tiny in an abyss_wide, but the hero spec requires the flame ≥8 % of frame height at 41-50. The treatment contradicts itself, and the KING lesson "Raymi tiny in wides" applies.
- The surface set is "nothing but her" on a sea to the horizon, which risks an empty stage.
- The hero rule "flame is the brightest thing" conflicts with six to seven vents all burning in acts 6-7.
- The devil guard (tako ears vs horns) appears only as a risk note. An under-lit face in wine-red water has no staging rule.

7. NOT APPLICABLE (treatment, not script).

EXTREME OR LESS. The intro should be more extreme, not shorter. Cold-open the finished fire for 2.5 s, then give each failed strike its own gag. Less would help the 115-143 stretch: fill it or cut it back to its essence.
