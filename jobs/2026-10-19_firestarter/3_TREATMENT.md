## The one idea
**"At the bottom of the ocean, where nothing can burn, the girl whose name means 'a lamp in the dark' wakes up from a
long darkness and sets the sea on fire - and when the whole deep is burning, she carries it up to the night sky."**
The song is a comeback told as arson (K/DA's first song in six years; "긴 어둠 끝 불을 밝혀" = at the end of the long dark,
light the fire). Raymi's version: the impossible fire - underwater - lit by the one creature whose name IS a lamp in
the dark (灯闇).
Rules of this world:
(a) **Fire is light.** Nothing burns up and nothing is destroyed; what the fire touches starts to GLOW (cracks, vents,
    creatures' fins, the water itself). "Burn it down" means "light it up". No victims, no smoke damage, no hellfire.
(b) **Light reveals the world.** The abyss is black; we only see what the fire lights (the LGW rule: the lamp shows the
    place). So the world GROWS on screen as her fire spreads - the video's change from start to end is literally how much
    of the sea we can see.
(c) **Water cannot put it out** (the gag the song hands us: "call the fire fighter", "you'll be begging for water"):
    every attempt to drown it is water poured into water. Only one thing can kill it: the cold current (the song's
    "바람 불어도" - even if the wind blows). It does, once (the stop at 50.0), and she relights it from her own body.
(d) **The lyric is written by fire**: cracks that glow into words on the floor, words in the rising bubbles of the
    vents, stamps on the hits, at the end fireworks writing in the sky. Never a subtitle band. Big type may sweep across
    her (toki 2026-10-19) - she is lit BY the words.
Fused with her sea: the only real fire on the sea floor is a hydrothermal vent (black smokers, 400 °C water) - so the
fire has a real home down there; "fireworks" are bubble bursts rising from the vents (the festival of "펑펑 터져 마치
축제 같지"); the "night sky" she runs across is the black water above the abyss with marine snow falling like stars.

## Raymi's part (not only the song)
- **Attitude:** smug, playful, competitive about the impossible (she is going to make fire work underwater because
  everyone says it can't - "competitive about completely inconsequential things"). Light tsundere: when the fire
  catches she pretends it was easy (a bored little shrug at 14.81), but her tail wags. "Confidence is cute": the
  comeback is hers, she does not ask permission.
- **Her body does the plot:** her stingray barb is the match - and its VENOM is gold ("gold venom on my teeth" is the
  one line in the song that is literally about her body); her glowing dots are the first spark and, in the pre-chorus,
  turn fire-coloured ("chaos running wild in my veins"); rays bury themselves in sand = the long darkness she wakes up
  out of at 0.0; rays ride currents = she surfs the heat columns in chorus 2 and rides one up in the bridge.
- **Her lore:** Salmon (best friend) is the fire fighter who tries three times (a bucket, an extinguisher, then gives up
  and warms its fins at the vent; in the finale it toasts her with the bucket); her **rain predictions** (wrong more
  often than right) are the second gag: at the surface Salmon looks at her for a forecast, she confidently predicts
  rain - and for once she is RIGHT - and the rain cannot put it out either (every drop lights a ring on the glowing
  sea); she collects knick-knacks (the bucket was one of hers). Her name 灯闇 is said by the song itself at 41.35.
- **What she wants:** to light up the dark she slept in - and to be SEEN doing it (a comeback). She gets it twice: the
  sea floor (act 3) and, after the cold current kills it, the whole sea up to the sky (act 9).
- **Not forced into every frame** (toki 2026-10-19): the fire running over the floor, the vents erupting, Salmon's
  bucket, the sauna, the glowing sea from above are shots without her or with her tiny - each is something SHE caused,
  and her return in the next shot lands harder.

  0-2, close and intimate; (2) **the main vent** - the one chimney she strikes, lava seams, mouth at eye height; acts 2-5;
  (3) **the chimney forest** - six tall black smokers close together, the heat shimmer between them she surfs; act 6;
  (4) **the giant at the back** - the 7th, tallest vent on a rise, the stage of the rap and the drop; act 7. Each zone is
  framed so the others are not seen (a new place per act), and the floor's crack map ties them together from above.
  Mineral crust around the chimneys' feet; marine snow falling. Lived-in: a few of her knick-knacks half-buried near her spot (the
  bucket is one - Salmon takes it from there), a small ring of stones she arranged. Physical sense: chimneys stand on
  the floor, nothing floats except what swims; the fire burns at vent mouths and along cracks (where real heat would be).
- **The cliff and the open water above:** a sea-mount wall rising beside the vent field (basalt, ledges, a crack network
  the fire climbs); beyond it open water - the glowing floor below, the marine snow, the light from above getting bluer
  as she rises.
- **The surface at night (new):** a calm sea to the horizon, a low moon, stars. Light sources that make sense: the
  heat column that carried her breaks the surface with her (a violet pool around her), the fire-finned fish that
  followed her up (gold dots on the water), later the rain rings spreading the glow. Never an empty stage: her, the
  fish crowd, Salmon with the bucket, the moon and, at the end, the fireworks.

## Animation principles in this video
- **Anticipation** before every strike (the tail coils back 4-6 frames) and before the drop (107.69-111.28: the floor
  creaks, she wobbles, everything compresses).
- **Squash and stretch** on every strike impact, on the landing at 83.74, on the takeoff at 115.57; **smear** on the
  tail whip, the flame toss (16.54), the surf dash; **overshoot** on every ignition (Fires `grow`: whoosh past full
  size, settle); **follow-through** of hair and tail; **holds** after each big move (a still beat makes the next hit).
- **Staging:** one read per moment - the flame, her face, or the word, never all three fighting; words never over a
  flame (BACKLOG).
- **Picture change on most loud beats:** a strike, an ignition, an eruption or a cut on the hits (song_map hits).
