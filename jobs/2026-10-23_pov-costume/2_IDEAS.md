## The idea
- **The one sentence:** She reveals her Halloween costume - she is going as her best friend, a salmon - and then the salmon walks in dressed as her.
- **World:** her deep sea floor on Halloween night (set `deep`, v2), carved jack-o-lanterns glowing on the rocks.
- **Why only Raymi:** (1) her best friend is a salmon (only her lore); (2) her pride in her look - she rates her costume before you do; (3) the salmon's costume IS her design (tako ears + purple).
- **Story of actions, muted:** She jumps into frame in a salmon costume and asks you to rate it -> a catwalk turn: she is going as her best friend -> proud of the fins -> a knock: the salmon floats in, wearing tako ears and a purple bow -> a stare-down: he copied her -> she shrugs and poses with him: couples costume -> both ask you what you are going as.
- **Main element:** the salmon costume on her (hood + fins) and the salmon in the Raymi costume.
- **Hook (spoken + stamp, <= 8 words):** "rate my halloween costume."
- **CTA (spoken + stamp, >= 2.5 s before the end):** "who wore it better?" (v2; was "what are YOU going as?")
- **Risk:** the camera-as-viewer moves must read as YOUR body (recoil, step, being grabbed), not as a cinematic
  move; her clips must keep her alive between lines (never a held pose for more than a beat).
