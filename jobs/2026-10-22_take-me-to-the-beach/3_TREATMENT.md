## The one idea
**"Every day is the same day: the alarm, the train, the desk, the hands that pull her strings - and every day another
of her glowing dots goes out. Dreaming of the beach does not get her out; the strings pull her back every time. On
the last T-A-K-E she breaks the loop for real, hands her boss his snow globe - 'you can have the mountains' - and
takes the beach."**
The one question the video asks: **can she get out of the loop?** (a dream cannot do it - she has to break it).
Symbol hierarchy (FIX 8 - nothing else gets added):
- **the clock** (how far the loop has gone): the calendar page + her dots as the battery;
- **the conflict** (what holds her): the strings + the snow globe;
- **the hero** (what she wants): the beach postcard.
Rules of this world:
(a) **The loop.** Every day starts with the same twin-bell alarm clock on the riff and the same torn calendar page.
    The same three camera set-ups repeat (alarm macro, train doors, desk from above), so the viewer reads "again"
    without a word. Days shown: DAY 1 (verse 1), DAY 2 (after chorus 1), DAY 3-30 in a page blizzard (Ado), DAY 365
    (the bridge) - the numbers jump because the days blur.
(b) **The strings.** Everybody who wants something from her is a white-gloved HAND from outside the frame with a string
    to her wrist: the alarm, the phone, the boss. When a string pulls, her arm moves FIRST and her body follows late
    (a marionette). One more string every day. Strings even reach into her dream and yank her back (chorus 1). She
    cuts the first one herself (Ado), the last ones at T-A-K-E.
(c) **The battery is her body.** 灯闇 - a lamp in the dark: she glows. Five dots on her (raymi5's starry dress lights)
    are her battery, and a flat 2D battery sign beside her head shows the SAME five dots (her manga reaction-icon
    language, so it reads at phone size). Each loop one dot goes dark. At the bridge one is left - and when the office
    lights go out, that last dot is the only light in the room. The beach (and dancing) relights them.
(d) **Snow globe vs postcard.** On her desk stand the two halves of the song: the boss's small SNOW GLOBE ("FORECAST:
    SNOW" on its base - grey mountains, falling snow: "you can have the mountains, you take the snow, it's way too
    cold") and her own BEACH POSTCARD pinned to the monitor. The globe stays one object, always on the desk. In the
    bridge the loop shrinks her INTO it (she is the figure in the globe, the boss's hand shakes it); at T-A-K-E she
    cracks out and grows back; at "you can have the mountains" she sets the cracked, empty globe in the boss's hands.
(e) **Two looks = two states** (her references, Usseewa): reality is a flat grey two-tone poster with halftone dots
    (the 2D layer), the beach is full colour, deep 3D, sun. Ado's rebellion is the blue -> red state. In chorus 2 the
    colour spreads out of her into the grey office.
Fused with her sea - why it is HERS, not any office worker's:
- Her town is the deep sea: the office floor is far down, cold and grey, MARINE SNOW drifts past the windows all day
  (the real snow of the deep ocean - the same snow that falls in the boss's globe). The beach is the shallow, sunny
  edge of the same sea, where rays lie in warm sand. Going free = going up, from cold to warm, from deep to shallow.
- Her body does the plot: the tail slaps the alarm, the barb cuts the strings, the tail writes on the glass (T-A-K-E),
  she slides out of the cracked globe flat like a ray, she buries herself in sand to switch off.
- Her lore: her rain predictions (she sticks a "SUN" note on the boss's "FORECAST: SNOW" globe - day 1 - and at the
  end she is right), Salmon (her best friend, in the postcard and at the beach), the onigiri she eats anyway.

- **Attitude:** sleepy baseline (canon), a polite smile on cue - the smile is a string pulling her cheek. Under it light
  tsundere annoyance (a tiny eye-roll each loop), never truly sad (canon). Tired -> fed up -> free. "Competitive about
  inconsequential things": she races the closing train doors every morning and loses - in chorus 2 she wins.
- **Her own gags:** the "SUN" sticky note on the snow globe (her rain predictions; wrong as always - except this time);
  "え？" (63.29) as her deadpan canon face; Salmon offended at "I don't have no friends".
- **What she wants:** one day that is hers. Dreaming gives her eight seconds of it; breaking the loop gives her the rest.
- **Not forced into every frame:** the alarm clock, the calendar blizzard, the hands, the globe, the empty desk, the
  last silent clock in the sand carry shots of their own.

## Animation principles in this video
- **Repetition with variation** (the loop): the same action and set-up each day, faster each time - shorter holds,
  less anticipation (autopilot), a smaller smile.
- **Strings = forced motion**: the pulled limb moves FIRST, the body follows late (drag), no anticipation - she does not
  want it. Her own moves (from Ado on) have full anticipation and overshoot - the difference is visible.
- **Squash and stretch / smears** on the wake-up snap, the tail slap, the barb cut, the four T-A-K-E blows, the
  door-race slide.
- **Holds**: the smile freeze (7.8), "え？" (63.29, the only locked camera), the finger on the boss's lips (75.05).
- **On twos** (VRM step 2) in the routine (mechanical), 30 fps in the dream, the dance and the finale (alive).
- **Singing**: phonetic lip sync v2 throughout - quiet in the routine (gain 0.6), full in the dream, the dance and
  the finale.
- Motions needed (Mixamo / own poses): sleep, wake up with a start, walk, squeezed, sit + type, slump, phone to ear,
  stand on a chair, dance (3 takes: desk, slide, aisle), dodge-spin, door slide, tail swing, throw / set down, pull,
  fall backwards, float, bury in sand, hug.

## Ending check
The last third (99-167 s) has the freshest place (inside the snow globe), the tightest cutting (every bridge line
flips), the darkest moment (lights out, one dot left), the biggest wow (T-A-K-E: the cracks, the break-out, the globe

STAYS. Every opening symbol pays off.
