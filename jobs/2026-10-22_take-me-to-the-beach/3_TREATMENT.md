## The one idea
**"Every day is the same day: the alarm, the train, the desk, the hands that pull her strings - and every day her
battery is a little emptier. She keeps a beach postcard on her monitor. On the last T-A-K-E she breaks the loop, hands
her boss the snow globe - 'you can have the mountains' - and takes the beach."**
Rules of this world:
(a) **The loop.** Each day starts with the same alarm clock (the riff IS the alarm) and the same torn calendar page
    (DAY 1, DAY 2, DAY 47, DAY 365 ...). Each loop is shorter and faster - the song's own "and the days go on, and on,
    and on". The same camera angles repeat, so the viewer reads "again" without a word (POP IN 2: a day as acts).
(b) **The strings.** Everybody who wants something from her - the boss, the phone, the salesman, the clock - is a
    HAND from outside the frame with a string to her wrist. When a string pulls, she moves (stamps, smiles, answers).
    More strings each day. She cuts the first one herself (Ado). People-pleasing made literal.
(c) **The battery.** A battery icon floats beside her like a manga sign (2D, her reaction-icon language): 100 % at the
    first alarm, one bar less every loop, red at the bridge. At the beach it fills. Everybody knows "social battery".
(d) **Snow globe vs postcard.** On her desk stand the two halves of the song: the boss's SNOW GLOBE (grey mountains,
    falling snow - "you can have the mountains, you take the snow, it's way too cold") and her own BEACH POSTCARD
    pinned to the monitor. The dream always starts by the camera falling INTO the postcard; the payoff is the globe
    going back to the boss.
(e) **Two looks = two states** (her references, Usseewa): reality is a flat grey two-tone poster with halftone dots
    (the 2D layer), the beach is full colour and deep 3D with sun. Ado's rebellion is the blue -> red state.
Fused with her sea: her town is under the sea (fish commuters in the train, kelp outside the office window, light
from far above); the beach is the shallow, sunny edge of the sea where rays bury in warm sand - not a holiday, the
place a stingray belongs. The postcard shows that shallow water from above.

## Raymi's part (not only the song)
- **Attitude:** relaxed / sleepy baseline (canon), polite smile on cue in the loop - the smile is a string pulling her
  mouth up. Under it: light tsundere annoyance (a tiny eye-roll every loop), "competitive about inconsequential
  things" (she races the closing train door every morning and wins once, chorus 2). Never sad for real (canon: tsundere
  stays playful) - she is tired, then fed up, then free. "Confidence is cute": she dances because she wants to.
- **Her body does the plot:** her stingray barb cuts the first string (71.86, "Take your hands off!"); her tail slaps
  the alarm off; she buries herself in warm sand at the beach (rays do that = her real way to switch off, "turnin' off
  my phone"); flat ray shape: she slips flat out of the cracked globe.
- **Her lore:** Salmon, her best friend, sits at the beach in every dream (holding a sign "WISH YOU WERE HERE" in the
  postcard); in the outro "I don't have no friends, ask anyone" Salmon looks at her offended - she grins and pulls it
  into the frame: "'til the end, my favourite one". Her rain predictions: the boss's snow globe is labelled "FORECAST:
  SNOW" - she predicts SUN on a sticky note (wrong as always? not this time). Onigiri at her desk in Ado's verse
  ("また構わずやる" - she eats where she must not).
- **What she wants:** one day that is hers. She gets it by breaking the loop, not by escaping it in a dream.
- **Not forced into every frame:** the alarm clock, the torn calendar, the hands, the globe, the empty desk at the end
  carry shots on their own.

## Animation principles in this video
- **Repetition with variation** (the loop): the same action, same camera, each day faster - shorter holds, less
  anticipation (she is on autopilot). The variation shows the state: each day a smaller smile.
- **Strings = forced motion**: when a string pulls, her limb moves FIRST and her body follows late (drag), like a
  marionette (no anticipation - she does not want it). Her own moves (Ado onward) have full anticipation + overshoot.
- **Squash and stretch / smears** on the wake-up snap, the tail slap, the tail cut, the T-A-K-E blows, the throw.
- **Holds**: the blank smile freeze (7.8), "え？" (63.29), the moment before she stands up (73.7).
- **On twos** (VRM step 2) in the routine (mechanical), full 30 fps in the dream and the dance (fluid = alive).
- **Natural singing**: phonetic lip sync v2 throughout, quieter mouth in the routine (gain 0.6), full in the dream and
  the finale.
- Motions needed (Mixamo / own poses): wake up with a start, brush teeth, walk, squeezed in a crowd, sit + type,
  slump, phone to ear, dance (2-3 takes), throw / toss, grab and pull, fall backwards, lie in sand, swing the tail.
