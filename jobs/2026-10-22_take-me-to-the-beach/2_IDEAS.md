## Idea A - On Air (the ocean channel's weather report)
- **The one sentence:** every day Raymi has to read other people's forecasts on live TV - "snow in the mountains" -
  until she grabs the map and forecasts the beach.
- **World** (built): her TV weather studio (built set, never in an uploaded video): map wall, ticker, news desk, two
  studio cameras with tally lights, softboxes, ON AIR, teleprompter. The routine = the broadcast loop: make-up, cue
  cards, the producer's notes, the phone ringing (the salesman), the teleprompter scrolling faster every day. The
  beach = the sunny map she keeps glancing at - in the dream the map wall opens and becomes the beach.
- **Why only Raymi:** her canon rain predictions (she really can forecast) - here she must read wrong forecasts
  written by others. The song is literally a weather map: mountains, snow, "way too cold", "that sun in my face".
  A stingray belongs to the shallow sunny water: the beach is her home, not a holiday.
- **Story of actions, muted:** she sits in make-up while three people talk at her; the ON AIR light snaps on (the can
  sound) and she smiles on cue, points at snow on the map; the cue cards pile up, the teleprompter races, her smile
  cracks; in the chorus the map melts into a beach and she floats in it; in Ado's verse she reads her OWN forecast
  (sun everywhere), the producer waves his arms; in chorus 2 she dances live on air while the crew freezes and the
  studio cameras swing to follow her; in the bridge on-air / beach cut against each other; at "T-A-K-E" she tears the
  map down, throws the stack of snow forecasts back at the producer ("you can have the mountains") and pulls the sun
  icon off the wall ("I'll take the beach") - the studio fills with sunlight.
- **Main element:** the weather map (snow mountains vs sun) - it carries the whole conflict in one picture.
- **Lyric medium:** the broadcast itself: teleprompter text, ticker band, map labels ("MOUNTAINS", "SNOW", "BEACH"),
  cue cards she holds, lower thirds - 2D TV graphics are 2D by nature (the 2D feel comes free). Ado's lines as an
  ALERT ticker in red, romaji + English as subtitles of the broadcast.
- **2D look:** routine = grey two-tone poster (camera grey), the beach = full colour; Ado's verse = Usseewa blue/red
  states; dance = speed lines; the map is a flat 2D graphic that can come alive.
- **Camera as transition:** the studio camera's lens is the cut (zoom INTO the studio camera's viewfinder -> the
  broadcast picture); crane down from the lighting grid; whip along the ticker into the next day.
- **5 wows:** 1. cold open flashes on the riff (dance on air / tearing the map / the sun) 0-9 s; 2. ON AIR snap on the
  can (17.65); 3. the map melts into the beach (chorus 1, 33.4); 4. she dances live on air, the crew frozen (chorus 2,
  78.0); 5. T-A-K-E: map torn down, files thrown back, she pulls the sun off the wall (118.7-137.3).
- **The Short:** S1 chorus 1 (33.4-52.9): "today's forecast: I'm leaving" - ends with the beach map and a cut back to
  the studio -> MV. CTA: "what's YOUR forecast?"
- **Risk:** a TV studio can feel like KING's / FIRESTARTER's idea boards (the set was drafted twice) - it must feel
  like a JOB, not a show. Needs new: the beach set, a producer + crew (figures that read at phone size - the crowd look
  pass from KING), cue cards / teleprompter text, a map that changes live.
- **Key frames:** boards/A1.jpg (the grind on air, grey), A2 (dancing live on air, chorus 2), A3 (she reaches into the
  sunny map - "I'll take the beach")

## Idea B - Office at 4000 m (the abyss is her job)
- **The one sentence:** Raymi works the night shift at the bottom of the ocean, where it is cold and dark forever -
  and the beach is the warm light far above that she can only see in dreams.
- **World** (built): the abyss from FIRESTARTER (basalt, black chimneys, marine snow) turned into an office floor: a
  cubicle ring of chimneys, a desk lamp (灯闇: she is the only light), files raining down like marine snow, the boss as
  a deep-sea anglerfish whose lamp is always on her. The beach = the surface: warm light through the water, sand, sun.
  Going up = going free (the whole video is a vertical journey: deep -> surface).
- **Why only Raymi:** a stingray at the bottom is in her nature; her glowing dots fade in the grind and come back in
  the sun; she buries herself in the sand to switch off ("turnin' off my phone"); flat body slips under the door.
- **Story of actions, muted:** the files fall, she stamps them in rhythm; the anglerfish's lamp snaps on her (can
  sound); her dots dim; in the "oh-oh-oh" the abyss pulls away (dolly zoom); chorus 1 she floats up a light beam to
  the surface and lies in the sun; Ado's verse: back down, she ignores the boss, stamps "NO" on every file; chorus 2
  she dances on the office floor in front of every deep-sea coworker; bridge: deep / surface flashes faster and
  faster as the chimneys close in; T-A-K-E: she shoves the file mountain back onto the anglerfish ("you can have the
  mountains") and grabs the sunbeam ("I'll take the beach") and rides it up.
- **Main element:** the light from above (the beam) - the only warm colour in the cold world.
- **Lyric medium:** stamps on files, words in the falling marine snow / paper, the beam writes the beach lines in
  warm light; Ado's lines as red stamps.
- **2D look:** the abyss in a grey-violet two-tone with halftone (a manga night page), the surface in full warm colour.
- **Camera as transition:** the camera rides the falling files down (match cut each day), rises with her up the beam
  (one continuous vertical move = the chorus), top-down into the cubicle ring.
- **5 wows:** 1. cold open flashes; 2. the anglerfish lamp on the can sound; 3. first rise up the beam (chorus 1);
  4. dancing on the office floor (chorus 2); 5. files shoved onto the anglerfish + she rides the beam to the surface.
- **The Short:** S3 Ado's verse (54.4-73.7): the stamp rebellion, ends on "Take your hands off!" -> MV. CTA: "what
  would YOU stamp NO on?"
- **Risk:** the abyss look repeats FIRESTARTER (the same set two packages in a row); dark videos lose phone viewers in
  the first seconds; the anglerfish boss is a new creature build. Needs new: office props, files, anglerfish, a beach.
- **Key frames:** boards/B1.jpg (top-down, tiny in the chimney cubicle, grey), B2 (the dissociation, long lens),
  B3 (she breaks into warm light - a sunset stand-in until the beach is built)

## Idea C - Paper Days (reality is a flat page, only the dream has depth)
- **The one sentence:** Raymi's life is a printed two-colour planner page that gets more crowded every day - only
  the beach is real, round and in colour, and at "T-A-K-E" she tears out of the page.
- **World** (built + the 2D layer as the MEDIUM): the routine is shot top-down and flat like a manga / planner page:
  two colours, halftone, to-do panels (built sets shot as panels: the weather studio, the desk, the street), the
  panels get smaller and more numerous as the days repeat ("and the days go on, and on"). The beach is the only place
  shot in full 3D with depth, colour and camera travel. Medium change = emotional change (Usseewa's colour states).
- **Why only Raymi:** a flat ray in a flat world - she literally fits the page, and her escape is becoming 3D (she
  is a flat polygon in canon: "rays are flat polygons", LGW); her ink friend Sumi prints the page.
- **Story of actions, muted:** she wakes in a single blue panel, the panels multiply around her (alarm, train, desk,
  phone); the can sound tears a new panel open; in the "oh-oh-oh" the panel borders close in; chorus 1 the panel
  cracks and the camera falls through into the 3D beach; Ado's verse in red panels, she scribbles over her to-do
  list; chorus 2 she dances across the panel borders while the drawn people in the other panels stare; bridge: flat
  page / 3D beach alternate on every line, the grid shrinks to a cell; T-A-K-E: she rips the page, throws the to-do
  sheets back ("you can have the mountains") and pulls the 3D beach in ("I'll take the beach").
- **Main element:** the panel grid (how big her space is) - from many tiny cells to one open world.
- **Lyric medium:** handwritten in the planner: to-do items, crossed out; Ado's lines as red ink scribbles; the beach
  lines in sunlight on the sand.
- **2D look:** the purest version of toki's references - two-colour flat pages with halftone; the 3D beach as the
  contrast.
- **Camera as transition:** zoom through a panel border into the next panel; the camera falls through the page into
  depth; spin cut when the page tears.
- **5 wows:** 1. cold open as panels flashing; 2. the can tears the panel; 3. falling through the page into 3D
  (chorus 1); 4. dancing across the panel borders (chorus 2); 5. ripping the page at T-A-K-E.
- **The Short:** S1 chorus 1: from a tiny panel to the 3D beach in one fall - "how many panels is your day?"
- **Risk:** panels need a multi-view render pass (several views at once - not built yet, BACKLOG); LGW's "Raymi in the
  box too often" - the box must shrink with meaning and be broken, not used as a frame every shot.
- **Key frames:** boards/C1.jpg (a printed blue panel from above), C2 (Ado's verse, red rage, worm's eye),
  C3 (the only full-colour 3D place)
