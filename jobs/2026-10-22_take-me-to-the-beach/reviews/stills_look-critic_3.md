1. DESCRIPTION
- Dominant colours: (a) near-black navy, desaturated, very low value (office, train, ending); (b) mid grey-blue to cool grey, low saturation, mid value (the grey/marionette office frames); (c) pure white and pale lavender, high value (TAKE, snow, blobs, platform). Accents: her electric indigo hair, a warm beige/tan bedroom (070, 075), pastel sky-cyan with peach in the dream (078).
- Light: no single source. 001 and 056 have flat cold ceiling light while she gets indigo front rim light. 074 has warm window light from the right plus white glow from the left. 078 glows from below. Her sprite is lit the same way everywhere, independent of the set.
- Eye path: face/hair first. In 001 then the bright ceiling panels and the chart. In the white frames (010, 028, 053) the eye goes to the white mass and lyric text and she gets lost.

2. COLOUR
- Saturation: her hair is the only strong saturation and is rightly the peak in 001, 056, 068, 074. But the indigo clips to flat blue and loses strand detail (068, 070). Other things compete: the red of 045, and the white TAKE / snow in 053 and 028.
- Skin: pale cool grey-blue in 001, 010, 063, but leaning too saturated violet-blue on the legs and arms in 001, near-black violet in 063, and clipped white at arm/cheek in 070 and 074. So it does not stay pale grey-blue under every light.
- Greys: the office grey frames (015, 030, 040, 052) are dead mid-grey, halftone and posterised, not coloured shadows. The world reads grey rather than matte-stylised.
- Shadows are mostly just darkened. Only the bedroom (070, 075) has warm coloured shadow.

3. LIGHT
- Her sprite carries its own rim light, not the set's. In 001 the light on her is blue from the front while the set is lit from above in white. In 056 her feet fade to a pale gradient with no cast shadow. She reads as pasted on.
- Contact: 001 and 056 have no readable contact shadow. 074's seat and desk contact is OK. The table in 070 has a pink-white glare that kills form. 078 has a glow ring and a small shadow.
- 010, 028, 053, 075 clip to pure white with no gradation, losing material and depth.

4. COMPOSITION + DEPTH
- Strong: 001 (low angle, speed-line diagonals, depth from foreground monitor through her to the desk rows), 056, 074, 078 (best figure-ground).
- Weak: 015 and 030 (she is about 8% of the frame in grey halftone); 045 (red monochrome, figure and set merge); 082 (tiny on a plinth in a dark void, text bigger than she is); 053 (TAKE huge, character lost); 063 (very tight face on near-black, no depth cue).
- Crops: 010 crops her face at the top edge; 068 crops her body at the left edge.
- Grayscale test: 001 passes. 015, 030, 040, 052 have almost no value separation between figure and set.

5. ASSET QUALITY + ARTEFACTS
- 074 hands: faceted low-poly white mitts, posterised stripes. Worst asset on the board.
- Blobs 028, 075: blown-out soft white lumps, no edge or texture.
- 001 chart: crisp and legible, good.
- Lyrics: puffy cloud letters with uneven baseline (023, 026, 070); partly garbled or split words in 028/030 ("mil lion people stayin"); 040 has dense small JP+EN text unreadable at phone size.
- Hair: hard black outline and blue banding; dark fringe between hair and background in 063.
- Mismatch: her painted sprite is far more detailed than the flat low-poly set (056, 001).

6. DETAIL + LOVE, PER PLACE
Office (001, 056, 040, 052, 023, 026, 068): 001 is the best-dressed shot (corkboard with notes, TEAMWORK poster, chart, plant, sticky notes, eyed creatures). 056/040/052 are rows of identical white desks and monitors, blank windows, no clutter: an empty stage.
Fixes: add violet shadow and warm lamp accents (1-2 orange points) against cool fluorescent spill; vary the desk rows (chair angles, mugs, paper stacks, plants, lamps, jackets, cables, at least 12 distinct props per shot); carpet texture and floor sheen; windows with a real city/sky and light shafts; AO contact shadows under desks and a cast shadow under her; depth fog so she separates in 056.

Bedroom (075, 070, 074): warm tan walls, wood window frames, filing cabinets, lamp are a good start and 074 is the best shot, but the floor is flat dark grey, walls are bare, cabinets are flat beige boxes.
Fixes: replace the pink-white glare on the table (070, 075) with a gradient; add bed and rumpled blanket texture, posters, string lights; rug, clock, books, plant; warm key plus cool fill with violet shadows; rebuild the 074 hands as soft rounded cloth mitts or use her painted hands.

Train / platform (row 4 col 1 and 067 region): mostly flat near-white panels, washed out, a "MIND THE GAP" sign cut off at the bottom-right edge, two small yellow handles, Raymi small in the doorway.
Fixes: seat rows, window frames with light streaks, pole and handles, ads, route map, floor tiles; drop wall value to a mid tone with warm interior light; scale her up and key her with the door light plus a floor shadow; move the sign inside the frame or crop it deliberately.

Snow-globe interior (053, 028, 023, 026, 067): white-on-white. TAKE in 053 is a strong graphic but figure and paper stacks vanish in the glare.
Fixes: tint snow pale teal/lavender over a darker deep-blue back wall; lower bloom exposure and make the paper stacks readable; keep her bigger in a darker pocket with cool rim; replace blown blobs with shaded rounded forms (gradient, mid-tone, core shadow).

Dream sea (078, 082): 078 is the strongest beach frame (pastel sky, glow plinth, headphone, controller, fish toy) but props are flat glossy primitives floating in blank cyan: no horizon, no sea, no sand. 082 is a dark void with a tiny plinth and cluttered lyrics.
Fixes: horizon, water gradient with gentle waves, sun disc, sand/shell ground; make 082's plinth and Raymi at least 3x larger and move lyrics out of the beam; coloured contact shadow and reflected glow on water; consistent matte toon shading on props, all grounded.

7. ONE ARTWORK?
No. Three looks in one video: her detailed painted sprite, the stylised matte rooms, and the posterised grey/halftone frames (015, 030, 040, 052) with low-poly white blobs. The halftone frames look like a different pipeline.
