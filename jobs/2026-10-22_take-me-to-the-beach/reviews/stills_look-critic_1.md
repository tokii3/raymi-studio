This breaks the studio rule. It also kills the form of her arms and legs, and in 074 the pile and her sleeve merge into one white mass.
- The saturation peak is correct in the office (her violet against grey). In the dream it is lost: 078 has sky-blue walls, a yellow fish, a purple controller and pink headphones all at similar saturation, and none of it is calmer than the rest. That is "several compete".

- The train is white-grey and milky. The window holes are pure black-navy, so it looks like cardboard.
- Red 045 is strong as a graphic. It has no mid-tone and the desk is a dead red slab.

6. DETAIL AND LOVE
- The office is an empty stage that happens to have 40 identical desks. There are no personal items, no plants, no cups, no paper on the desks, no coats on the chairs, no sticky notes, no cables, no wear, and no clock. The place has no story except the one lyric about "a million people".
- The strongest storytelling props are the "FORECAST: SNOW" device (068, nicely built but tiny) and the fish photo on the desk (028 and 074). These two are the quality the rest should match.
- The train is a prop kit: white boxes, rings and signs. There is no seat texture, no poster, no map line, no floor pattern and no handrail shine.
- The dream is the most lively set and the most chaotic. There is no clear idea of what the objects are, and they float with no ground logic.
- 082 is mostly empty dark navy with a few specks. The ending frame has no beach, so the payoff ("take me to the beach") does not exist as a place.

7. ONE ARTWORK?
No. At least four languages:
(a) a toon 3D girl,
(b) a matte grey boxy office,
(c) posterised halftone comic frames,
(d) a soft pastel low-poly dream with bloom.
The jump from (b) to (d) is the idea, but the transition is a hard cut in value and style. The 3D model is lit more strongly than any set around her.

FIX 1
observation: Raymi's skin and skirt clip to pure white in 001, 068, 082 (office), 023 and 074 (dream). In 074 her sleeve disappears into the white pile.
effect: She looks over-exposed, the form of limbs is lost, and she stops being the pale grey-blue character. The pile is a blank white shape.
cause: Key light and bloom too strong and no tone-mapping headroom (02, 01). Studio rule: her skin stays pale grey-blue under any light.
change: Cap her skin at about value 85 percent (about #D5DDF0 at the peak) with a cool blue-grey shadow tone of about 55 percent. Cut the key intensity by about 40 percent and the bloom threshold up. Give the pile its own colour (peach or pink) and the same cap, with rim colour from the set.
priority: high
level: structural:studio
retest: Pull values on 001, 023, 074. Her skin must show at least three tones and no pixel region above 245 outside the eyes and rim highlights.
