## 6. Detail and love
- Office: it is the richest place. It has a cork board with notes, a TEAMWORK poster, a wall clock, a vent, plants, mugs, pen pots, filing cabinets, snow windows and desk lamps. It reads as an office at phone size. The big chart screen is the best prop: readable, funny, and it frames her.
  - Still cheap: identical streaky desk fronts, an empty floor, bare desk tops, and the same plant repeated everywhere.
- Train: an empty stage. Only the rings and a floor line say "commute".
- Bedroom: nearly bare. A desk, a grey wall and a floor, with no bed, clothes, posters or personal objects. The warm window wall in 074 is the only sign of life.
- Dream sea: a sticker sheet of toy props floating on a gradient. The reef coral (thin stems with white tips) is a good consistent motif. The seabed beyond the pedestal is empty.

## 7. One artwork?
- The office holds together as one hand. The matte toon look and coloured shadows match her sprite.
- The sea props and the train look like a lower-effort hand (plain geometry, flat colour). The posterised frames are a deliberate third look.
- Her over-bright light fights the dim set in every office shot, so she reads as composited.
