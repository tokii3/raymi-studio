## 2. What the song says
Story in 5 sentences: Everybody on this "people-pleasing planet" tells the singer how to live - salesmen, preachers,
people with plans for her. She owes them nothing ("not a penny") and wants them to "can it". Instead of fighting she
picks a side of the world: they can have the mountains, the snow, the cold - she takes the beach, the sun in her face.
Ado's verse turns the same feeling into anger - stop listening, the puffed-up guy, "who am I supposed to become? Take
your hands off!". It ends alone but free ("better off alone", phone off, "no friends - got me 'til the end, my
favourite one": she is her own favourite person).

## 4. Who loves this song and why
Two audiences in one song: Imagine Dragons fans (Western mainstream, gaming / sports edits) and Ado fans (J-pop /
anime / VTuber audience - Raymi's people). Comments on the official video celebrate the collab ("best collab ever"),
Ado's verse switch, the "T-A-K-E" spell-out. Fans expect: Ado's verse to feel like a different, darker energy; the
T-A-K-E build to explode; the mountains vs beach split as a picture. Relatable core: burnout, everyone telling you
how to live, wanting to escape - very shareable as a Short.

## 6. Where Raymi meets this song
1. She is a stingray: the BEACH is literally her edge of the world - the shallow, sunny water where rays bury in warm
   sand. Mountains and snow are the opposite of everything she is.
2. 灯闇 (lamp in the dark): her routine is the dark, the beach is light - her dots glow less and less in the grind,
   full in the sun.
3. Her canon rain predictions -> she could be the WEATHER presenter forced to read other people's forecasts ("you can
   have the mountains, you take the snow" is literally a weather map).
4. Burying in sand = her real way of switching off ("turnin' off my phone").
5. Salmon (best friend) as the one who is there "'til the end, my favourite one" - or the twist: her favourite one
   is herself (the lyric says no friends).
6. Sumi's ink = the dark mood of the dissociation in the "oh-oh-oh".
7. Her "devil" complaint (canon: people call her a devil) = the people-pleasing planet judging her (神か仏か: "a god or
   a Buddha?" - people judge her as something she is not).
8. Flat ray shape: she flattens to slip OUT of the crowd / under a door when she breaks free.
9. Her tail as the "push comes to shove" - a swipe that sends the files back.
10. The spoon (lore) as her small private joy object in the grey office - the thing she takes to the beach.
11. Her voice and attitude: deadpan, a bit lazy, sudden bursts - perfect for "え？" (Huh?).
