## 2. What the song says
Kanaria never explained the lyrics; they are fragments (pixiv encyclopedia via search). Two readings dominate:
- **The king's last show** (Real Sound, UtaTen column): imprisonment (幽閉), a "show", a wish, warnings, and the
  shout "you are KING" read as the house arrest, trial and execution of Louis XVI, told by his executioner.
- **The forced smile** (translation sites): someone locked in a role, told to be clever and cheerful, grinning with
  bared teeth for an audience - "you are KING" is irony: the crowned one is the prisoner.
Both meet in one picture: **someone confined and put on display, who must perform, and the crowd calls them king.**
The original MV shows exactly that still: a girl on a red throne, the crown NOT on her head but in her lap.

Story of the song in 5 sentences (my reading, for our use): A clever one is locked away. Everyone wants something
from her - a show, a wish, a smile. She performs: left side, right side, teeth bared, pa pa pa. The crowd chants
that she is king. Whether the crown is a prize or a cage is the question the song never answers.

  the "la la love" stretch), a plateau to ~116 s, falling off at the end.

## 6. Where Raymi meets this song
1. **Confined + on display = an aquarium.** Raymi in a glass tank as the star exhibit; "the show" is the aquarium
   show; WARNING = "do not tap the glass" signs; left side / right side = the crowd on both sides of a tunnel tank;
   pa pa pa = phone flashes on the glass. Her canon line "my real voice needs an aquarium with really good acoustics".
2. **The crown in the lap** -> her crown is a bottle cap (she collects knick-knacks), a spoon is the sceptre.
3. **Competitive about inconsequential things** -> she crowns herself KING of something absurdly small (the tank,
   the rock, the ranking of ocean creatures) and defends it.
4. **"Confidence is cute"** (her value) -> the forced grin becomes a REAL grin by the end; the irony turns into
   self-crowning. The song's question (prize or cage?) gets her answer.
5. **Bared teeth** -> rays have tooth plates; her open-mouth fang sprites; the stingray barb as the "teeth" of her tail.
6. **灯闇 = lamp in the dark** -> the show's spotlight; when the show ends, the lights go out (the song's "execution"
   made brand-safe: lights out, curtain).
7. **Salmon** = her one loyal subject (best friend), the only one who stays when the crowd leaves.
8. **Sumi** = the little jester / stage hand.
9. **Ranking of ocean creatures** -> the court: creatures ranked on a leaderboard, "pick-up" (line 22) = a gacha
   banner of herself.
10. **Rhythm games** (hobby) -> "left side / right side" as rhythm-game lanes, notes from both sides.
11. **Flat ray shape / gliding** -> the throne is the sea floor; she can bury in sand and disappear (the empty throne).
12. **Rain predictions** -> a weather-girl "warning" bulletin (ワーニング).
Never: devil / demon framing, horns (her tako ears must never read as horns), blood, real executions.
