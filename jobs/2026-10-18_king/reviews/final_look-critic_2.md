LOOK CRITIC - KING MV (2:15), kit_mv2/view (first_glance, full_00/01/03/05/06/08/09/11/12/13/15 and seq_04 seen at full size; other images in the folder not opened)

1. DESCRIPTION
Dominant colours in the underwater world: deep navy blue (hue about 215, medium saturation, low value), violet and indigo rock masses (hue about 250, medium saturation, low to mid value), and pink and magenta accents (shell, coral, castle roofs; hue about 320, high saturation, mid to high value). In the casino shots the colours are near-black purple (value under 10), a saturated red felt (hue 0, high saturation, mid value) and pale yellow card faces (high value). Raymi is pale blue-white skin with black and violet clothes. Light comes from the upper left or top onto the set (hard cast shadows in full_01 and full_09). Raymi is lit softly from the front and has her own glow, so it is not the same direction as the set light. The eye goes first to the brightest, most saturated or largest shape: the phone frame (full_00), the pearl and shell (full_03), the red felt and yellow cards (full_09), and the faces in the close-ups. It ends last on dark empty areas or edge props. Foreground, subject and background are mostly clear in the close-ups. In the wide casino shots (full_06, full_12, full_13) the background is black void.

2. COLOUR
The saturation peak is right in the shell, castle and card scenes: the pink shell and the red felt carry the colour. In the underwater scenes the pink and cyan props compete with Raymi's face, so there are several peaks (full_05: pink coral right, cyan drop, white-pink Japanese text). The big masses are calm, but the heavy rocks are a flat uniform blue-violet with almost no value range, so they look like cut paper (full_05, full_01). full_01 (top-down) is washed out into a lilac-white floor with a light hex pattern; this is the pale, plastic-looking, grey-violet area of the film and it clashes with the deep navy of the rest. The black voids in full_06, full_08, full_12 and full_13 are 80 to 90 percent of the frame; they are not coloured shadow, just black, and the card shots lose all depth. Shadows are mostly darkened rather than coloured, except the blue shadows in full_01. Her skin stays pale grey-blue (good) but is unlit: in the dark casino scenes she glows as if self-lit.

3. LIGHT
There is no single consistent source. In full_09 and full_11 a spot on the felt shows warm yellow and red pools and a clear contact shadow under her feet, so she does stand in that set. In full_15 a hard cast shadow falls across the floor, but she has no matching shadow; the dark lump covering her hips and legs is a prop (a rock or cushion) that is heavily darkened and swallows her lower body. In full_08 (lying behind the sign) her tail and leg float over a black ground without any contact shadow, and the yellow card behind her head is lit completely differently from her. In full_13 (purple light) she is lit by the pink spot, but the tilted card next to her gets the same spot, which is believable. Contact shadows exist in the 3D props, but the sprite is lit unlit and flat in the wide underwater shots (first_glance, full_00), so she looks pasted next to the castle.

4. COMPOSITION + DEPTH
full_00: the phone screen at the left occupies about 55 percent of the frame, a black rectangle with Raymi's selfie; the second Raymi sits against the castle right. Two identical Raymi figures plus the hard black rectangle make the focus uncertain. full_03: strong, clear: the pearl and shell centred, one focus, good silhouette. full_06 (the crowd): the back of her head with a crown at the right; silhouettes of the crowd as flat navy blocks with grey cards held up; readable but crude. full_08: lying pose, good readable silhouette and strong colour, but the sign '無邪気に' is an unlit pale slab that sits in front of her chest; it covers her hand/chest and is a plastic-looking prop. full_09: the Japanese text sign pair '苦い 思いも' hanging in a black void with nothing around it; nonsensical placement, almost unreadable at phone size (grey on black, low contrast). full_12 and full_13: tiny Raymi (about 10 to 15 percent of frame height) in a huge dark frame; the top 50 percent of the frame is empty black. Figure-ground in grayscale is fine for the card scenes, weak for the dark-on-dark crowd and rocks. Awkward crops: full_15 crops her at the hips by a prop and the floor line cuts her legs; full_09 puts her at the bottom edge with the arm just inside the frame.

6. DETAIL + LOVE
The casino table scenes show the most care: rim line, card backs with pattern, shadows from the stacks. The underwater world is the opposite: every prop is a primitive shape with flat colour and no wear, no texture, no secondary objects (no sand ripple, no light caustics except in the top-down floor, no moss). It is an empty stage with a few toys. The crowd in full_06 and the card tower in full_09 read at phone size only as dark shapes. The throne/shell reads as a shell; the castle reads as a castle; the coins read as brown cylinders. Much of the frame is black void, which looks like a missing set, not a mood.

7. ONE ARTWORK?
No. Three sources are visible: (a) the painted anime sprite (high detail, soft shading); (b) flat low-poly primitives for the sea set (no texture, plain colour); (c) cards, chips and felt with a better textured treatment, plus flat vector UI (phone frame, water drop, text outlines). The detail gap between her painting and the set is large; the set looks like a prototype next to her.

FIX LIST

FIX 1
observation: full_15 (finale, Raymi arms up): a dark lump prop covers her hips and legs; yellow stripes stick out at its sides; a pearl plus a tiny crown on her head.
effect: it reads as broken geometry and removes her body at the most emotional moment; the pearl and crown scale does not match earlier shots.
cause: prop placement and scale (module 05 composition, 09 3D); the prop is dark on dark so it also fails figure-ground.
change: remove or move the lump behind her (or lower it below her knees), lighten it to at least 25 percent value so it separates from the floor, give the crown the same scale as in the earlier shots (about 120 px), and either remove the pearl or place it on the shell behind her.
priority: high
level: local
retest: still of the finale shows her full body to the knees, no prop crossing her hips, crown size equal to full_05.

FIX 2
observation: full_06, full_09, full_12, full_13: 50 to 90 percent of the frame is pure black; in full_09 two grey signs hang in black space with no context.
effect: the sets look unfinished and the viewer cannot read where she is; the text sign is unreadable at phone size.
cause: no ambient fill, no coloured shadow, no backdrop (module 01 colour, 02 light, 05 depth).
change: add an ambient colour fill (deep purple or red-brown, value 12 to 18 percent) and a far wall or curtain with at least two depth layers (distant lights, bokeh, banner); raise text signs to at least 60 percent value on a lit background and 4.4 percent of frame height minimum.
priority: high
level: structural:studio
retest: grayscale view shows at least three value layers in each casino shot; the phone-size view of the sign is readable.

FIX 3
