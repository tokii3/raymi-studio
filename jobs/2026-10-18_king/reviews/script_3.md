**1. Retell**

The text carries a muted viewer through most of it.

- A dark hall, a phone flash, and a stingray girl posing in a tank. She puts a bottle cap on as a crown and rehearses a grin in the glass.
- Taps on the glass make her flinch. A child waves, and she drops the smile the moment the child turns away.
- She buries herself in the sand and the throne is empty. A thrown coin makes her burst out, grin harder and snatch the cap back.
- Phones flash and she zigzags between the crowd halves. A wall of phones points at her. The lights go off bank by bank, her grin falls, and she drops the cap.
- A souvenir deck with her face on every card sits on the ledge. She taps the glass from inside, the deck sinks, and she shrinks into the King card.
- At card scale, a lamp plays whatever its light touches. She tries to climb to it, collapses her house of cards, and breaks out through the card's mirrored half. She topples the coin towers and hits the lamp with a coin.
- In the black her dots light up and the spades peel off the cards as little rays. She grows back to full size, takes the cap and crowns herself. The LED board flips to KING.

This works. The weak points are the lamp rule (shots 44-46) and the growth (75), covered in FIX 2 and 3.

**2. Slop test**

Only Raymi can do these moments:
- Burying in sand as the empty throne (17-18).
- Rays are flat, so she fits on a card (43-44).
- The spade is her silhouette and wakes as rays (57, 71-72).
- 灯闇: her dots are the only light (70).
- The salmon gets the coin (81).
- The cap chain (4, 9, 20, 38, 76-77).
- Shot 53 once had her crying as a pile-buried ray, but the build override changes it (see FIX 4).

That is well over three, so it is not slop.

**3. Against the top three**

The day half has the strength of Renai and LGW: one place, one rule, one return. The weak point is the card-scale rule. "Whatever the lamp covers, it plays" asks the viewer to learn a law, and Renai needs no explanation. It is proved three times (44, 45, 46), but only 46 is loud. The lamp is also never planted in the day section, so the night mechanism appears out of nowhere. The felt "slid in with the deck" is never shown.

**4. Dead stretches**

- **Shot 75 (120.02-121.0, 1 s)** carries the scale payoff, the dead lamp, the stuck coin and the cap glance. That is not readable. It is also the end of wow 5.
- **Shot 81 (127.7-131.8, 4.1 s)** is a single slow pull-back wide. The last quarter has the quietest picture and only one event (the coin to the salmon).
- **Shot 2 (4.6 s)** has two hits for a letter-by-letter LED. Letters light per riff hit, but hits are only 3.62 and 7.59.
- **Shots 36-37** are Raymi back-turned and motionless (WADE_183), then SMUG_WINK to CALM_REST_164. That is two shots of standing while the hall empties.
- **First second:** a phone, a flash and her pose. It is fine.

**5. Unreasoned elements**

- The work lamp has no day-side plant.
- The felt mat "slid in with the cards" has no beat.
- Shot 14's flashes "do the rhythm" while she freezes. This is fine as an inversion, but the statue is the only way she is shown being on display in this stretch.

**6. Sense and love**

- At card scale the coin towers (her wish coins, planted in 22) make sense.
- The coin she hurls is "a disc half her size". She flicks it straight up to a lamp "high above her". That is physically a stretch. A throw would need a winding-up beat or a trajectory plan, and 68 only states the flick.
- The "tank floor" cardtable has no shown seam to the day tank (kelp and rocks behind). 42 and 75 have to carry it.
- The cap lies on its pebble through the whole night. The one frame that shows it (75) has 1 s, so the pebble is easily missed.

**7. Script-specific**

- **The Build changes table contradicts the rows and the script's own rules.** The shot table says "puffed pout", "heart wave", "crying", "royal wave" and "CU wink". The override swaps all of them for CALM_REST / HAPPY_SURF_180 / SMUG_WINK. The screening contract now has two sources that disagree.
- **Shot 82:** the table promises CU_HAPPY_002, "a different sprite from every forced one". The override makes it SMUG_WINK_169, which is the court card's print and the day pose. Rule 5 says that pose is only used under someone else's light. The last frame now repeats the pose the film spent 130 s unlearning. The violet light is the only difference.
- **Sprite reuse:**
  - CALM_REST_164 / 166 stand in for roughly 12 shots (2, 4, 7, 13, 35, 37, 38, 39, 73, 75, 76, 77), many of them as "stands, looks".
  - HAPPY_SURF_180 stands in for 10, 20, 21, 26, 27, 57 and 81.
  - SHOUT_WET_177 is used for 53 (crying), 66 (screaming) and 68 (coin shot).
  - "She stands" is the dominant act in the weakest rows. The camera travels, but the pose does not.
- **Camera:** it travels well (worm's eye, top-down, roll, crane). The repeat is the glass_close / throne_ms pair through the day.
- **Lyric treatments** vary per line and sit on objects. This is the strongest part.
- **Density table:** the stops (17, 33, 69) are the only holds. That is correct.

**Extreme or less?** Stronger would be fewer rule words in the night and a louder lamp. Less would be dropping the shrink and its explanation. The deck-sinks idea is strong enough that the shrink needs only the one shown beat.
