Basis: kit frames (first_glance, seq_00/03/05/07/09, full_05, full_09, phone_08) and script/shots.json. I did not see the whole video, so some of this is sampled.

1. Per moment
- 0-15 s: no lyrics. The shot list wants none, so this is fine.
- 21.6-26 s ("DO NOT TAP THE GLASS" at about 22 s): the yellow sign is a real object of the set and sits in the camera's perspective. That works. The Japanese word "ワーニング" sits above her head as a flat outlined layer that pops in and out. It is tiny and sits next to a tiny sub-line. It reads as "slapped on top", not as part of the sign.
- 53.5-55.7 s ("歯をむき 出して", seq_05): red pixel-ish words sit flat in the lower middle of a dark frame, directly over her torso and arm. They appear row by row. They are small, thin and low-contrast against the dark background and dress. They are not lit by the scene and not in perspective.
- 56-57 s ("パッパッパ", "照れくさいね"): "照れくさいね" is white marker type with a dark outline, tilted, flat and floating beside her. At 56.5 s it is tiny (about 4 % of the frame height), then jumps to big at 57 s. It is not on any object. "パッパッパ" is yellow and flat. The same family of treatment appears everywhere: a flat outlined marker word, rotated slightly.
- 58.5-59.5 s ("レフトサイド / ライトサイド"): two lines in small white type at phone size, almost unreadable in the crowd and light shaft. They are a layer on top.
- 60.5-62.5 s ("パッパッパッハハ"): giant yellow marker type across the lower frame, cut off at the right edge ("ハ" clipped) and sitting over the crowd. It is big and bold, which is good, but it is pasted flat in front of the picture.
- 63-66 s ("You are KING"): this is on a stage board, so it is in the set. That is the best idea in the video. At 63.0-63.5 s the board is far away and the text is about 1 % of the frame, so "You are" cannot be read. "KING" is readable.
- 79-90 s (card table): the lyrics are cards or tiles placed in the world, for example "苦い" and "思いも" on grey tiles at about 88.5 s. This is the right idea, but the tiles are small and pale grey on black. They do not read at phone size, and they do not move word by word with strong intent. Elsewhere the card-table lyrics return to the flat red/yellow outlined layer.
- 105 s onward: I could not check the last third. There are only a few lyric shots after 112 s ("You are KING", "You are"), and the shot list gives no lyrics for the final 20 s, which is intentional.

2. Hierarchy and readability
- Many lines have no hierarchy. A line is one size with no key word. The only hierarchy in the sample is between lines, not inside them.
- Size jumps between lines: tiny (about 4 % of the frame height) on one line, giant (more than 10 %) on the next. It reads as random and not as emphasis.
- Phone size: "レフトサイド/ライトサイド", the "You are" at 63 s and the tile lyrics at 88 s fail the phone test. Reading speed is fine because the lines are short.
- Words that cover her face: none seen. The text over her body at 53.5-55 s is a placement fault, not a designed choice. Crossing the frame edge on "パッパッパッハハ" cuts a glyph.
- The cut word ("ハ") is clipped at the right edge.

3. Variety
- Within the scene objects there is some variety: sign, board, tiles and cards. The free-floating lyrics all use the same family of treatment (outlined marker, flat, same drop shadow), so toki's "immer dieselbe Umrandung" still applies. There is no variation of colour logic between the aquarium and the card table: yellow, red and white appear regardless of the scene light.

Extreme or reduced version: the board and sign idea, pushed to every key word, would be much stronger than half the lines in the world and half pasted on.
