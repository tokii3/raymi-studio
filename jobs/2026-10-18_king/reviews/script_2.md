SCRIPT CRITIC - 4_SCRIPT.md (KING)

1. RETELL (muted)
Dark hall. A phone flashes on a ray-girl in a lit tank and she poses. The LED lights up STAR EXHIBIT. A crowd of silhouettes fills the hall. She puts a bottle cap on her head and practises three grins in the glass. Taps on the glass make her flinch. She buries herself in the sand. A coin lands on her and she bursts out, grinning harder. She zigzags left and right for both halves of the crowd. The crowd crowns her. A wall of phones shows her face. Lights go off bank by bank and her grin drops. A souvenir deck with her face on it sits on the chute ledge. She taps the glass, the deck sinks, and she shrinks into the King card. At card scale a swinging lamp snaps her into a smile whenever its light touches her. She builds a card house toward the lamp and it collapses on her. She pulls out of the card, slaps copies face down, and flicks a coin into the bulb. Black. Her dots light up, the spades turn into little rays, she grows back to full size, crowns herself with the cap, and the LED flips to KING. Her violet-lit wink is the last image. I can retell it. The day half reads fully muted. The night half needs the scale rule and the lamp rule, and I can only retell it because I read the explanations.

2. SLOP TEST
Not slop. Only Raymi does these: burying in sand (the cap alone, then the eyes opening), the bottle cap crown, the hoarded wish coins, the flat ray fitting on a card, the spade as a ray, the dots as the last light (灯闇), the salmon at the glass, the flinch at loud noises. That is far more than three.

3. AGAINST THE TOP THREE
Renai, LGW and POP IN 2 each have one clean engine a muted viewer reads in a second. This idea stacks six: day/night, scale shift, a lamp that deals cards, merch, the spade seed, the LED. The lamp rule needs three proofs (shots 44-47) and the scale rule needs a table. If the viewer misses a rule, 78-113 becomes "a girl with cards and a lamp". Less would be stronger: cut 53-54 and fold 60 into 59. The extreme version would be stronger in the shrink, the key image, which gets almost no screen time.

4. DEAD STRETCHES
Nothing runs over 8 s and the first second works. The opposite problem: about 25 consecutive cuts of 1-1.9 s from the same tank_front/glass_close/hall_wide family (shots 6-31). It is a pose slideshow with too few distinct camera positions in the day. Shot 2 (4.6 s, tiny in a wide, adjusts the kelp) is the thinnest action in the intro. Shot 81 is a 4 s hold with only a small coin flick. The last third is strong.

5. UNREASONED ELEMENTS
- LED flips (80): the cause is never stated. Does her jump flip it? The treatment says "its own night circuit".
- Stamp is the default type treatment, 11 uses (6, 11, 14, 20, 21, 27, 31, 52, 61, 63, 67). The "varied lyric objects" claim is only half true.
- Lamp as dealer is an imposed rule, not a found one. The viewer takes it on trust.
- Shot 59 pops her out of the card's mirrored half, but 58 never puts her in the card. The mechanism is missing, so wow 4 reads as a random flip.
- Shot 9: the child's fog-writing is on the outside of the glass, so it is mirrored to the camera inside.

6. SENSE AND LOVE
- Scale contradiction in 41-43: she is 1.55 m and the cards are about 9 cm. The 52 faces are illegible, the cards cannot be a "lid closing", and in 43 she lies chin-on-hands on a card smaller than her hand. The viewer sees a giantess and confetti.
- Shot 49: a three-level house of cards, each card her own height, does not plausibly reach a lamp "high above".
- Shot 75: the coin stuck in the dead lamp's cage is the proof of the whole scale conceit, but at real size it is a tiny coin in a cage and will not read in about 1 s.

7. SCRIPT MECHANICS
- Raymi only poses or stands in shots 7, 14, 17, 33, 34, 36, 37, 82. Day acting is mostly sprite swaps.
- Shot 43 (1.25 s) carries lie-down, push-in, shrink and card-pass. Shot 75 (0.98 s) carries crane, growth, evidence and cap glance. Both are overpacked.
- Times match the measured map (stops 38.3-39.4, 64.3-65.4, 113.4-114.6).
- The final sprite CU_HAPPY_002 has a cut left edge, which the script itself flags. I could not verify that framing.
