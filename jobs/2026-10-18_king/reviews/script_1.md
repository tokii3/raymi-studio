REVIEW of jobs/2026-10-18_king/4_SCRIPT.md (82 shots, with shots.json, vs 3_TREATMENT.md and 1_DOSSIER.md)

1. RETELL (muted)
A dark hall. A phone flashes and a ray-girl in a lit tank winks for it. She puts on a bottle cap, practises the grin in the glass, and flinches at taps. A coin hits the sand and she bursts out, grinning harder. The hall crowns her and every phone holds her face. The lights bang off and her grin drops. A deck of cards printed with her face sinks into the tank and she dives into the King card. At night one lamp plays her like a card, and she builds a house of cards toward it. It collapses on her, she fights back and knocks the lamp out with a coin. Her own glow wakes the spades into rays, the LED flips to KING, and she winks and goes black. Mostly this reads. Two things don't. The lamp rule ("whatever the pool touches, it plays") needs 3-4 repeats before a muted viewer gets it. The scale of the cards, the tank and Raymi is not readable (see 6).

2. SLOP TEST
Not slop. These moments only work as Raymi:
- Burying in the sand for the empty throne (17-18).
- The bottle-cap crown.
- Flat on a card because rays are flat (44).
- Flinching at taps (fear of loud noises).
- 灯闇: her dots as the last light (70).
- The coin hoard that becomes the chip towers.
- Spades as ray silhouettes.
That is well over three.

3. AGAINST THE TOP THREE
Renai, LGW and POP IN 2 each live in one readable world. KING has two (day aquarium, night card table) joined by a scale flip. That bridge (shots 41-43 and 75) is the weakest, least-decided part of the script. The idea also has no small, funny, human beat like Renai's desk. The salmon is a cameo, and the treatment's coin-to-salmon beat was dropped. 82 shots in 135 s (about 1.6 s each) plus about 35 new components is a feasibility risk.

5. UNREASONED ELEMENTS
- The claim that SMUG_WINK_169 "means the opposite" when lit by herself (74, 82) cannot be seen. It is the same sprite as the court card (46, 51, 68). Only light and colour can carry that difference, and the text assigns neither.
- Lyric treatments are narrow, and the reasons come after the choice. Glass finger-writing is on shots 9, 12, 23, 24, 25, 26 and 30. Night is "card" or "stamp" on about 25 rows (44-66), with one material.
- Shot 40: why does her tap push the deck down the coin slot? There is no causal beat.
- "52 copies" is an idea for the poster, not a picture. Nobody will count them.

6. SENSE AND LOVE
- Scale is contradictory.
  - Shot 41: she spins among small cards, so she is full size.
  - Shot 43: she lies on the King card, still full size.
  - Shot 44: she is card-sized.
  - Shot 75: the cards are "small" and she is "full size".
  - The text never says when she shrinks.
  - The lamp is a real work lamp, yet she pops its bulb with a coin she hoarded at full size, and that coin is stuck in the cage. At card scale the lamp would be far away and the coin huge.
- Cap continuity is broken.
  - It is on her head at 30, 32 and 34. It lies in the sand at 17.
  - No row takes it off after 34.
  - Shot 76 says it is "the cap from stop 1, nobody is watching".
  - The wow 5 payoff depends on this.
- In 44-51 the lamp-pool rule makes her a puppet reacting to a spotlight. That is a rule, not a want.
- Empty stage: shots 2, 3 and 36-38 are mostly set and crowd, with Raymi small.

VERDICT: BLOCK: fault - the scale logic of the deck, tank and card table is contradictory in shots 41-44 and 75, and the cap's chain (head at 30-35, "sand at stop 1" at 76) is broken, so the key wow 3 and wow 5 cannot be filmed as written.

FIX 1
observation: Raymi's size is never stated across 41-44 and 75. She is full size among small cards at 41, "lies over the King card" at 43, card-sized at 44, and full size again at 75. The lamp is a real work lamp and the coin is hers.
effect: Wow 3 reads as cards falling around a big girl. The scale flip at 75 reads as a cut, and the lamp kill has no physical logic.
cause: Principle 15 (places make sense) and Principle 2 (story of actions).
change: Pick one rule and write it into the rows. For example, she shrinks in 43 as the portrait fills the frame, and both sets share one world scale (cards ~9 cm, lamp ~60 cm). Every night row carries a "scale" field. At 75 the crane shows the cards at real size next to the full-size her. The coin she flicks must be a chute coin at her scale, and the dead lamp in the cage is the proof.
priority: high

FIX 2
observation: The cap is on her head at 30-35, on the sand at 76 ("from stop 1"), and there is no row for taking it off.
effect: The crown's path (costume, theirs, hers) breaks. The finale payoff of "the cap from the same spot" fails.
cause: Principle 14 (an anchor returns with a change) and the cap chain in the treatment.
change: Add a row at 38 or 39-40: the grin falls and she lifts the cap off and sets it on the sand. At 62.89 the crowd crowns her with it. At 68 it slips off, or she leaves it on the throne. At 120 it must be in a place the viewer saw.
priority: high

FIX 3
