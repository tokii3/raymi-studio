## Idea A - The Star Exhibit
- **The one sentence:** the aquarium's star exhibit has to perform for the glass all day - until she turns the
  glass around and crowns herself in the dark.
- **World** (one place / medium, built - what it is made of): a public aquarium tank (sand, rocks, kelp, coral, the
  tank's toy castle, a clam-shell throne, her bottle-cap crown) behind a big glass front, and the dark visitor hall
  in front of it (velvet rope, built silhouettes holding phones, an LED "STAR EXHIBIT" board, a "DO NOT TAP THE
  GLASS" sign). Light: the tank's cyan water light vs the violet dark of the hall; at the end only her own glow.
- **Why only Raymi** (name / body / lore / voice): a stingray IS the aquarium star (touch pools, ray tunnels); her
  canon line "my real voice needs an aquarium with really good acoustics"; rays bury themselves in sand = the song's
  empty throne without anybody dying; 灯闇 = a lamp in the dark = the finale when the hall goes black; she collects
  knick-knacks, so her crown is a bottle cap she found; competitive about nothing, so she ends up rating the humans.
- **Story of actions, muted** (5 sentences): Morning, the crowd presses against the glass and she performs on cue -
  left side, right side, a grin with bared teeth for the phone flashes. At the first stop the throne is suddenly
  empty (she buried herself in the sand) and the crowd panics, then she bursts out with her bottle cap and the
  show goes on harder. After closing time she plays alone with the toy castle and cries herself down. In chorus 2
  the camera flips: from inside the tank WE see the humans squashed against the glass - they are the exhibit, and
  she hangs a sign on them. The lights go out, and in the dark only she glows: she puts the crown on her own head.
- **Lyric medium** (where the words live in this world): the aquarium's own signage - the LED board, the exhibit
  placard ("STINGRAY - STATUS: KING"), the WARNING signs (ワーニング = DO NOT TAP THE GLASS, flashing), words
  written on the glass with a finger, the visitors' phone screens.
- **5 wows** (time + what, strongest last):
  1. 38.3 - the first stop: the throne is empty, only the bottle cap lies there; two eyes open in the sand.
  2. 51.7 - "left side, right side": she darts between two crowds (tunnel tank), smears, the phones flash on every pa.
  3. 64.3 - the stop on "YOU": every phone in the hall points at her, a wall of screens all showing her face.
  4. 100.9 - "how annoying": reverse angle, the humans pressed against the glass are the exhibit; she rates them.
  5. 113.4 - lights out, only her glow; on the last "You are KING" she crowns herself; the LED board flips to KING.
- **The Short** (window + its own twist): 49.6-65.5 (Short window A: last pre-chorus line into chorus 1 and the
  "YOU" stop). Twist: shot from a visitor's phone (vertical = the phone you film her with); on the stop she taps the
  glass back at YOU. CTA: "which sea creature is the real king of the aquarium?"
- **Risk** (what could make it weak): glass between her and us is close to Let's Get Weird (the screen glass) - so
  the glass never breaks here, the turn is the reversed point of view. The crowd must not look like generic
  mannequins (silhouettes with phones, each a different shape, lit by the tank). Needs a tunnel-tank variant of the set.
- **Key frames:** boards/A1.jpg (the exhibit + the crowd), A2 (the empty throne, the stop), A3 (lights out, she
  crowns herself)

## Idea B - King of Spades
- **The one sentence:** Raymi is the King of Spades in a deck that keeps getting played - she climbs out of her card,
  loses her castle, and wins the table with her own suit.
- **World** (one place / medium, built - what it is made of): a giant card table on the sea floor under one swinging
  lamp - ultramarine felt with a gold print, chip towers, a deck, a house of cards, and the KING card with her own
  painted portrait (double-headed like a real court card). Raymi is card-sized: a card is 1.8 m tall. Light: one
  warm lamp cone in the deep blue dark; kelp and bubbles far behind.
- **Why only Raymi** (name / body / lore / voice): the spade ♠ IS a stingray's silhouette (diamond body, thin tail) -
  her body is the suit; rays are flat, she can flatten into a card ("rays are flat polygons" gag); competitive about
  inconsequential things = a card game she takes deadly seriously; she collects knick-knacks (chips, cards).
- **Story of actions, muted** (5 sentences): The deck is shuffled across the lens and her face flickers on every card -
  she is printed on the King, smiling the court card's smile, played and slapped down again and again. At the stop the
  King card turns over and the portrait is EMPTY: she climbed out and stands on the felt, card-sized among the chip
  towers. She builds a house of cards as her castle, it collapses on "ダウン", she is buried under cards. Then every
  spade on every card comes alive as a little ray and swims to her. She sits on top of a new house of cards with a
  halo of swimming spades as her crown, and the last card dealt to us shows her - winking, not forced.
- **Lyric medium** (where the words live in this world): the cards themselves - every word dealt onto the felt as a
  card, key words on the King's index, chips stamped with words, the felt's gold print.
- **5 wows** (time + what, strongest last):
  1. 3.0 - the shuffle: a cascade of cards across the lens, her face flickering card by card on the riff.
  2. 38.3 - the stop: the King card flips and its portrait frame is empty.
  3. 51.7 - "left side, right side": the card rotates 180 degrees and she pops out of its mirrored half upside down
     (the camera rolls with her).
  4. 89.0 - "ダウン": the house of cards collapses in slow motion around her.
  5. 113.4 - the spades come alive as real rays and spiral up into a crown around her on the rebuilt tower.
- **The Short** (window + its own twist): 49.6-65.5. Twist: the Short is a card dealt to YOU - it lands face down,
  flips on "You are KING" and she is on it. CTA: "what card would you be?"
- **Risk** (what could make it weak): nobody is playing - the game must read without hands (cards move by themselves,
  the swinging lamp is "the house"); could drift into Alice-in-Wonderland card soldiers; the card shuffle needs real
  animation work (many moving cards).
- **Key frames:** boards/B1.jpg (the table and the King card), B2 (left side / right side between the giant cards),
  B3 (top of the house of cards under the lamp)

## Idea C - Storm Warning
- **The one sentence:** Raymi is the always-wrong weather girl of a cheerful TV show, forced to grin through every
  forecast - until the storm she was warned about turns out to be her.
- **World** (one place / medium, built - what it is made of): a TV weather studio - a big ocean weather map wall with
  islands and symbols, a ticker band, a news desk "WEATHER WITH RAYMI", two pedestal studio cameras with red tally
  lights, softboxes on stands, a lighting truss, an ON AIR box. Light: bright TV blue + warm softboxes; then storm
  violet; at the end only her glow in the flooded, powerless studio.
- **Why only Raymi** (name / body / lore / voice): her canon running gag - she predicts rain and is wrong more often
  than right; ワーニング = a storm warning; the ocean is her element, so the storm is literally her sea; 灯闇 = her
  glow when the studio power dies; "confidence is cute" - she owns the wrong forecast with a smug face.
- **Story of actions, muted** (5 sentences): ON AIR: she forecasts sunshine with a grin that shows her teeth, pointing
  left side, right side at the map while the ticker keeps scrolling WARNING behind her. The warnings pile up on the
  map, the cameras roll closer like a crowd, and the ratings call her KING. In the break, alone, rain starts leaking
  from the lighting truss onto her and she cries herself down. Then the map's storm spiral gets her face, she rips
  the script and her sea floods the studio - cameras topple, she surfs the weather map. The power dies, only she
  glows, and the map reboots: "TODAY: RAYMI - 100 %" - for once her forecast is right.
- **Lyric medium** (where the words live in this world): the broadcast's own type - the ticker, the teleprompter,
  map labels and the WARNING banner, the ON AIR box, the studio camera's viewfinder overlay.
- **5 wows** (time + what, strongest last):
  1. 0.0 - cold open through a studio camera's viewfinder: 3-2-1, the tally light goes red, her grin.
  2. 38.3 - the stop: "PLEASE STAND BY", the studio dark, the presenter mark empty.
  3. 51.7 - "left side, right side": she flicks the storm fronts across the map and the map obeys.
  4. 100.9 - "how annoying": the flood - water bursts in, cameras topple, she surfs the map.
  5. 113.4 - power cut, only her glow, the map reboots with her as the forecast.
- **The Short** (window + its own twist): 49.6-65.5. Twist: vertical = a phone weather-app alert that turns into her
  broadcast; ends on "100 % chance of Raymi". CTA: "what's the weather where you are?"
- **Risk** (what could make it weak): the song's royal image (crown, being crowned) is weakest here - KING becomes
  "#1 in the ratings"; the flood needs convincing water; a TV studio can feel corporate unless it is HER show with
  love in the details.
- **Key frames:** boards/C1.jpg (ON AIR, the forced grin at the map), C2 (left side / right side through the studio
  camera), C3 (the flooded studio, her glow)

says (someone confined and displayed for a crowd), the turn is a camera idea only a 3D set can do (the reversed
glass), and the payoff comes from her name, like Let's Get Weird. B is the most surprising find (the spade is a ray)
and could become a set piece inside A or a later lore Short. Weiss ich nicht sicher: whether the aquarium feels too
close to LGW's glass for toki - the boards show the difference.
