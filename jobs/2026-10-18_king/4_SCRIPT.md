## What this script settles (open notes from the treatment reviews)
1. **The deck enters without a keeper or a cart.** A visitor left a loose souvenir deck on the wish chute's ledge
   (hall side, at the coin slot). After closing SHE taps the glass from inside (71.96 / 72.31) and the flat cards slide
   one after another down the coin slot into her water - cards are flat like coins (and like rays). Act 4 breathes:
   one beat for the salmon (70.15), the rest belongs to the sinking spiral (wow 3).
2. **ONE scale rule, shown twice, never cut around** (review script_1 FIX 1):
   | when | shots | her size | what the viewer sees |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | day and closing | 1-42 | full size (1.55 m set units) | the deck is real playing cards, hand-sized: in the sinking (41-42) they flutter around her like leaves |
   | the shrink | 43 (76.65-77.96) | full -> card-sized | she lies over the hand-sized King card; as the camera pushes into the portrait her figure is drawn into it and shrinks to the card's size (puppet height tween), the card face fills the frame, a card passes the lens |
   | the night | 44-74 | card-sized (her height = one card) | the `cardtable` set IS the tank floor at card scale (1 set metre = 5 cm): the felt is the souvenir deck's little playing mat that slid in with the cards, the "chips" are her wish coins (each a disc half her height), the kelp and rocks behind are the tank's own, the hanging lamp is the tank's real night-service work lamp |
   | the growth | 75 (120.02-121.0) | card-sized -> full | one continuous crane up: the rays lift her and she grows back while the cards, the coin towers and the dead work lamp shrink to their real size around her |
   | finale | 75-82 | full size | the real deck lies scattered on the sand, small |
   Every night row says CARD SCALE in its set column.
3. **The lamp is underwater and physical.** It is the tank's night-service work lamp: a caged lamp on a cable,
   submerged, so currents can swing it (88.93). At card scale it is a giant lamp high above her, and the coin she
   hurls (112.83) is a disc half her size - big enough to smash the bulb through the cage. The growth shot (75)
   shows the evidence at real size: the dead work lamp on its cable, the coin stuck in its cage.
4. **The cap chain is continuous** (review script_1 FIX 2): on the sand by the throne (frame 0) -> she puts it on
   (11.59, shot 4) -> knocked off and caught (18.81, shot 9) -> left on its pebble when she buries herself (stop 1,
   38.3) -> she snatches it back on as she bursts out (43.0, shot 20) -> on her head through chorus 1 -> she pulls
   it off and drops it on the same pebble when the grin falls (69.1, shot 38) -> it stays in the tank all night
   (outside the deck) -> lies there at the growth (75) -> she lifts it on the vocal (121.0) -> on her own head
   (122.5).
5. **The performance pose only under someone else's light** (FIX 3): SMUG_WINK_169 (arms out, wink) is the court
   card's print and her day pose - used only while phones, the LED or the lamp are on her (1, 27, 32, 35, 37, 46, 51,
   68). In her own light she strikes it once by reflex (74) and breaks it into a real laugh; the finale's poses are
   new (HAPPY_SPIN_178 / 189, HAPPY_JUMP_160 / 161) and the last wink is a different sprite (CU_HAPPY_002, laughing,
   finger up) lit violet from below by her dots instead of amber from above.
6. **Lyric objects vary per line** (FIX 4): day = breath-fog on the glass (9, 23), finger-writing on the glass only
   for left / right (24, 25), the DO NOT TAP sign (11, 21), the engraved brass placard (7, 12), phone screens (10,
   13, 26, 27), stone (14), bubbles (15, 28), the LED (16, 30, 32, 35), the chute plate (19, 22), sand (6, 29), the
   sand cloud (20); night = cards DEALT only for five lines (44, 49, 50, 56, 68), engraved coins (45, 54), the card's
   own print (46, 58, 59, 60), sob bubbles (47, 53), the lamp's pool writing light (51), the felt's gold / red print
   (55, 62), the rim's gold piping as a ticker (64, 65), stamps only where something slams (52, 61, 63, 67); her
   light = words drawn in violet light (57, 70).
7. **One rule at night, proven on a card first, then on her** (FIX 7): whatever the lamp's pool covers, it plays.
   Proof 1 (shot 44, 79.0-79.18): the pool slides off her onto the next card, which flips face up and slaps down by
   itself. Proof 2 (shot 45): every card the swinging pool crosses does the same. Proof 3 (shot 46, 81.17, loud,
   camera kick): the pool hits HER and she snaps into the court card's smile - the same sprite printed on the card
   beside her. Her want against the rule: reach the lamp and switch it off (48 looks up, 49 builds the ladder, 51
   reaches). After that the rule returns only as story beats (88.93 at the top of the house, 112.07 the verdict). The
   PICK UP banner and the spade seed are one object: her own King card (it rises as the prize at 94.0, she grabs
   it at 96.1, its spade glows under her fingers at 97.27).
8. **Lyrics are written** wherever the picture wants a word (toki 2026-10-18: missing glyphs are built in her style;
   `raymi font ensure` runs before stills / renders). Never written: 逝く前に (17.0) and the whole line 無様に〇ねる
   (83.86) - acted for brand safety. The written word always sits on an object of the act: day = glass (finger
   writing), the DO NOT TAP sign, the placard, the chute's brass plate, the LED, phone screens, the sand; night =
   cards (dealt), the felt's gold print, stamps on falling cards; her light = glowing bubble-words. CLOSED / STAR
   EXHIBIT / KING on the LED are props (prop signs), not lyric layers.
9. **Times corrected:** act 3 ends at 66.57 (the first light bank); house levels 85.68 / 86.4 sit inside their row;
   the tail starts at 127.7 (section bound); the second "You are KING" starts 65.78, its KING is 66.55.
10. **Every loud stretch has an event per beat or hit** (density table below), the measured vocal span 121.0-122.2
   gets its own picture (the cap lifts on the voice), and the last image is an action on the last beat (132.89).

Camera names (`hall_wide`, `tank_front`, `throne_low`, `throne_ms`, `inside_out`, `top_down`, `glass_close`;
`table_wide`, `felt_low`, `card_ms`, `house_up`) are the built cameras in `studio/src/three/sets/sets.json`; numbers
in shots.json are world metres. Sprite names are short forms of the catalogued ids (`PEACE_WINK_131` =
`WS_F_PEACE_WINK_UV_131`).
