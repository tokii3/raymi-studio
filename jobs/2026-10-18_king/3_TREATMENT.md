## The one idea
**"By day she is the aquarium's star exhibit, smiling on cue. At night the gift shop's souvenir deck sinks into her
tank and she is only a picture on a card. She wins by becoming the one light nobody can sell: her own."**
The song's core (dossier 2): someone confined and put on display, who must perform, and the crowd calls them king -
prize or cage? We answer it with ONE place seen in two lights, so it stays one story, not a scene mix (toki's core:
faster, darker, more variety than the aquarium alone):
1. **DAY = the tank seen from the hall** (set `aquarium`): glass, crowd, phones, the LED board "STAR EXHIBIT". Light
   comes from outside (tank lamps, phone flashes). Everything she does is FOR someone. The crowd says "You are KING".
2. **NIGHT = the same tank floor seen at card scale** (set `cardtable`): at closing the keeper parks the gift shop's
   cart by the glass - on it the souvenir deck, her face printed on the King of Spades. SHE wants to see what they
   made of her: she taps the glass from inside (the one who taps is now her), the box topples into the wish chute and
   sinks into her tank. She is pulled into the King card and is card-sized among the 52 printed copies of herself.
   One light only: the tank's night service lamp hanging over the open top, swinging. **The lamp is the dealer:**
   whatever its pool touches, it plays - cards flip and slap down, and when it falls on HER she snaps flat into the
   court card's smile, exactly like the day grin for the flashes. In the dark beyond the pool she is herself.
3. **HER LIGHT** (finale): she climbs, fails, fights, and finally flicks her last wish coin into the bulb - the lamp
   goes out (stop 113.4) and the only light left is her own glow (灯闇 = a lamp in the dark). The spades on every card
   (planted at 97: the spade on her card glows once in her hand, then the lamp washes it out) wake in her light as
   little rays and become her court and her crown; the scale flips back, and in the black, empty aquarium she puts
   the bottle cap on her own head. Now SHE says "You are KING".
Rules of this world: (a) light always tells who owns the moment, and whoever's light falls on her, she performs for
(the flashes by day, the lamp by night - only in her own light is she herself);
(b) the lyric lives only on things that exist here (LED board, warning sign, wish-chute plate, phone screens, cards,
the felt's gold print, her glow) - no subtitle band ever; (c) the bottle cap is the crown in every act and only its
REASON changes (costume for the crowd -> left lying in the sand -> printed on merch -> her own choice).
Fused with her sea: the wish chute's coins are the "お願い" (wishes) the crowd throws at her, and at night the same
coins are the chip towers on the felt; the spade ♠ is a stingray's silhouette, so her court is literally her kind.

## Raymi's part (not only the song)
- **Attitude:** day = the professional idol grin that drops the second nobody looks (light tsundere: bored pout behind
  the glass, perfect wink when a phone comes up). Night = sulking, then furious (competitive about the most
  inconsequential thing: a card game nobody is playing). Finale = "confidence is cute": she crowns herself for nobody.
- **Her body does the plot:** rays bury themselves in sand (the song's empty throne without anybody dying); rays are
  flat (she fits on a card - lore gag "rays are flat polygons", she flattens herself onto the King card at 77.96);
  her glowing dots are the last light (her name); her tail barb is her "teeth" at night.
- **Her lore:** she collects knick-knacks (the bottle cap came down the wish chute once; she hoards the wish coins);
  the salmon is her best friend - the only creature that stays at the glass when the crowd leaves at closing and the
  one she gives a coin to in the finale; fear of loud sudden noises = the glass tapping (she flinches every time).
- **What she wants:** to stop being something other people own (exhibit, photo, merch). By day she only hides (the
  sand); at closing she acts for the first time (taps the box in, out of vanity and curiosity); at night her want is
  one visible goal: **reach the lamp and switch it off** (out of its pool she is herself). She gets it not by escaping
  the tank but by putting out every light that isn't hers and deciding what the crown means.
- **Dark share (toki: faster, darker):** the hall is dark from frame 0 (the tank is the only light), the tank's
  spot never fully returns after stop 1, and from 66.57 (lights bang off) to the end it is night or black: 66.6 of
  134.9 s = half the video is dark, and the dark half holds three of the five wows, the biggest among them. Verse 1 is cut to six beats so the day moves faster.
