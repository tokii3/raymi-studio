# IDEAS - Rain Prediction Record #11: rain in Tokyo tomorrow

Chosen from the POV Shorts plan of 2026-10-23 (nine claims, toki picked 1-7). Series dossier:
`knowledge/studies/pov_shorts_series_2026-10.md`.

## The idea
- **The one sentence:** Live on 'Weather with Raymi', she leans into the studio camera and bets one hundred percent on rain in Tokyo tomorrow, despite a record of three right out of ten - and if she is wrong, she simply covers the lens.
- **World:** the weather studio (set `weather`): map wall, WEATHER WITH RAYMI desk, ticker, ON AIR box, studio cameras. The lens is the studio camera = your TV.
- **Why only Raymi:** (1) her running rain-prediction gag + the public counter (series lore only she has); (2) tsundere-confident about a 30 % record ('basically a streak'); (3) she covers the lens with her hand when the truth threatens - the camera is you, she hides from you.
- **Story of actions, muted:** She leans into the camera and makes a bold call -> the camera pulls back: the ticker shows her record 3 of 10 -> she calls it a streak -> she slaps the map: a rain cloud over Tokyo, 100 % -> she leans in and whispers that if she is wrong you never saw this, her hand covers the lens -> back on air, arms crossed, the bet stands.
- **Main element:** her face at the lens (the call) and the map's rain cloud over Tokyo with '100%' (the bet).
- **Hook (spoken + stamp, <= 8 words):** "rain tomorrow. one hundred percent."
- **CTA (spoken + stamp, >= 2.5 s before the end):** "right or wrong? bet in the comments."
- **Risk:** the camera-as-viewer moves must read as YOUR body (recoil, step, being grabbed), not as a cinematic
  move; her clips must keep her alive between lines (never a held pose for more than a beat).
