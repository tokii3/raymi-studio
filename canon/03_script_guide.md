# Script guide (cleaned from the old 03_Video-Scripting-Guide + Video-Script-Bible, 2026-10-17)

What to write and how to pace it. How it is drawn lives in `studio/`, the checks in `raymi/plan.py`.

## Core rules
- Hook = first 0-3 s, ONE curiosity gap, never answered in the hook. Must work with sound off: first frame + hook
  stamp alone carry it. No logo, no "hey guys", no slow build. Stamp <= 8 words.
- Setup resolves within ~8 s, then deliver exactly what the hook promised; the payoff closes the question.
- Raymi's personality is the product, not the topic (`01_character.md`). Lowercase, no em dashes, ocean words,
  tsundere-lite. Brand-safe: no religion, politics, sexual content.
- Anti-slop test: could this script run on any other VTuber channel with the name changed? Then it is not done -
  it needs a moment that only works because it is Raymi (lore callback, her reaction, her design).
- Spoken text: plain speakable English, numbers as words, no symbols/kaomoji (the check refuses them).
- Timing estimate: about 2.5 words per second; the real voice take is the truth.

## Shape of a lore Short (15-35 s)
- 0-3 s HOOK, 3-8 s SETUP with a mini promise, BODY in beats, last ~3 s PAYOFF + loop/CTA.
- Loop ending (last line or picture echoes the hook) works well for lore.
- CTA is a specific comment question in her voice, on screen >= 2.5 s before the end - never "like and subscribe".

## Pacing (updated with the 2026-10 analytics: faster than the old 4-7 s guide)
- A visual change every 2-4 s (new beat, act, icon, key word camera kick). Reveal beats may breathe up to ~5 s,
  at most once per video.
- Her expression changes every beat (the check enforces a new sprite); a repeat only as a deliberate loop callback.
- Sumi (male mascot, small black stingray) only for a reaction cutaway or a punchline, not as decoration.
  (No Sumi asset is in this repo yet - deferred.)

## Formats
- Lore / fact / bit Shorts in her voice (running gags in `01_character.md`).
- Opinion Shorts on gaming / anime / internet topics (never politics/religion/sexual topics). No reaction content.
- Song Shorts (12-20 s) only with a Raymi line or gag; rights per song with toki (see CLAUDE.md).
- Later: long-form lore pieces and music videos.

## Before a script is done
- Hook stands alone with sound off. Payoff closes the hook. One distinctly-Raymi moment.
- Not a repeat: check `07_video_catalog.md` and the newest `10_kanal_puls*`.
