# Let's Get Weird (longestsoloever) - TREATMENT (toki 2026-10-07: concept A + B combined)

## The one idea
**"Something lives in the dark inside your screen - and she brought a lamp."**
The video happens in the dark. There are exactly two lights, and everything we see is shown by one of them:
1. **the screen** (your TV in the MV, your phone in the Short): the glass is the wall between her world and yours (A).
2. **her UV lamp**: Touyami = 灯闇, "a lamp in the dark". What it touches reveals invisible ink: the lyrics, the field
   journal's secrets and real ciphers (B).
The lyric medium is **ink**: written in her field journal ("ANOMALIES OF THE DEEP vol. III"), on the inside of the
glass and later on the walls of your room, visible only where light falls. No caption bar, ever.
Story: she is trapped behind the glass (Bill: "trapped in the minds of anyone dumb enough to let me inside"), gets
you to open the door, breaks the glass on the first chorus, floods your room with her deep sea and makes it hers.
Fused with her ocean (tamagotchi rule): the flood IS her sea; the journal is a marine biologist's book of anomalies -
and she is the anomaly in it ("specimen #001").

## Raymi's part (not only the song)
- Villain mode as HER character: smug, confident, JoJo energy (ゴゴゴ menacing kana when she is ominous).
- Her eye inside a gold triangle (the 'flat polygon cyclops') - the engine's triangle window, always HER painted eye.
- Lore gag in the MV ("flat polygon cyclops"): she checks herself in the journal - "rays are flat polygons. rhombus."
- Ciphers (Gravity Falls tradition): 5 real ciphers (Atbash / Caesar-3 / A1Z26) hidden in the video, each a Raymi
  line; the pinned comment asks who decodes all five. The Short's CTA is a cipher.

## Animation principles (every move, not decoration)
- **Anticipation** before every hit: the picture pulls back / compresses for 2-6 frames before a slam or a drop.
- **Squash + stretch** on slams (type and shards), **overshoot** on every arrival (back_out), never linear.
- **Exaggeration**: the drop is not a cut but an explosion: shards fly at the lens, camera shake 3 frames, impact frame.
- **Smear / motion blur** on whips (comp shutter), **follow-through** of hair and tail (puppet), **hold** after a big
  move (a still beat makes the next one hit).
- **Staging**: one read per moment - the ink word, or her face, never both fighting.
- The picture changes on most loud beats (kick on every beat at 140 BPM -> camera punch, crack, ink sweep or cut).
