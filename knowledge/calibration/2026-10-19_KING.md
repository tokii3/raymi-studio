(verbatim critic answers) vs `jobs/2026-10-18_king/toki.md` (her words). Evidence for the studio - KING is not a

## What the critics did well
- Every mechanical fault class they found in the pilot became a guard (cut edges, clipped text, props in the lens,
  a sliced face) and never came back - the guards are the critics' best output.
- Lyrics: type-critic and the Short's blind viewer named toki's complaint (flat, small, untranslated) before her.

## What they got wrong
1. **Missed the main element.** Nobody knew what the video hangs on, so the crown (13 x 7 cm on a 1.55 m figure,
   0.2-4.2 % of the frame height in 28 of 30 shots - measured afterwards) was never checked as such.
2. **Blocked the art she approved.** 6 of 7 critics BLOCKed on look / motion / rhythm / story taste she approved or
   praised. A BLOCK on taste makes every package a FAIL and the verdict meaningless.

## What changed (2026-10-19)
- Every shot critic's FIRST question: is the main element (`kit/script/heroes.json`, per shot in `kit/script/shots.json`
  `hero`) readable at phone size? Unreadable main element = `BLOCK: fault`. The blind viewer (no intentions) names
  what it thinks the video is about and whether that thing reads at phone size.
- type-critic's first questions: weight and size at phone size; a Japanese line without romaji + English = a fault.
- BLOCK only for real faults a viewer would call a mistake, or generic slop. A component toki liked / approved
  (`studio/catalog/status.json`) is never "slop" by itself - notes on it are FIX lines for toki.
- Lyric style frames 1 (`jobs/2026-10-19_lyrics-v2/reviews/style_type-critic_1.md`): the type-critic called the
  JP + romaji + English stack "slop" - toki asked for exactly that, so it is never slop; its valid points became studio
  parts (lyrics hidden behind a prop = a guard; romaji / English 0.7 of a line, >= 5 % at phone size; carrier objects).
- The studio measures what the critics missed: hero size before every render (`check.ts heroFaults`), its value
  contrast after (`raymi/hero.py`), lyric v2 size at phone size.
