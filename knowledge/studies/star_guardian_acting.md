## Picture - what makes it work
- **Acting, not illustration.** Every shot is a character DOING something with a clear intention (hold out roses,
  scheme, grab, drop a sack, swing a wand, fall asleep). The story is readable with the sound off.
- **Icons are the dialogue.** Speech bubbles with ONE symbol: heart, "!", "?", "...", "!?", anger vein, X, lightbulb,
  "Zz", or a tiny portrait of the person she thinks of. They POP in (scale 0 -> overshoot -> settle in 2-3 frames),
  wobble while held, pop out. That replaces every word.
- **Simple assets, used cleverly.** Chibi sprites with a thick white sticker outline, 2-3 drawings per character
  (idle, reaction, action). Two flat sets (stage with stars, bedroom with bunk bed) reused all video. Props are simple
  shapes with a role: roses, the "Mine" sign, a whiteboard, a sack, a camcorder screen, a plush bear, a wand.
- **Animation inside long shots.** Only 33 hard cuts in 107 s (shots of 5-11 s on the wide stage). The energy comes
  from what moves IN the shot: hops with squash + stretch, walks with bounce, bubbles popping, people entering and
  leaving, the camera slowly pushing.
- **Smear frames for speed.** A dash = 2-3 frames of the figure stretched into a thin vertical streak with blur (and
  its floor shadow stays a frame longer), then gone; arrivals the same in reverse. A fight = everyone turns into a
  blur cloud for ~6 frames and reappears rearranged. A swing = the wand drawn in 2-3 positions + an arc of sparkles.
- **Held drawings, "animation on 2s".** Key poses are held 2-4 frames each (not interpolated mush) - snappy.
- **Reaction close-ups.** After a wide action, a cut-in to one big face with an icon (1.5-3 s): the joke lands on
  the face. Wide (what happens) <-> close (how she feels) is the core rhythm.
- **Frames as objects of the world.** The camcorder / CCTV screen with REC dot and pause icon tells "they saw the
  recording" without a word (P4).
- **Colour = story.** Grey manga panels for the "normal" world, full colour when the Star Guardians enter.
- **Transitions:** mostly a dip to black of 4-8 frames on a musical accent (scene change); a glitch (horizontal slice
  shift + RGB split, 6 frames) for the twist; a whip-zoom with motion blur into a close-up (79.07 s); a panel sliding
  in from the side (81.03 s); manga panels that slide up / down to rearrange the layout.

## Sound
- One upbeat song (~152 BPM), continuous; the loudness is flat (RMS ~0.23-0.29) - no big drops.
- Cuts sit on musical ACCENTS (vocal / melodic onsets, within 0.1 s), not mechanically on every beat.
- The music fades out 84-88 s while the picture goes quiet (everyone asleep) - picture and sound end together; a
