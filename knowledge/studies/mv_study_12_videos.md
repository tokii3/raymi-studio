# Video study 2026-10-08 - what great music videos DO (project-independent)

toki 2026-10-08: "Videos analysieren und unabhaengig von konkreten Projekten draus lernen, Regeln anpassen /
verfeinern / lockern / neu formulieren - vielleicht sind es zu viele Regeln, die alle dasselbe sagen."
Material: the 3 official Riot MVs (HEARTSTEEL Live My Life + PARANOIA, K/DA MORE; inbox/_reference_videos/riot,
downloaded for study) + toki's 9 reference videos (This Is War 5 + 6, YOASOBI Idol, Ado Usseewa, Reol LUVORATORRRRRY,
Giga BRING IT ON, kradness & Reol Okochama Sensou, +male, Holy Lance Explosion Boy). Looked at one frame every 2 s of
every video + full-size lyric frames of both This Is War videos. The result is docs/RULES.md (the principles); this
file is the evidence.

## Per video - the one thing it teaches
- **HEARTSTEEL Live My Life** (3D, 2:52): a DAY in the band's neighbourhood - pizza shop, shared flat, laundromat,
  supermarket, basketball court on a bridge, skate park, diner. The song says "live my life", so the video is ordinary
  life with friends. Camera from impossible places: top-down rotating over the sofa, from INSIDE the washing machine
  (the porthole is the frame), worm's eye against the sky, an extreme close-up of a mouth. Media inside the world:
  CCTV fisheye "REC cam 03", 4:3 camcorder footage with black sides, a TV, polaroids. Payoff: the day's polaroids on
  the fridge, everyone in the diner. Transitions through things: a ball flies across the cut, a whip pan.
- **HEARTSTEEL PARANOIA** (3D + 2D, 3:42): a night heist / chase that ends at sunrise. Every singer gets his own world
  (laser security grid, car chase, wrestling ring, desert arena, underwater vortex). The STYLE changes at turning points
  (3D -> painterly 2D smears -> comic frames). Surveillance and thermal camera images with a hand-drawn circle and "?"
  on the footage. Payoff: mugshots of every member holding a name board. Night -> sunrise = the arc in light.
- **K/DA MORE** (3D, 3:50): every member has her own COLOUR WORLD (red Akali, purple Evelynn, gold Ahri, teal Kai'Sa,
  ice-blue Seraphine) - variety between sections, unity inside each. Giant type as architecture: names and words as
  walls behind silhouettes. One anchor set returns ~6x and grows (the gold mane hall). Story: Seraphine watches from
  her small room -> walks into the light -> joins them on stage (the newcomer arc). A black pause (1:44-1:47) before
  the gold explosion. Macro shots (claws, eyes), upside-down camera, letterbox throughout.
- **This Is War 5 + 6** (lyric videos on splash art): ONE type family per video (TIW5 heavy condensed, TIW6 thin
  elegant sans). Variety never comes from more fonts, it comes from: size hierarchy (key word 60-90 % width, fillers
  tiny in the gaps), ONE accent colour or material per key word (MEAT with blood, FUSE gold, FURY fire glow, COMPETITOR
  red), angle + perspective that follow the character's pose ("ONE CANINE ARMY" bends along the spear), blocks that
  fill the negative space and lock into the silhouette. The type colour comes from the picture's light (Renekton fire
  -> orange glow, Nasus blue -> white/blue, Taliyah sand -> gold). Quiet lines are two small words in the dark.
- **YOASOBI Idol**: a title card that is an event (giant アイドル on pink with confetti), ONE symbolic object scaled up
  in a white void (the red heart), a recurring motif (star eyes), the character repeated as a wallpaper pattern,
  vertical text flooding the frame as a wall (引き立て役 x30), the ending as a small TV in an empty white room.
- **Ado Usseewa**: two colour states = two emotional states (blue good student / red rage); vertical lyrics in boxes
  and huge characters taking half the frame; extreme tilts, a red laser grid as the rage.
- **Reol LUVORATORRRRRY**: the medium is a video game - title screen "PUSH > BUTTON", game UI, pixel font; the lyric
  ええええ becomes giant pixel blocks the characters stand between. The type IS the medium's own type.
- **Giga BRING IT ON**: caution-tape bands carry the lyrics in EVERY frame (one device = one video), "BATTLE Round 1"
  game cards; toki: the strobe here is too much.
- **Okochama Sensou**: a sibling quarrel in one mansion; lyrics in two colours = two speakers.
- **+male**: one classroom, the chalkboard carries the lyrics, the class as black silhouettes.
- **Holy Lance Explosion Boy**: black / white / one red accent; giant kanji (男, 正と乱, 爆裂) as the picture itself,
  geometric symbols (hexagon, 0.02mm) and ink splats framing every shot.

## What all of them share (-> docs/RULES.md principles)
1. One world per video, taken from what the song says, with a change from start to end and a payoff at the end.
2. Frames inside the picture are OBJECTS of that world (porthole, CCTV, camcorder, TV, polaroid, chalkboard, game
   screen, mugshot board) - never an abstract box around the character.
3. The camera goes everywhere: inside things, top-down, upside-down, macro, worm's eye; the shot size changes on every
   cut; the character is almost never a centred bust in front of a background.
4. Colour is planned per section (a colour world per singer / emotional state) and unified inside the section.
5. Type is part of the picture: one family, variety through size / accent / material / angle, coloured by the scene's
   light, locked into the composition, or the medium's own type (game, chalkboard, tape, neon, signs).
6. The picture does what the line means, through the character's own body and props.
7. One anchor (set or object) returns and changes; the end pays it off (polaroids, mugshots, the room turned stage).
8. Contrast and rhythm: dense frames next to empty ones, a pause before the drop, calm that is composed.
9. Cuts and transitions go through things (a ball, a door, a whip, light).

- Let's Get Weird MV did 1, 6 (partly) and 9 well (the shuffle, the triangle build), failed 3 (Raymi in the box), 5
