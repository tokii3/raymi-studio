## What is on screen
- **Format:** cinemascope 2.35:1 (854 x 362 stream) - wide black bars, the frame reads as film, not as a game capture.
- **Place:** no built set. ONE character alone in a dark violet void made of FX: thick smoke / ribbon trails that curl
  round her, embers, star specks, layered haze in front of and behind her. Depth comes from smoke layers, not walls.
- **Light:** she IS the light. Warm orange-yellow fire light on her face, chest and hands against cold violet everywhere
  else (complementary pair, very high contrast). Under-light and rim light from her own glow; heavy bloom / halation
  on every bright edge; the background falls to near black. No flat front light anywhere.
- **Rhythm - two speeds:**
  - 103.0-107.3 s (a held, sung phrase): a slow, dramatic camera on ONE hero image - low angle, wide lens, very
    close; her hand reaches INTO the lens (foreshortened, nearly fills the frame), the camera pushes in on her face.
    About 4 s with almost no cut, carried by the push and the glow.
  - 107.7-111.3 s: full-figure dance in the smoke; the camera drifts / orbits slowly at a distance while the smoke
    ribbons trail her movements (the ribbons ARE the motion design).
  - 111.7-114.7 s (the hits): a burst - a new angle every 0.33-0.67 s on the beat: low wide on a high kick -> frontal,
    arms spread wide -> face close-up, head tilted -> high kick from below -> three-quarter wide -> close-up that
    dissolves into a fire flare (the flare is the transition).
- **Poses:** every pose is an extended, asymmetric silhouette - high kicks, arms spread to the frame edges, one arm up,
  hand thrust at the lens, head tilted. Each is held a few frames as a clear shape, then snaps to the next. No
  standing-around frame.
- **Lens:** low angles (looking up = power), wide focal length close to her (strong foreshortening of hands / legs),
  dutch tilts, push-ins; she fills the frame almost always (head-to-hip or full figure edge to edge).
- **Transitions:** hidden in light and smoke - a flare whites the frame out, a smoke ribbon wipes across, her hand
  covers the lens.

## For the studio (my translation - what Watch Me / every 3D Raymi film should take)
1. **Her glow as the key light:** in dark moments her dots / ultraviolet light her face from below and the side; the
   set falls off to near black; one complementary accent (warm vs her ultraviolet). Never a flat front fill.
2. **Motion trails:** ribbons following her hands, feet and tail (the last N positions as a glowing ribbon) - the
   dance becomes graphic, the frame fills with HER movement.
3. **Haze layers:** soft smoke / haze in front of and behind her in the set's colour - depth without walls.
4. **Camera vocabulary:** low-angle hero shot (lens below her chest, wide fov ~55, ~1 m), hand-into-lens moments,
   slow push on sung held notes, beat bursts (a new angle on each hit) in the chorus, flare / smoke / hand covers as
   the transitions.
5. **Poses:** extended silhouettes (kicks, arms wide, reach to the lens); hold a few frames, then snap.
6. **Cinemascope** letterbox (2.35:1) as a film option.
7. **Fill:** she fills the frame; the empty room is never the picture.
