# CASEBOOK - every music video so far: the idea, toki's verdict, why

The question for every new script: is this idea as strong as the top three, and does it avoid what sank the rest?
Verdicts are toki's words (old engine VIDEO_LEARNINGS / PROGRESS / catalog). Exemplars in `exemplars/`.

## The standard (toki: "der Standard sollte sein wie Renai oder Let's Get Weird")

### Renai Circulation (2026-10-04, MV 4:11 + Short) - "best video"
- **Idea:** the aquarium desk. A desk world (sticky notes, polaroids, washi tape, a phone stream) on an indigo mat,
  fused with her sea (glass aquarium desk with a sea floor below, Sumi-ink writing, jellyfish stickers). Finale: the
  real Raymi under the glass.
- **Why it worked:** the world holds REAL images, so Raymi stays painted; every lyric line lives on an object (note,
  homework, chat); depth from overlapping paper; physical transitions carry the beat (page flies, sticker slaps).
- **Failed first:** v1 manga notebook converted Raymi into the medium -> "odd, low quality, empty, made without love".
- Exemplar: `exemplars/renai_ANALYSIS.md`.

### Let's Get Weird (Bill Cipher fan song, 2026-10-07, MV 3:16 + Short) - "the new standard", Short = biggest hit
- **Idea:** "something lives in the dark inside your screen - and she brought a lamp". Two lights only: the screen
  (the wall between her world and yours) and her UV lamp (her name 灯闇 = lamp in the dark) revealing ink lyrics.
  She breaks the glass on "WEIRD" and floods your room with her sea. Short = YOUR phone glass, crack per beat.
- **Why it worked:** the idea comes from her name; a story with a turn (trapped -> breaks out -> owns your room);
  5 planned wows; lore gag inside (she flattens to paper: "rays are flat polygons"); real ciphers for the comments.
- **Weak (toki):** 15 s calm intro, Raymi in the box too often, lyrics same colour / size all video, weak spots
  ("I'm a demon" small in a corner), dynamics only late. Strong: the shuffle, the triangle build-up, fast cuts at 3:00.
- Exemplar: `exemplars/lets-get-weird_TREATMENT.md` (the format a treatment should have).

### POP IN 2 (B-Komachi, 2026-10-04, MV + Short) - "built sets were the best"
- **Idea:** "a day with your oshi". You carry Raymi as an acrylic stand; she comes alive and pops into your whole day
  (bedroom, train, classroom, kitchen, concert); at the concert the roles flip and SHE finds YOU; at night she is a
  stand on your nightstand again - and winks.
- **Why it worked:** a story with acts = times of day = built 3D sets; she lives IN real places; a frame device with
  meaning (letterbox while she is "only merch", opens when she is alive).
- **The method that made v3:** a moment audit (what is it / what should it show / how better) - see
  `exemplars/pop-in-2_STORY_and_AUDIT.md`: static, first idea = placeholder, overuse, sticker in front of wallpaper.

## Good, with limits
- **Take Me to the Beach (2026-10-22..24, the first 3D film, MV + Short):** toki on the rebuild: "Sets / Idee /
  Kameras / Effekte schon ganz gut, aber Umsetzung muss besser werden". The idea (grey routine as strings and a
  battery, her dream sea as the beach metaphor, the office unfolding) held; the execution failed on structural
  faults: clips that never played, hand-guessed heights (floating / sinking / clipping), no chair, a damped mouth,
  flat even light, black frames. The structural pass (run 58 / 59) fixed them as classes (scene probe, support
  rays, push-out, chair, vocal envelope, light depth, textures v2, 8-bit post). toki's final verdict 2026-10-24 on
  run 58: "raymi ist immernoch in gegenstände geklippt, animationen nicht flüssig, raymi fällt immernoch vom himmel
  ... es wirkte alles nicht stimmig, zusammengeklatscht ... keine farbliche abstimmung, keine materialien" - parked,
  a lesson for Watch Me. Lessons: a 3D film needs its bodies in the space measured, not guessed; fix classes, not
  shots; a probe every 15th frame on 13 bone points is NOT a screening (QC v2); many sets with separate looks read
  "zusammengeklatscht" - one place, one colour script, one material set (Watch Me).
- **YOASOBI Idol (2026-10-01/02, 5 versions):** "schon sehr gut" at v5. Lessons: a rule-based director cannot
  choreograph (hand-written shot list was the turn), measure tempo / sections, cut one frame before the syllable,
  save the finale's set pieces.
- **Mesmerizer (2026-10-05):** her painted sea twisted into a whirlpool, an iris frame device, one look per act. v1 (op-art
  code patterns) "always a pattern, flat, cheap"; over-correction to "only Raymi on painted backgrounds" -> the mix.
- **It's Me, It's Verity (2026-10-07):** one medium = YOUR devices (phone UI carries every lyric). Lyrics did not
  match the music (bar-grid mapping), CTA too late.
- **KICK BACK (2026-10-16/17):** "gefaellt mir schon ganz gut, mir gefaellt wie raymi sich bewegt" - laundromat built
  in Blender; faults: frozen things behind glass, 7.7 s silence at the end, sprite holes cut by the engine.

- **KING (2026-10-18/19, first raymi-studio film):** toki on the pilot: "fehler sehe ich keine" (camera, lyrics, effects)
  - asked only for a real crown and a deeper grade; approved the final MV and Short as they are. Every critic
  (blind viewer + 6 craft) still said FAIL on the final - the roots they named are studio / script work, not guards:
  a placeholder-looking crowd, Raymi tiny / still in hall wides, primitive props next to her painting, story beats too
  short to read muted, flat outlined lyrics. Lesson: the guards killed every mechanical fault class (cut edges, clipped
  text, props in the lens, a sliced face) for good; the critics and toki judge the art differently - toki's verdict wins,
  the critics' structural notes go to the next look pass (BACKLOG).
  **toki's verdict on the final (2026-10-19, `jobs/2026-10-18_king/toki.md`):** better after the colour mix (the per-act
  grade); "animationen tranitions und effekte mag ich alle sehr - ein großer schritt nach vorne"; uploaded "trotz kleiner
  fehler". Faults: the crown - THE main element - hard to read ("muss selber erkannt werden"); the lyrics still too
  small, too thin, pasted on, and Japanese lines need romaji + English under them; thumbnails for the Short too, always
  4-5 to choose from. What became studio parts: the hero guard + contrast check, lyrics v2 (heavy weight, scene colour,
  lit, on surfaces, romaji + English), the thumbnails stage, the catalog status, the grade library as the look step,
  the critic calibration (`knowledge/calibration/2026-10-19_KING.md`).

- **POV Shorts week (2026-10-23/24, 7 talking 3D lore Shorts):** the lens is the viewer - she glares at you on the
  train, bets on rain in Tokyo, covers the lens, taps back on the aquarium glass, plays rock paper scissors with
  'stingray'. Voice line by line (edge-tts) + a Kevin MacLeod bed + own SFX; script-critic BLOCK x7 in round 1 (timing
  written by hand), PASS x7 in round 2 once every time was computed from the bed map + the takes (`raymi pov`).
  toki on the stills: "gefaellt mir schon ganz gut", then the finish ask (no burnt / loud colour, fg-mg-bg, depth +
  motion blur). toki on the finals: "gefallen mir schon gut" - taken as they are; for next time: face more centred and
  a little higher, Sashimi overexposed, hair sometimes flies, mouth half open when she is silent. What became studio
  parts: kind pov, voice_lines + mix + bed maps + sfx, the timing check, worn props, POV fx (cover, pov hand, ripple,
  algae, freeze, polaroid, stamps), act flatten, calm grades pov / pov_warm, DoF per close-up, camera motion blur, the
  exposure check (+ washed), mouth closed when silent, calmer hair. Lessons: a pose sheet from a 3/4 view before a
  build that relies on new poses; measure her head height on stills before framing a seated / crouched shot; the CI
  ffmpeg install needs a fallback (apt hung for hours).

## Failed - and the root
- **K/DA MORE v1 (2026-10-09):** "Raymi-Slideshow aus PowerPoint, bodenlos". Pretty 3D plates as stills + 2D zoom,
  even cuts, lyrics standing on top, no pilot. Every gate passed. v2 fixed it with a story of actions (one fan ->
  she wants more -> fans become jellyfish with lightstick tentacles -> card stunt "MORE").
- **Know My Name (2026-10-10):** "AI filter look, flat slapped-on lyrics, async lyrics".
- **Live My Life (2026-10-12..15):** music and lyrics did not match, half the text missing, gulls' feet in her belly,
  grey and washed out, the song's turns got no cut, "no sense"; toon plates next to painted Raymi. Cancelled.
- **Queencard (2026-10-17):** "grell / uebersaturiert" - loud colour everywhere instead of calm masses + accents.
- **Watch Me (K/DA, 3D, 2026-10-24..25):** stopped by toki: "du ignorierst alle anweisungen, referenzen und
  hinweise". Brief = one place, one camera ride, she flirts with / presents herself to the lens; reference K/DA
  FIRESTARTER 1:43-1:55. Got: generic rule cameras (director, v3 orbit, move functions), she never addresses the lens
  (no clip for it), boards and guards green while the video was "total langweilig". Root: her picture turned into
  rules, the reference summarised but never matched frame by frame, the missing core (lens play) worked around
  instead of named. Post-mortem: knowledge/HANDOFF_2026-10-25_watchme_stopped.md.
- **Electric Angel + 3 Shorts (2026-10-06):** "statisch, undynamisch, nur eine Abfolge von Raymi-Animationen, nichts
  wird gewagt" - a day spent on error gates instead of the idea.
